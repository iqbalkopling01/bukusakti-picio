## Basis materi

Materi Gagal Jantung/Decompensatio Cordis, fisioterapi penyakit jantung, pemeriksaan kardio.

## Definisi

Heart failure adalah syndrome ketika jantung tidak mampu memenuhi demand sirkulasi secara adekuat tanpa peningkatan filling pressure.

## Patofisiologi dan hubungan sebab-akibat

Reduced systolic pump atau impaired filling memicu neurohormonal compensation, sodium-water retention dan increased vascular resistance. Pulmonary congestion menimbulkan dyspnea/orthopnea. Peripheral congestion menimbulkan edema. Deconditioning menambah exercise intolerance.

## Manifestasi dan impairment

Dyspnea exertion, orthopnea, PND, fatigue, edema, reduced exercise tolerance, tachycardia.

## Pemeriksaan fisioterapi

History weight/edema, orthopnea, medication, hospitalization. HR, BP, SpO2, edema, RR, symptoms. Functional capacity 6MWT bila stable. Monitor exercise response.

## ICF / problem list

Pisahkan impairment, activity limitation, participation restriction, faktor personal, dan faktor lingkungan. Pilih problem prioritas berdasarkan safety, tujuan pasien, dan modifiability.

## Intervensi

Pada stable compensated HF: aerobic training low-moderate individualized, resistance training, breathing, education self-monitor weight/symptoms, energy conservation. Acute decompensated HF memerlukan stabilization sebelum exercise progression.

## Progression dan evaluasi

Walking distance, RPE/dyspnea, HR/BP response, edema/weight trend as medical plan, ADL.

## Red flags / escalation

Acute pulmonary edema, new chest pain, syncope, rapidly increasing edema/weight, severe hypotension, resting dyspnea worsening.

## Practical workflow kardiorespirasi

### 1. Stability sebelum exercise

Pada Heart Failure, ukur atau review HR, BP, RR, SpO2 dan symptom sebelum intervensi sesuai setting. Observasi work of breathing, color, cognition, edema atau line/tube. Pertanyaan pertama bukan “teknik apa?”, tetapi “apakah pasien cukup stabil untuk teknik ini?”.

### 2. Tentukan primary limitation

Bedakan secretion retention, ventilation/chest expansion problem, dyspnea control, impaired oxygenation, low exercise capacity, cardiac output limitation, atau simple deconditioning. Satu pasien dapat memiliki beberapa.

### 3. Pemeriksaan terarah

History → vitals → breathing pattern → auscultation bila kompeten → cough/sputum → chest mobility → functional capacity. Pilih Borg/mMRC/6MWT atau task bedside sesuai severity.

### 4. Differential contributor

ischemia/acute coronary syndrome, arrhythmia, heart failure/decompensation, pulmonary disease, anemia/deconditioning, medication/orthostatic effect.

### 5. Intervensi sesuai masalah

Secretion → airway clearance dan mobilization. Low lung expansion → thoracic expansion/position/mobility. Dyspnea obstructive → breathing control/pursed lip pada responder. Deconditioning → graded aerobic/resistance. Post-op → breathing/cough + early mobility. Selalu monitor response.

## Progression

Bed/chair activity → standing → corridor walking → interval endurance → continuous endurance → stair/community. Progress berdasarkan symptoms dan vital response, bukan hanya jumlah hari rawat.

## SOAP kardiorespirasi

S: dyspnea, cough/sputum, fatigue, sleep/orthopnea, activity tolerance.

O: pre/during/post vitals, breath sound/cough, distance/task, Borg.

A: apakah limitation respiratory, cardiac, peripheral muscle, fear, atau combination?

P: tulis dose walking/exercise berikutnya dan airway strategy yang dipertahankan/dihentikan.

## Deep Dive: mekanisme sampai evaluasi

### Patofisiologi yang perlu benar-benar dipahami

Impaired filling and/or pumping reduces effective cardiac output and increases filling pressures. Pulmonary congestion produces dyspnea/orthopnea; systemic venous congestion produces edema. Chronic neurohormonal activation and inactivity contribute to skeletal-muscle deconditioning.

### Pemeriksaan terarah

History dyspnea, orthopnea/PND, edema, fatigue, weight change, medication and hospitalization. Vitals, rhythm awareness, SpO2, edema, breathing, functional mobility and exertional response. Review ejection fraction/medical classification but treat functional limitation. Use Borg, 6MWT and strength.

### Intervensi berdasarkan target impairment

When medically stable, cardiac rehab uses graded aerobic and resistance exercise, education, pacing and self-monitoring. Acute decompensation with congestion/unstable vitals is not routine exercise time. Breathing/position can relieve symptom; coordinate fluid/medication with team.

### Evaluasi dan kriteria progression

6MWT/workload, HR/BP, Borg, symptoms, edema/weight trend from medical plan, ADL and hospitalization/exacerbation.

### Differential / kondisi yang jangan terlewat

COPD, anemia, PE, ischemia, arrhythmia and renal/fluid disorders.
