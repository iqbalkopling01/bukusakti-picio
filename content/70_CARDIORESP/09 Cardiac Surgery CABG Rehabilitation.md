## Basis materi

Materi Pre/Post Cardiac Rehabilitation for Cardiac Surgery dan laporan tata laksana Post OP CABG.

## Definisi

CABG dan cardiac surgery memperbaiki perfusion/struktur tetapi menghasilkan sternotomy/incision pain, respiratory impairment sementara, weakness dan deconditioning.

## Patofisiologi dan hubungan sebab-akibat

Anesthesia, pain dan bed rest menurunkan lung volume dan cough. Surgical stress meningkatkan fatigue. Early mobilization membantu functional recovery dan mencegah complications.

## Manifestasi dan impairment

Incisional pain, reduced deep breath, ineffective cough, weakness, reduced walking, fear movement.

## Pemeriksaan fisioterapi

Pre/post-op vitals, breath sounds, SpO2, cough, chest expansion, wound/sternal precaution, mobility, gait, RPE.

## ICF / problem list

Pisahkan impairment, activity limitation, participation restriction, faktor personal, dan faktor lingkungan. Pilih problem prioritas berdasarkan safety, tujuan pasien, dan modifiability.

## Intervensi

Breathing exercise, supported cough/huff, circulation exercise, bed mobility technique, sit-stand, walking progression, stair, education sternal/wound precautions sesuai local protocol. Later cardiac rehab aerobic + resistance.

## Progression dan evaluasi

Track walking distance, RPE, HR/BP, SpO2, independence, cough, pain. Progress daily activity based on response.

## Red flags / escalation

Chest pain ischemic, arrhythmia, wound infection/dehiscence, severe dyspnea, unstable BP, syncope.

## Practical workflow kardiorespirasi

### 1. Stability sebelum exercise

Pada Cardiac Surgery and CABG Rehabilitation, ukur atau review HR, BP, RR, SpO2 dan symptom sebelum intervensi sesuai setting. Observasi work of breathing, color, cognition, edema atau line/tube. Pertanyaan pertama bukan “teknik apa?”, tetapi “apakah pasien cukup stabil untuk teknik ini?”.

### 2. Tentukan primary limitation

Bedakan secretion retention, ventilation/chest expansion problem, dyspnea control, impaired oxygenation, low exercise capacity, cardiac output limitation, atau simple deconditioning. Satu pasien dapat memiliki beberapa.

### 3. Pemeriksaan terarah

History → vitals → breathing pattern → auscultation bila kompeten → cough/sputum → chest mobility → functional capacity. Pilih Borg/mMRC/6MWT atau task bedside sesuai severity.

### 4. Differential contributor

ischemia/acute coronary syndrome, arrhythmia, heart failure/decompensation, pulmonary disease, anemia/deconditioning, medication/orthostatic effect.

### 5. Intervensi sesuai masalah

Secretion → airway clearance dan mobilization. Low lung expansion → thoracic expansion/position/mobility. Dyspnea obstructive → breathing control/pursed lip pada responder. Deconditioning → graded aerobic/resistance. Post-op → breathing/cough + early mobility. Selalu monitor response.

## Progression

Bed/chair activity → standing → corridor walking → interval endurance → continuous endurance → stair/community. Progress berdasarkan symptoms dan vital response, bukan hanya jumlah hari rawat.

## SOAP kardiorespirasi

S: dyspnea, cough/sputum, fatigue, sleep/orthopnea, activity tolerance.

O: pre/during/post vitals, breath sound/cough, distance/task, Borg.

A: apakah limitation respiratory, cardiac, peripheral muscle, fear, atau combination?

P: tulis dose walking/exercise berikutnya dan airway strategy yang dipertahankan/dihentikan.

## Deep Dive: mekanisme sampai evaluasi

### Patofisiologi yang perlu benar-benar dipahami

CABG restores coronary blood flow by bypass grafts but early post-op impairment includes sternotomy pain, ventilation reduction, secretion retention, weakness and bed-rest deconditioning. Surgical/anesthesia effects can create atelectatic pattern.

### Pemeriksaan terarah

Check lines/drains, hemodynamic/rhythm stability, oxygen, wound, pain, breathing pattern, auscultation, cough, chest expansion, mobility and orthostatic response. Use ICU mobility/ADL scales in acute phase and 6MWT later.

### Intervensi berdasarkan target impairment

Early breathing expansion and supported cough/huff as indicated, positioning, shoulder/thoracic movement within wound precautions, bed mobility, sitting/standing and progressive walking. Later cardiac rehab adds aerobic/resistance conditioning and risk-factor education. Sternal precautions should follow current local surgical protocol rather than rigid universal rules.

### Evaluasi dan kriteria progression

Pain, oxygen need, respiratory signs, transfer/ambulation, Borg, 6MWT, ADL and wound. Monitor arrhythmia, hypotension, angina-like symptom and infection.

### Differential / kondisi yang jangan terlewat

Post-op pulmonary complication, heart failure, graft/ischemic issue, DVT/PE and wound instability.
