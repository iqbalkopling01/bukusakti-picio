## Basis materi

Materi Penyakit Jantung Infeksi.

## Definisi

Infeksi jantung dapat melibatkan endocardium/valves, myocardium, atau pericardium dengan konsekuensi yang berbeda.

## Patofisiologi dan hubungan sebab-akibat

Inflammation/infection dapat merusak valve, mengganggu myocardial function atau menyebabkan pericardial pain/effusion. Systemic infection meningkatkan metabolic demand dan fatigue.

## Manifestasi dan impairment

Fever/systemic symptoms, chest pain depending structure, dyspnea, fatigue, arrhythmia.

## Pemeriksaan fisioterapi

Fisioterapi screening stability, vitals, symptoms, mobility/deconditioning. Acute infection bukan waktu untuk exercise training berat.

## ICF / problem list

Pisahkan impairment, activity limitation, participation restriction, faktor personal, dan faktor lingkungan. Pilih problem prioritas berdasarkan safety, tujuan pasien, dan modifiability.

## Intervensi

Setelah medically treated/stable: graded mobility dan reconditioning. Cardiac-specific precautions mengikuti diagnosis dan cardiology.

## Progression dan evaluasi

Exercise tolerance, vitals, fatigue, function.

## Red flags / escalation

Fever with hemodynamic change, chest pain, arrhythmia, acute dyspnea.

## Practical workflow kardiorespirasi

### 1. Stability sebelum exercise

Pada Infective Heart Disease, ukur atau review HR, BP, RR, SpO2 dan symptom sebelum intervensi sesuai setting. Observasi work of breathing, color, cognition, edema atau line/tube. Pertanyaan pertama bukan “teknik apa?”, tetapi “apakah pasien cukup stabil untuk teknik ini?”.

### 2. Tentukan primary limitation

Bedakan secretion retention, ventilation/chest expansion problem, dyspnea control, impaired oxygenation, low exercise capacity, cardiac output limitation, atau simple deconditioning. Satu pasien dapat memiliki beberapa.

### 3. Pemeriksaan terarah

History → vitals → breathing pattern → auscultation bila kompeten → cough/sputum → chest mobility → functional capacity. Pilih Borg/mMRC/6MWT atau task bedside sesuai severity.

### 4. Differential contributor

ischemia/acute coronary syndrome, arrhythmia, heart failure/decompensation, pulmonary disease, anemia/deconditioning, medication/orthostatic effect.

### 5. Intervensi sesuai masalah

Secretion → airway clearance dan mobilization. Low lung expansion → thoracic expansion/position/mobility. Dyspnea obstructive → breathing control/pursed lip pada responder. Deconditioning → graded aerobic/resistance. Post-op → breathing/cough + early mobility. Selalu monitor response.

## Progression

Bed/chair activity → standing → corridor walking → interval endurance → continuous endurance → stair/community. Progress berdasarkan symptoms dan vital response, bukan hanya jumlah hari rawat.

## SOAP kardiorespirasi

S: dyspnea, cough/sputum, fatigue, sleep/orthopnea, activity tolerance.

O: pre/during/post vitals, breath sound/cough, distance/task, Borg.

A: apakah limitation respiratory, cardiac, peripheral muscle, fear, atau combination?

P: tulis dose walking/exercise berikutnya dan airway strategy yang dipertahankan/dihentikan.

## Deep Dive: mekanisme sampai evaluasi

### Patofisiologi yang perlu benar-benar dipahami

Infective endocardial/myocardial/pericardial conditions can impair cardiac function and cause systemic inflammation. Active infection may produce fever, fatigue and hemodynamic instability; embolic or valvular complications can occur depending diagnosis.

### Pemeriksaan terarah

Review exact medical diagnosis and infection status. Vitals, fever, cardiac symptoms, fatigue and mobility. Do not apply generic cardiac exercise intensity during active unstable infection. Screen neurologic/embolic symptoms and heart failure signs.

### Intervensi berdasarkan target impairment

During acute infection focus on safe mobility/prevention if medically allowed. Progressive conditioning begins after stabilization/medical clearance, with close symptom monitoring.

### Evaluasi dan kriteria progression

Vitals, fatigue, mobility and cardiac symptom. Recurrence fever, chest pain, dyspnea or neurologic sign prompts medical review.

### Differential / kondisi yang jangan terlewat

Noninfective inflammatory disease, heart failure, pneumonia/systemic infection.
