## Basis materi

Materi case study primary spontaneous pneumothorax dan kardiorespirasi.

## Definisi

Pneumothorax adalah udara dalam pleural space yang mengurangi negative intrapleural pressure sehingga lung mengalami partial/complete collapse.

## Patofisiologi dan hubungan sebab-akibat

Air masuk pleural cavity memisahkan visceral dan parietal pleura. Lung recoil inward sehingga ventilation sisi tersebut menurun. Tension pneumothorax meningkatkan intrathoracic pressure dan mengganggu venous return, merupakan emergency.

## Manifestasi dan impairment

Sudden chest pain, dyspnea, reduced breath sounds sisi affected, tachycardia.

## Pemeriksaan fisioterapi

Pneumothorax akut memerlukan medical management terlebih dahulu. Setelah stable/chest tube management, PT assess breathing, mobility, pain, secretion, activity, posture. Jangan melakukan technique yang meningkatkan pressure secara tidak sesuai pada active untreated pneumothorax.

## ICF / problem list

Pisahkan impairment, activity limitation, participation restriction, faktor personal, dan faktor lingkungan. Pilih problem prioritas berdasarkan safety, tujuan pasien, dan modifiability.

## Intervensi

Setelah medical stabilization: breathing expansion gentle, shoulder/chest mobility, early mobilization, cough bila secretion, conditioning. Coordinate chest tube safety.

## Progression dan evaluasi

SpO2, dyspnea, mobility, chest/shoulder ROM, walking tolerance.

## Red flags / escalation

Sudden worsening dyspnea, hypotension, tracheal deviation/tension signs, chest tube disconnection, acute chest pain require emergency response.

## Practical workflow kardiorespirasi

### 1. Stability sebelum exercise

Pada Pneumothorax, ukur atau review HR, BP, RR, SpO2 dan symptom sebelum intervensi sesuai setting. Observasi work of breathing, color, cognition, edema atau line/tube. Pertanyaan pertama bukan “teknik apa?”, tetapi “apakah pasien cukup stabil untuk teknik ini?”.

### 2. Tentukan primary limitation

Bedakan secretion retention, ventilation/chest expansion problem, dyspnea control, impaired oxygenation, low exercise capacity, cardiac output limitation, atau simple deconditioning. Satu pasien dapat memiliki beberapa.

### 3. Pemeriksaan terarah

History → vitals → breathing pattern → auscultation bila kompeten → cough/sputum → chest mobility → functional capacity. Pilih Borg/mMRC/6MWT atau task bedside sesuai severity.

### 4. Differential contributor

infection/exacerbation, heart failure, pulmonary embolism, pneumothorax, anemia/deconditioning, musculoskeletal chest pain depending presentation.

### 5. Intervensi sesuai masalah

Secretion → airway clearance dan mobilization. Low lung expansion → thoracic expansion/position/mobility. Dyspnea obstructive → breathing control/pursed lip pada responder. Deconditioning → graded aerobic/resistance. Post-op → breathing/cough + early mobility. Selalu monitor response.

## Progression

Bed/chair activity → standing → corridor walking → interval endurance → continuous endurance → stair/community. Progress berdasarkan symptoms dan vital response, bukan hanya jumlah hari rawat.

## SOAP kardiorespirasi

S: dyspnea, cough/sputum, fatigue, sleep/orthopnea, activity tolerance.

O: pre/during/post vitals, breath sound/cough, distance/task, Borg.

A: apakah limitation respiratory, cardiac, peripheral muscle, fear, atau combination?

P: tulis dose walking/exercise berikutnya dan airway strategy yang dipertahankan/dihentikan.

## Deep Dive: mekanisme sampai evaluasi

### Patofisiologi yang perlu benar-benar dipahami

Air enters pleural space, reducing negative intrapleural pressure and allowing partial/complete lung collapse. Tension pneumothorax can shift mediastinum and impair venous return, becoming life-threatening.

### Pemeriksaan terarah

Acute unilateral pleuritic pain/dyspnea, reduced chest movement, reduced breath sounds and hyperresonance may occur. Do not perform provocative respiratory exercise in unstable untreated pneumothorax. Monitor vitals/SpO2 and medical drainage status.

### Intervensi berdasarkan target impairment

Acute treatment is medical, often observation or chest drain depending size/type. Physiotherapy after stabilization focuses on positioning, breathing expansion within tolerance, mobility, secretion management if needed and reconditioning. Protect drain and follow restrictions.

### Evaluasi dan kriteria progression

SpO2, dyspnea, chest expansion, mobility and imaging/medical resolution. Sudden worsening with hypotension/distress is emergency.

### Differential / kondisi yang jangan terlewat

PE, pneumonia/pleurisy, musculoskeletal chest pain and acute coronary disease.
