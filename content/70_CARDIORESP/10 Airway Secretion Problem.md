## Basis materi

Materi teknik fisioterapi pembersihan jalan napas, ACBT, chest mobility.

## Definisi

Retention terjadi ketika mucus production meningkat atau cough/mucociliary clearance tidak memadai.

## Patofisiologi dan hubungan sebab-akibat

Dehydrated/thick secretion, airway inflammation, weak cough, pain, neuromuscular weakness, immobilization atau reduced lung volume menyebabkan mucus menetap. Retention meningkatkan airway resistance dan infection/atelectasis risk.

## Manifestasi dan impairment

Productive cough tidak efektif, audible secretion, coarse crackles/rhonchi, dyspnea, recurrent infection.

## Pemeriksaan fisioterapi

Assess sputum amount/viscosity/color, cough peak/effectiveness, breath sounds, SpO2, hydration context, positioning, respiratory muscle strength when relevant.

## ICF / problem list

Pisahkan impairment, activity limitation, participation restriction, faktor personal, dan faktor lingkungan. Pilih problem prioritas berdasarkan safety, tujuan pasien, dan modifiability.

## Intervensi

ACBT, huff/cough, PEP/device bila trained, positioning/postural drainage selected, mobilization, hydration coordination. Manual percussion/vibration hanya bila indicated dan no contraindication. Neuromuscular weakness may require assisted cough techniques by trained clinician.

## Progression dan evaluasi

Sputum cleared, breath sounds, dyspnea, SpO2, cough effectiveness, frequency infection.

## Red flags / escalation

Hemoptysis, untreated pneumothorax, severe instability, acute respiratory distress.

## Practical workflow kardiorespirasi

### 1. Stability sebelum exercise

Pada Airway Secretion Retention, ukur atau review HR, BP, RR, SpO2 dan symptom sebelum intervensi sesuai setting. Observasi work of breathing, color, cognition, edema atau line/tube. Pertanyaan pertama bukan “teknik apa?”, tetapi “apakah pasien cukup stabil untuk teknik ini?”.

### 2. Tentukan primary limitation

Bedakan secretion retention, ventilation/chest expansion problem, dyspnea control, impaired oxygenation, low exercise capacity, cardiac output limitation, atau simple deconditioning. Satu pasien dapat memiliki beberapa.

### 3. Pemeriksaan terarah

History → vitals → breathing pattern → auscultation bila kompeten → cough/sputum → chest mobility → functional capacity. Pilih Borg/mMRC/6MWT atau task bedside sesuai severity.

### 4. Differential contributor

infection/exacerbation, heart failure, pulmonary embolism, pneumothorax, anemia/deconditioning, musculoskeletal chest pain depending presentation.

### 5. Intervensi sesuai masalah

Secretion → airway clearance dan mobilization. Low lung expansion → thoracic expansion/position/mobility. Dyspnea obstructive → breathing control/pursed lip pada responder. Deconditioning → graded aerobic/resistance. Post-op → breathing/cough + early mobility. Selalu monitor response.

## Progression

Bed/chair activity → standing → corridor walking → interval endurance → continuous endurance → stair/community. Progress berdasarkan symptoms dan vital response, bukan hanya jumlah hari rawat.

## SOAP kardiorespirasi

S: dyspnea, cough/sputum, fatigue, sleep/orthopnea, activity tolerance.

O: pre/during/post vitals, breath sound/cough, distance/task, Borg.

A: apakah limitation respiratory, cardiac, peripheral muscle, fear, atau combination?

P: tulis dose walking/exercise berikutnya dan airway strategy yang dipertahankan/dihentikan.

## Deep Dive: mekanisme sampai evaluasi

### Patofisiologi yang perlu benar-benar dipahami

Mucociliary clearance and effective cough normally remove secretions. Infection, dehydration, weak cough, airway disease or immobility can increase secretion retention, obstruction and atelectatic risk.

### Pemeriksaan terarah

Ask amount/color/viscosity, cough strength, difficulty expectorating and fatigue. Auscultation, SpO2/RR and chest movement; observe cough/huff. In neuromuscular disease assess inspiratory/expiratory strength from medical/respiratory team when available.

### Intervensi berdasarkan target impairment

Select technique based on mechanism: active cycle of breathing, huff/cough, positioning, PEP/device if available and indicated, manual technique, suction/assisted cough within scope and protocol, plus mobilization/hydration coordination. Stop if instability, severe bronchospasm or intolerance.

### Evaluasi dan kriteria progression

Amount/ease of sputum clearance, breath sounds, work of breathing, SpO2 and function. More sputum immediately after treatment may reflect successful mobilization, not deterioration.

### Differential / kondisi yang jangan terlewat

Pulmonary edema, asthma bronchospasm, pneumothorax and aspiration.
