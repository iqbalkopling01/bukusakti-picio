## Basis materi

Materi Valve Disease dan cardiac rehab.

## Definisi

Stenosis menghambat forward flow melalui valve, regurgitation memungkinkan backward flow. Hemodynamic consequence tergantung valve dan severity.

## Patofisiologi dan hubungan sebab-akibat

Pressure atau volume overload menyebabkan chamber adaptation/hypertrophy/dilation. Severe disease menurunkan cardiac output atau meningkatkan pulmonary pressure. Exercise tolerance berkurang.

## Manifestasi dan impairment

Dyspnea, fatigue, palpitation, syncope/chest pain pada severe lesions, edema.

## Pemeriksaan fisioterapi

Medical diagnosis/severity via echocardiography menjadi penting. PT monitor HR, BP, symptoms, exercise tolerance dan post-surgical status. Auscultation murmur bukan dasar PT menentukan severity.

## ICF / problem list

Pisahkan impairment, activity limitation, participation restriction, faktor personal, dan faktor lingkungan. Pilih problem prioritas berdasarkan safety, tujuan pasien, dan modifiability.

## Intervensi

Stable mild/moderate disease: exercise sesuai cardiology clearance. Post valve surgery: early mobilization, breathing, walking, progressive conditioning mengikuti cardiac rehab.

## Progression dan evaluasi

Vitals, RPE, walking capacity, functional independence.

## Red flags / escalation

Syncope, angina, resting dyspnea, unstable arrhythmia, acute HF signs.

## Practical workflow kardiorespirasi

### 1. Stability sebelum exercise

Pada Valvular Heart Disease, ukur atau review HR, BP, RR, SpO2 dan symptom sebelum intervensi sesuai setting. Observasi work of breathing, color, cognition, edema atau line/tube. Pertanyaan pertama bukan “teknik apa?”, tetapi “apakah pasien cukup stabil untuk teknik ini?”.

### 2. Tentukan primary limitation

Bedakan secretion retention, ventilation/chest expansion problem, dyspnea control, impaired oxygenation, low exercise capacity, cardiac output limitation, atau simple deconditioning. Satu pasien dapat memiliki beberapa.

### 3. Pemeriksaan terarah

History → vitals → breathing pattern → auscultation bila kompeten → cough/sputum → chest mobility → functional capacity. Pilih Borg/mMRC/6MWT atau task bedside sesuai severity.

### 4. Differential contributor

ischemia/acute coronary syndrome, arrhythmia, heart failure/decompensation, pulmonary disease, anemia/deconditioning, medication/orthostatic effect.

### 5. Intervensi sesuai masalah

Secretion → airway clearance dan mobilization. Low lung expansion → thoracic expansion/position/mobility. Dyspnea obstructive → breathing control/pursed lip pada responder. Deconditioning → graded aerobic/resistance. Post-op → breathing/cough + early mobility. Selalu monitor response.

## Progression

Bed/chair activity → standing → corridor walking → interval endurance → continuous endurance → stair/community. Progress berdasarkan symptoms dan vital response, bukan hanya jumlah hari rawat.

## SOAP kardiorespirasi

S: dyspnea, cough/sputum, fatigue, sleep/orthopnea, activity tolerance.

O: pre/during/post vitals, breath sound/cough, distance/task, Borg.

A: apakah limitation respiratory, cardiac, peripheral muscle, fear, atau combination?

P: tulis dose walking/exercise berikutnya dan airway strategy yang dipertahankan/dihentikan.

## Deep Dive: mekanisme sampai evaluasi

### Patofisiologi yang perlu benar-benar dipahami

Stenosis creates pressure overload; regurgitation creates volume overload. Compensation can preserve resting function until symptoms/exertional limitation emerge. Severe disease can lead to pulmonary hypertension, heart failure or arrhythmia.

### Pemeriksaan terarah

History exertional dyspnea, chest pain, syncope, palpitations and fatigue. Vitals and exertional response; medical echocardiography defines severity. Avoid high-intensity testing in severe symptomatic/unstable disease without clearance.

### Intervensi berdasarkan target impairment

Exercise prescription follows medical severity and post-intervention status. Stable mild/moderate disease may exercise with monitoring; severe symptomatic disease needs cardiology management. Post valve surgery rehabilitation follows cardiac-surgery principles.

### Evaluasi dan kriteria progression

Symptom threshold, workload, HR/BP, 6MWT and ADL. New syncope/chest pain needs escalation.

### Differential / kondisi yang jangan terlewat

Coronary disease, heart failure, arrhythmia and pulmonary disease.
