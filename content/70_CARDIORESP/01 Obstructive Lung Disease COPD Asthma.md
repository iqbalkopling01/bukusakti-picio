## Basis materi

Materi Penyakit Paru Obstruksi, fisiologi respirasi, breathing exercise, airway clearance.

## Definisi

Obstructive disease ditandai peningkatan resistance airflow terutama saat expiration. COPD bersifat persistent airflow limitation dengan emphysema/chronic bronchitis contribution, sedangkan asthma memiliki variable airway narrowing dan hyperresponsiveness.

## Patofisiologi dan hubungan sebab-akibat

COPD: inflammation, mucus, airway remodeling dan loss elastic recoil menyebabkan expiratory flow limitation dan air trapping. Hyperinflation memendekkan inspiratory muscle length dan meningkatkan work of breathing. Asthma: bronchoconstriction, airway inflammation dan mucus menyebabkan episodic narrowing. V/Q mismatch dapat menurunkan gas exchange.

## Manifestasi dan impairment

Dyspnea, prolonged expiration, wheeze, cough/sputum, reduced exercise tolerance, accessory muscle use, barrel-chest/hyperinflation pattern pada sebagian COPD.

## Pemeriksaan fisioterapi

History exacerbation, smoking/exposure, sputum, dyspnea, medication/inhaler, oxygen. RR, HR, SpO2, breathing pattern, chest expansion, auscultation, cough, sputum, Borg/mMRC, 6MWT. Review pulmonary function test bila tersedia.

## ICF / problem list

Pisahkan impairment, activity limitation, participation restriction, faktor personal, dan faktor lingkungan. Pilih problem prioritas berdasarkan safety, tujuan pasien, dan modifiability.

## Intervensi

Breathing control dan pursed-lip breathing bila membantu symptom. Airway clearance bila secretion problem. Aerobic dan resistance training merupakan core pulmonary rehab. Education pacing, inhaler coordination with medical plan, energy conservation, physical activity. Positioning forward lean dapat membantu dyspnea.

## Progression dan evaluasi

Track Borg, mMRC, 6MWT, exercise workload, recovery, exacerbation frequency, activity participation. Progress duration/intensity with symptom/vitals monitoring.

## Red flags / escalation

Acute severe dyspnea, confusion, cyanosis, chest pain, severe desaturation, inability speak, silent chest/severe asthma, hemoptysis significant require urgent care.

## Practical workflow kardiorespirasi

### 1. Stability sebelum exercise

Pada Obstructive Lung Disease: COPD and Asthma, ukur atau review HR, BP, RR, SpO2 dan symptom sebelum intervensi sesuai setting. Observasi work of breathing, color, cognition, edema atau line/tube. Pertanyaan pertama bukan “teknik apa?”, tetapi “apakah pasien cukup stabil untuk teknik ini?”.

### 2. Tentukan primary limitation

Bedakan secretion retention, ventilation/chest expansion problem, dyspnea control, impaired oxygenation, low exercise capacity, cardiac output limitation, atau simple deconditioning. Satu pasien dapat memiliki beberapa.

### 3. Pemeriksaan terarah

History → vitals → breathing pattern → auscultation bila kompeten → cough/sputum → chest mobility → functional capacity. Pilih Borg/mMRC/6MWT atau task bedside sesuai severity.

### 4. Differential contributor

infection/exacerbation, heart failure, pulmonary embolism, pneumothorax, anemia/deconditioning, musculoskeletal chest pain depending presentation.

### 5. Intervensi sesuai masalah

Secretion → airway clearance dan mobilization. Low lung expansion → thoracic expansion/position/mobility. Dyspnea obstructive → breathing control/pursed lip pada responder. Deconditioning → graded aerobic/resistance. Post-op → breathing/cough + early mobility. Selalu monitor response.

## Progression

Bed/chair activity → standing → corridor walking → interval endurance → continuous endurance → stair/community. Progress berdasarkan symptoms dan vital response, bukan hanya jumlah hari rawat.

## SOAP kardiorespirasi

S: dyspnea, cough/sputum, fatigue, sleep/orthopnea, activity tolerance.

O: pre/during/post vitals, breath sound/cough, distance/task, Borg.

A: apakah limitation respiratory, cardiac, peripheral muscle, fear, atau combination?

P: tulis dose walking/exercise berikutnya dan airway strategy yang dipertahankan/dihentikan.

## Deep Dive: mekanisme sampai evaluasi

### Patofisiologi yang perlu benar-benar dipahami

COPD involves persistent airflow limitation from small-airway disease and/or emphysematous destruction, leading to expiratory flow limitation, air trapping and dynamic hyperinflation. Asthma involves variable airway inflammation, bronchoconstriction and hyperresponsiveness, often more reversible. Both can increase work of breathing; COPD adds peripheral muscle deconditioning.

### Pemeriksaan terarah

History dyspnea, wheeze, cough/sputum, exacerbation, smoking/exposure, inhaler/oxygen and activity. Vitals/SpO2, breathing pattern, accessory muscle, auscultation, cough/sputum and exercise response. Pulmonary function from medical record informs obstruction. Use mMRC/Borg/6MWT and strength/endurance.

### Intervensi berdasarkan target impairment

Pulmonary rehab core: aerobic and resistance training, education/pacing and physical activity. Pursed-lip breathing/forward lean can reduce dyspnea in responders. Airway clearance only when retained secretion is present. Coordinate inhaler/oxygen plan medically. Asthma requires trigger/action plan and exercise with control status considered.

### Evaluasi dan kriteria progression

6MWT/workload, Borg/mMRC, recovery time, strength, activity and exacerbation. Monitor SpO2 according to clinical setting.

### Differential / kondisi yang jangan terlewat

Heart failure, PE, pneumonia, pneumothorax, anemia and dysfunctional breathing.
