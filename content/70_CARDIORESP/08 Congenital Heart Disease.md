## Basis materi

Materi Congenital Heart Defect dan pediatri cardiorespiratory.

## Definisi

CHD adalah structural heart abnormality sejak lahir yang memengaruhi blood flow, oxygenation, pressure atau workload jantung.

## Patofisiologi dan hubungan sebab-akibat

Shunt kiri-kanan dapat meningkatkan pulmonary blood flow. Shunt kanan-kiri dapat menyebabkan cyanosis. Obstruction meningkatkan pressure load. Surgical correction/palliation mengubah physiology sehingga exercise prescription harus individualized.

## Manifestasi dan impairment

Infant feeding fatigue, poor growth, cyanosis pada certain lesions, dyspnea, reduced exercise tolerance, developmental delay secondary illness.

## Pemeriksaan fisioterapi

Review diagnosis/surgery/oxygen/cardiology restrictions. Vitals, SpO2, feeding/activity tolerance, development, gross motor, fatigue.

## ICF / problem list

Pisahkan impairment, activity limitation, participation restriction, faktor personal, dan faktor lingkungan. Pilih problem prioritas berdasarkan safety, tujuan pasien, dan modifiability.

## Intervensi

Developmental facilitation, gentle conditioning, play-based activity, breathing/airway management if needed, post-op mobility, caregiver education. Intensity mengikuti cardiology/medical clearance.

## Progression dan evaluasi

Developmental outcome, activity tolerance, SpO2 response, fatigue, participation.

## Red flags / escalation

Cyanotic spell, syncope, severe dyspnea, feeding distress, unstable vitals.

## Practical workflow kardiorespirasi

### 1. Stability sebelum exercise

Pada Congenital Heart Disease, ukur atau review HR, BP, RR, SpO2 dan symptom sebelum intervensi sesuai setting. Observasi work of breathing, color, cognition, edema atau line/tube. Pertanyaan pertama bukan “teknik apa?”, tetapi “apakah pasien cukup stabil untuk teknik ini?”.

### 2. Tentukan primary limitation

Bedakan secretion retention, ventilation/chest expansion problem, dyspnea control, impaired oxygenation, low exercise capacity, cardiac output limitation, atau simple deconditioning. Satu pasien dapat memiliki beberapa.

### 3. Pemeriksaan terarah

History → vitals → breathing pattern → auscultation bila kompeten → cough/sputum → chest mobility → functional capacity. Pilih Borg/mMRC/6MWT atau task bedside sesuai severity.

### 4. Differential contributor

ischemia/acute coronary syndrome, arrhythmia, heart failure/decompensation, pulmonary disease, anemia/deconditioning, medication/orthostatic effect.

### 5. Intervensi sesuai masalah

Secretion → airway clearance dan mobilization. Low lung expansion → thoracic expansion/position/mobility. Dyspnea obstructive → breathing control/pursed lip pada responder. Deconditioning → graded aerobic/resistance. Post-op → breathing/cough + early mobility. Selalu monitor response.

## Progression

Bed/chair activity → standing → corridor walking → interval endurance → continuous endurance → stair/community. Progress berdasarkan symptoms dan vital response, bukan hanya jumlah hari rawat.

## SOAP kardiorespirasi

S: dyspnea, cough/sputum, fatigue, sleep/orthopnea, activity tolerance.

O: pre/during/post vitals, breath sound/cough, distance/task, Borg.

A: apakah limitation respiratory, cardiac, peripheral muscle, fear, atau combination?

P: tulis dose walking/exercise berikutnya dan airway strategy yang dipertahankan/dihentikan.

## Deep Dive: mekanisme sampai evaluasi

### Patofisiologi yang perlu benar-benar dipahami

Congenital structural lesions alter pressure/volume load, shunting and oxygenation depending defect. Repaired and unrepaired patients have very different physiology, so diagnosis-specific cardiology information is essential.

### Pemeriksaan terarah

History exact lesion/repair, cyanosis, feeding/growth in child, fatigue, exercise restriction and medication. Vitals including SpO2 baseline, growth, mobility/development and exercise tolerance. Understand permitted activity from cardiology.

### Intervensi berdasarkan target impairment

Developmentally appropriate activity and exercise within medical parameters, respiratory care around surgery when relevant, motor-development support and family education. Avoid generic saturation targets because some cyanotic conditions have different baselines.

### Evaluasi dan kriteria progression

Growth/development, functional capacity, symptom and individualized oxygenation/exercise response.

### Differential / kondisi yang jangan terlewat

Pulmonary disease, anemia, arrhythmia and postoperative complication.
