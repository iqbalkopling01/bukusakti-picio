## Basis materi

Materi formulir kasus bronkopneumonia, pemeriksaan pernapasan, airway clearance.

## Definisi

Bronchopneumonia adalah infection/inflammation patchy pada bronchioles dan adjacent alveoli yang dapat menghasilkan secretion, consolidation dan impaired gas exchange.

## Patofisiologi dan hubungan sebab-akibat

Infection memicu inflammatory exudate dan mucus. Alveoli yang terisi cairan memiliki ventilation rendah sementara perfusion dapat tetap ada sehingga V/Q mismatch dan hypoxemia terjadi. Pain/fatigue/bed rest menurunkan deep breathing dan mobility sehingga secretion clearance makin buruk.

## Manifestasi dan impairment

Fever dapat ada, cough, sputum, dyspnea, tachypnea, crackles, reduced SpO2, fatigue, decreased mobility.

## Pemeriksaan fisioterapi

Medical stability, vitals, SpO2, RR, auscultation, cough effectiveness, sputum, chest expansion, mobility, activity response. Review X-ray/lab dari tim medis.

## ICF / problem list

Pisahkan impairment, activity limitation, participation restriction, faktor personal, dan faktor lingkungan. Pilih problem prioritas berdasarkan safety, tujuan pasien, dan modifiability.

## Intervensi

Positioning, breathing expansion, ACBT/huff/cough bila secretion, early mobilization dan walking saat stable. Hydration/medical infection treatment dikelola tim. Avoid routine aggressive percussion bila tidak indicated.

## Progression dan evaluasi

Monitor RR, SpO2, temperature, breath sounds, sputum, cough, mobility, walking. Goal adalah improved ventilation/clearance/function, bukan sekadar “bunyi paru hilang” sekali sesi.

## Red flags / escalation

Respiratory distress, sepsis, hemodynamic instability, worsening hypoxemia, altered consciousness.

## Practical workflow kardiorespirasi

### 1. Stability sebelum exercise

Pada Bronchopneumonia, ukur atau review HR, BP, RR, SpO2 dan symptom sebelum intervensi sesuai setting. Observasi work of breathing, color, cognition, edema atau line/tube. Pertanyaan pertama bukan “teknik apa?”, tetapi “apakah pasien cukup stabil untuk teknik ini?”.

### 2. Tentukan primary limitation

Bedakan secretion retention, ventilation/chest expansion problem, dyspnea control, impaired oxygenation, low exercise capacity, cardiac output limitation, atau simple deconditioning. Satu pasien dapat memiliki beberapa.

### 3. Pemeriksaan terarah

History → vitals → breathing pattern → auscultation bila kompeten → cough/sputum → chest mobility → functional capacity. Pilih Borg/mMRC/6MWT atau task bedside sesuai severity.

### 4. Differential contributor

infection/exacerbation, heart failure, pulmonary embolism, pneumothorax, anemia/deconditioning, musculoskeletal chest pain depending presentation.

### 5. Intervensi sesuai masalah

Secretion → airway clearance dan mobilization. Low lung expansion → thoracic expansion/position/mobility. Dyspnea obstructive → breathing control/pursed lip pada responder. Deconditioning → graded aerobic/resistance. Post-op → breathing/cough + early mobility. Selalu monitor response.

## Progression

Bed/chair activity → standing → corridor walking → interval endurance → continuous endurance → stair/community. Progress berdasarkan symptoms dan vital response, bukan hanya jumlah hari rawat.

## SOAP kardiorespirasi

S: dyspnea, cough/sputum, fatigue, sleep/orthopnea, activity tolerance.

O: pre/during/post vitals, breath sound/cough, distance/task, Borg.

A: apakah limitation respiratory, cardiac, peripheral muscle, fear, atau combination?

P: tulis dose walking/exercise berikutnya dan airway strategy yang dipertahankan/dihentikan.

## Deep Dive: mekanisme sampai evaluasi

### Patofisiologi yang perlu benar-benar dipahami

Patchy bronchiolar/alveolar infection causes inflammation, mucus/exudate and consolidation. Ventilation falls in affected units; V/Q mismatch and increased work of breathing can cause dyspnea. Dense consolidation transmits tactile/vocal vibration better and may produce percussion dullness.

### Pemeriksaan terarah

Vitals including RR/SpO2/temperature, work of breathing, cough/sputum, inspection, palpation chest expansion/fremitus, auscultation and percussion when competent. Check mobility and fatigue. In children include feeding/play and caregiver report.

### Intervensi berdasarkan target impairment

Medical antimicrobial/supportive therapy treats infection. Physiotherapy targets secretion retention, ventilation/mobility and deconditioning only when present: positioning, mobilization, breathing control, huff/cough and selected airway clearance. Avoid routine aggressive chest percussion without indication.

### Evaluasi dan kriteria progression

RR, oxygen need/SpO2, work of breathing, auscultation, sputum, dyspnea and mobility. Fever or respiratory deterioration takes priority over exercise progression.

### Differential / kondisi yang jangan terlewat

Asthma/exacerbation, atelectasis, pleural effusion, pneumothorax and heart failure.
