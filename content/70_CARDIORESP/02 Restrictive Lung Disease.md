## Basis materi

Materi Penyakit Paru Restriksi dan fisiologi respirasi.

## Definisi

Restrictive pattern berarti reduced lung expansion/volume akibat pulmonary parenchymal atau extrapulmonary restriction.

## Patofisiologi dan hubungan sebab-akibat

Interstitial/parenchymal fibrosis meningkatkan stiffness dan menurunkan compliance. Extrapulmonary restriction dapat berasal chest wall, obesity, neuromuscular weakness, pleural problem. Tidal volume dan vital capacity dapat menurun sehingga RR meningkat untuk mempertahankan ventilation. Diffusion impairment dapat menyebabkan exertional desaturation pada parenchymal disease.

## Manifestasi dan impairment

Dyspnea, tachypnea, reduced deep inspiration, low exercise tolerance, dry cough pada ILD, reduced chest expansion.

## Pemeriksaan fisioterapi

History etiology, dyspnea, cough, oxygen, exercise. RR/SpO2 at rest/exercise, chest expansion, auscultation crackles bila present, respiratory muscle function, 6MWT. Pulmonary function shows reduced volumes with context.

## ICF / problem list

Pisahkan impairment, activity limitation, participation restriction, faktor personal, dan faktor lingkungan. Pilih problem prioritas berdasarkan safety, tujuan pasien, dan modifiability.

## Intervensi

Aerobic conditioning individualized, breathing/chest mobility, respiratory muscle training pada selected weakness, pacing, oxygen use per medical prescription. Airway clearance hanya bila secretion present, bukan otomatis.

## Progression dan evaluasi

6MWT, SpO2 response, dyspnea, RR recovery, functional tolerance.

## Red flags / escalation

Rapid desaturation, acute dyspnea, chest pain, infection signs, new hemoptysis.

## Practical workflow kardiorespirasi

### 1. Stability sebelum exercise

Pada Restrictive Lung Disease, ukur atau review HR, BP, RR, SpO2 dan symptom sebelum intervensi sesuai setting. Observasi work of breathing, color, cognition, edema atau line/tube. Pertanyaan pertama bukan “teknik apa?”, tetapi “apakah pasien cukup stabil untuk teknik ini?”.

### 2. Tentukan primary limitation

Bedakan secretion retention, ventilation/chest expansion problem, dyspnea control, impaired oxygenation, low exercise capacity, cardiac output limitation, atau simple deconditioning. Satu pasien dapat memiliki beberapa.

### 3. Pemeriksaan terarah

History → vitals → breathing pattern → auscultation bila kompeten → cough/sputum → chest mobility → functional capacity. Pilih Borg/mMRC/6MWT atau task bedside sesuai severity.

### 4. Differential contributor

infection/exacerbation, heart failure, pulmonary embolism, pneumothorax, anemia/deconditioning, musculoskeletal chest pain depending presentation.

### 5. Intervensi sesuai masalah

Secretion → airway clearance dan mobilization. Low lung expansion → thoracic expansion/position/mobility. Dyspnea obstructive → breathing control/pursed lip pada responder. Deconditioning → graded aerobic/resistance. Post-op → breathing/cough + early mobility. Selalu monitor response.

## Progression

Bed/chair activity → standing → corridor walking → interval endurance → continuous endurance → stair/community. Progress berdasarkan symptoms dan vital response, bukan hanya jumlah hari rawat.

## SOAP kardiorespirasi

S: dyspnea, cough/sputum, fatigue, sleep/orthopnea, activity tolerance.

O: pre/during/post vitals, breath sound/cough, distance/task, Borg.

A: apakah limitation respiratory, cardiac, peripheral muscle, fear, atau combination?

P: tulis dose walking/exercise berikutnya dan airway strategy yang dipertahankan/dihentikan.

## Deep Dive: mekanisme sampai evaluasi

### Patofisiologi yang perlu benar-benar dipahami

Restrictive pattern reflects reduced lung volume from parenchymal stiffness (intrinsic) or chest wall/neuromuscular/obesity causes (extrinsic). Reduced compliance raises inspiratory work and may cause rapid shallow breathing and exercise desaturation.

### Pemeriksaan terarah

History dyspnea, cough, occupational/systemic disease, neuromuscular/chest wall condition. Observe RR/pattern, chest expansion, auscultation and SpO2 at rest/exertion. Review pulmonary function showing reduced volumes with appropriate ratio pattern. Assess 6MWT and muscle capacity.

### Intervensi berdasarkan target impairment

Aerobic/resistance rehabilitation, pacing and breathing/chest mobility as indicated. Oxygen prescription is medical. Airway clearance only if secretion issue. In neuromuscular restriction, cough strength and respiratory muscle support become more important.

### Evaluasi dan kriteria progression

Exercise distance, dyspnea, SpO2 response, chest mobility and activity. Significant desaturation or rapid decline requires medical review.

### Differential / kondisi yang jangan terlewat

Obstruction, heart failure, pulmonary vascular disease and deconditioning.
