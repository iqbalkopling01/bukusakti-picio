## Basis materi

Materi ACL Guide, sports injury, literature review exercise.

## Definisi

ACL injury causes mechanical and sensorimotor knee instability. Reconstruction may be performed depending patient/sport.

## Patofisiologi dan hubungan sebab-akibat

Rupture reduces anterior/rotational passive stability. Effusion inhibits quadriceps. After reconstruction graft undergoes healing/remodeling while neuromuscular and whole-limb capacity must recover.

## Manifestasi dan impairment

Swelling, extension loss, quadriceps weakness, instability, fear, reduced jump/change-direction.

## Pemeriksaan fisioterapi

Effusion, ROM especially extension, quad strength, gait, squat, balance. Later dynamometry, hop battery, jump landing, running, agility, psychological readiness.

## ICF / problem list

Pisahkan impairment, activity limitation, participation restriction, faktor personal, dan faktor lingkungan. Pilih problem prioritas berdasarkan safety, tujuan pasien, dan modifiability.

## Intervensi

Early extension/quad activation, swelling control, gait. Progressive strength quad/hamstring/hip, running criteria, plyometric, deceleration, change direction, sport practice.

## Progression dan evaluasi

Criterion-based: no/low effusion, full functional ROM, strength and hop quality, running/COD tolerance, readiness. Limb symmetry alone can hide bilateral weakness.

## Red flags / escalation

New instability, locked knee, DVT, infection post-op.

## Practical workflow saat pasien datang

### 1. Triage 2–3 menit

Untuk ACL Rehabilitation and Return to Sport, jangan langsung melakukan special test. Pastikan mekanisme dan symptom behavior masuk pola yang masuk akal. Tanyakan trauma, onset, irritability, kemampuan weight bearing atau menggunakan anggota gerak, night/systemic symptoms, neurologic symptoms, dan tujuan aktivitas. Lakukan red-flag screen sebelum loading agresif.

### 2. Comparable sign

Pilih satu sampai tiga gerakan atau task yang mereproduksi masalah secara aman, misalnya squat, reach, stair, gait, grip, shoulder elevation, atau sport movement. Comparable sign ini akan dipakai untuk melihat apakah pemeriksaan dan intervensi benar-benar mengubah fungsi.

### 3. Scan objektif

Urutan yang efisien: observation → AROM → PROM bila perlu → resisted/muscle performance → palpation terarah → neurological screen jika ada radiation/paresthesia → special test berdasarkan hipotesis → functional test. Jangan melakukan semua special test yang dihafal. Pilih tes yang dapat membedakan hipotesis.

### 4. Differential yang harus tetap di kepala

fracture, meniscal injury, ligament injury, patellofemoral pain, hip referral, lumbar/neural referral, inflammatory/infective joint.

### 5. Setelah tes

Rangkum temuan menjadi pola: pain-dominant, stiffness-dominant, weakness/capacity-dominant, instability, neural, atau mixed. Pilih intervensi yang menargetkan pola tersebut.

## Contoh goal dan progression

Short-term goal harus terukur, misalnya “dalam 2 minggu pasien meningkatkan knee extension dari -10° menjadi -3° dan melakukan sit-to-stand 5 kali tanpa peningkatan nyeri > baseline keesokan hari.” Long-term goal berbasis aktivitas, misalnya kembali berjalan 30 menit, naik tangga, mengangkat barang, atau kembali latihan.

Progression umumnya bergerak dari controlled low-load → full ROM/load → higher resistance/endurance → speed/power → complex functional/sport task. Jika condition berupa fracture/post-op, progression tetap tunduk pada biological healing dan precaution.

## Contoh evaluasi SOAP singkat

S: pasien melaporkan perubahan nyeri, stiffness, confidence, dan activity tolerance sejak sesi sebelumnya.

O: ulangi comparable sign dan minimal satu ukuran objektif seperti ROM, force, gait, sit-to-stand, hop, atau task. Catat intervensi dan dosis aktual.

A: jelaskan apakah kapasitas meningkat, gejala stabil, atau loading terlalu tinggi. Hubungkan perubahan dengan goal.

P: pertahankan, progress, regress, atau ubah intervensi. Tentukan apa yang akan diukur sesi berikutnya.

## Deep Dive: mekanisme sampai evaluasi

### Patofisiologi yang perlu benar-benar dipahami

ACL injury disrupts anterior/rotational knee stability and often includes bone/meniscus/cartilage injury. Reconstruction requires graft healing plus recovery of quadriceps force, neuromuscular control and sport capacity.

### Pemeriksaan terarah

Effusion, extension/flexion, quadriceps activation, gait, strength, hop/jump, landing, change direction and patient confidence. Respect graft/surgeon/meniscus restrictions. Extension loss and persistent effusion are key barriers.

### Intervensi berdasarkan target impairment

Early full extension/quad activation and gait, progressive strength, running criteria, plyometric, acceleration/deceleration, COD and sport-specific exposure. Return-to-sport is criteria-based plus adequate biological time and shared decision, not single hop symmetry.

### Evaluasi dan kriteria progression

Effusion/ROM, quadriceps and hamstring force, hop battery, running/COD quality, training exposure and psychological readiness.

### Differential / kondisi yang jangan terlewat

Cyclops/arthrofibrosis, meniscal injury, graft failure, DVT and patellofemoral pain.
