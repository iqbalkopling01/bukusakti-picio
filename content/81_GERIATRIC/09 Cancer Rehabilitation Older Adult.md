## Basis materi

Materi Neoplasm geriatri dan oncology.

## Definisi

Cancer rehab menangani impairment akibat tumor, surgery, systemic therapy, radiation dan deconditioning.

## Patofisiologi dan hubungan sebab-akibat

Mechanism depends cancer/treatment: fatigue, neuropathy, muscle loss, cardiotoxicity, bone metastasis, lymphedema. Older adult reserve lebih rendah.

## Manifestasi dan impairment

Fatigue, weakness, pain, balance, neuropathy, reduced endurance, ADL.

## Pemeriksaan fisioterapi

Cancer type/stage/treatment, blood counts if relevant via team, bone metastasis, lines, neuropathy, fatigue, function, falls.

## ICF / problem list

Pisahkan impairment, activity limitation, participation restriction, faktor personal, dan faktor lingkungan. Pilih problem prioritas berdasarkan safety, tujuan pasien, dan modifiability.

## Intervensi

Individual exercise, balance, strength, aerobic, lymphedema care if trained, energy management, functional task. Modify load at bone lesion.

## Progression dan evaluasi

Fatigue, walking, strength, participation.

## Red flags / escalation

Fever/neutropenic illness, pathological fracture, spinal cord compression, acute DVT/PE.

## Practical workflow geriatri

### 1. Jangan menganggap decline sebagai “karena umur”

Pada Cancer Rehabilitation in Older Adults, tanyakan baseline sebelum masalah, onset decline, falls, medication, cognition, vision/hearing, nutrition, continence, social support dan environment. Acute decline selalu membutuhkan kemungkinan medical cause.

### 2. Pemeriksaan fungsi

Vitals termasuk orthostatic bila perlu → gait/device → 5xSTS/TUG → balance → strength → endurance → cognition screen sesuai indikasi → ADL/environment. Pilih tes secukupnya agar pasien tidak kelelahan sebelum latihan.

### 3. Differential contributor

regional mechanical disorder, neurological disorder, vascular disorder, infection/inflammation, fracture/trauma, systemic disease.

### 4. Intervention

Gunakan multicomponent approach: strength terutama lower limb/power, balance/reactive stepping, gait, aerobic, task practice dan home safety. Untuk frail patient, mulai volume rendah tetapi jangan berhenti pada exercise sangat ringan selamanya jika kapasitas membaik.

## Evaluasi

Outcome harus menangkap perubahan independence, bukan angka test saja. Catat falls/near falls, device level, walking outside home dan caregiver assistance.

## SOAP

S: fall, dizziness, fatigue, confidence, ADL.

O: TUG/5xSTS/gait/vitals + task.

A: faktor risiko yang paling modifiable saat ini.

P: progression dan strategi safety berikutnya.

## Deep Dive: mekanisme sampai evaluasi

### Patofisiologi yang perlu benar-benar dipahami

Cancer and treatment can cause fatigue, sarcopenia, neuropathy, cardiopulmonary toxicity, bone fragility and functional decline. Older adults have less reserve and more comorbidity.

### Pemeriksaan terarah

Treatment status, bone metastasis, blood counts/medical restrictions, fatigue, pain, neuropathy, balance, strength, gait, ADL and cardiopulmonary response.

### Intervensi berdasarkan target impairment

Individualized aerobic/resistance/balance at tolerable dose, energy management, fall prevention, lymphedema/wound referral and task training. Modify load for bone metastasis, severe cytopenia or acute treatment toxicity.

### Evaluasi dan kriteria progression

Fatigue, strength, gait, ADL, participation and treatment tolerance.

### Differential / kondisi yang jangan terlewat

Infection, thrombosis, pathologic fracture and cardiotoxicity.
