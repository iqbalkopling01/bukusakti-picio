## Basis materi

Materi Alzheimer/dementia, MMSE, Clock Drawing.

## Definisi

Dementia adalah syndrome cognitive decline mengganggu independence. Alzheimer merupakan neurodegenerative cause common.

## Patofisiologi dan hubungan sebab-akibat

Progressive synaptic/neuronal loss pada Alzheimer memengaruhi memory, orientation, executive dan later motor/function. Physical inactivity dan fall risk meningkat.

## Manifestasi dan impairment

Memory, navigation, judgment, dual-task, gait slowing, ADL dependence.

## Pemeriksaan fisioterapi

Caregiver history, cognition screen MMSE/clock as appropriate, gait, TUG, balance, strength, environment, behavior, communication.

## ICF / problem list

Pisahkan impairment, activity limitation, participation restriction, faktor personal, dan faktor lingkungan. Pilih problem prioritas berdasarkan safety, tujuan pasien, dan modifiability.

## Intervensi

Simple consistent exercise, strength, walking, balance, cueing, routine, caregiver participation, environment safety. Use one-step commands when needed.

## Progression dan evaluasi

Falls, mobility, participation, caregiver burden/function; cognitive score not sole rehab outcome.

## Red flags / escalation

Acute delirium, sudden cognitive change, focal neuro signs, infection.

## Practical workflow geriatri

### 1. Jangan menganggap decline sebagai “karena umur”

Pada Dementia and Alzheimer Disease, tanyakan baseline sebelum masalah, onset decline, falls, medication, cognition, vision/hearing, nutrition, continence, social support dan environment. Acute decline selalu membutuhkan kemungkinan medical cause.

### 2. Pemeriksaan fungsi

Vitals termasuk orthostatic bila perlu → gait/device → 5xSTS/TUG → balance → strength → endurance → cognition screen sesuai indikasi → ADL/environment. Pilih tes secukupnya agar pasien tidak kelelahan sebelum latihan.

### 3. Differential contributor

acute delirium/illness, orthostatic hypotension, medication effect, vestibular/sensory deficit, neurologic event, fracture/pain, deconditioning/frailty.

### 4. Intervention

Gunakan multicomponent approach: strength terutama lower limb/power, balance/reactive stepping, gait, aerobic, task practice dan home safety. Untuk frail patient, mulai volume rendah tetapi jangan berhenti pada exercise sangat ringan selamanya jika kapasitas membaik.

## Evaluasi

Outcome harus menangkap perubahan independence, bukan angka test saja. Catat falls/near falls, device level, walking outside home dan caregiver assistance.

## SOAP

S: fall, dizziness, fatigue, confidence, ADL.

O: TUG/5xSTS/gait/vitals + task.

A: faktor risiko yang paling modifiable saat ini.

P: progression dan strategi safety berikutnya.

## Deep Dive: mekanisme sampai evaluasi

### Patofisiologi yang perlu benar-benar dipahami

Neurodegenerative cognitive decline affects memory, executive function, spatial orientation and later motor/ADL performance. Physical inactivity, falls and caregiver dependence can increase as disease progresses.

### Pemeriksaan terarah

Caregiver history, baseline cognition/behavior, mobility, falls, ADL, vision/hearing and pain. Use simple cognitive screens such as MMSE/Clock Drawing if appropriate and authorized, but function and caregiver report are central.

### Intervensi berdasarkan target impairment

Repetitive familiar task practice, walking/aerobic activity, strength/balance, environmental cueing and caregiver education. Keep instructions short, routine consistent and task meaningful. Avoid complex dual-task when unsafe.

### Evaluasi dan kriteria progression

Falls, walking, transfers, ADL assistance, participation and caregiver burden. Cognitive score may change slowly and is not the only rehab outcome.

### Differential / kondisi yang jangan terlewat

Delirium, depression, medication effects, stroke and normal pressure hydrocephalus.
