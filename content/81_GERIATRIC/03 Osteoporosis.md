## Basis materi

Materi gangguan postural dan osteoporosis geriatri, strengthening precautions.

## Definisi

Osteoporosis adalah skeletal disorder dengan reduced bone strength dan fracture risk.

## Patofisiologi dan hubungan sebab-akibat

Bone remodeling imbalance menghasilkan lower bone mass/microarchitecture. Aging, hormonal factors, inactivity dan medication/conditions contribute. Vertebral/hip/wrist fragility fracture menyebabkan disability.

## Manifestasi dan impairment

May asymptomatic until fracture, kyphosis/height loss, back pain after compression fracture, fear movement.

## Pemeriksaan fisioterapi

Fracture history, posture, balance/fall risk, strength, functional task. Do not perform high-force spinal manipulation on fragile bone.

## ICF / problem list

Pisahkan impairment, activity limitation, participation restriction, faktor personal, dan faktor lingkungan. Pilih problem prioritas berdasarkan safety, tujuan pasien, dan modifiability.

## Intervensi

Weight-bearing activity, progressive resistance, balance/fall prevention, spinal extensor/posture, safe lifting. Avoid loaded end-range spinal flexion/twisting in high fracture risk depending individual.

## Progression dan evaluasi

Falls, strength, function, posture; bone density monitored medically not by PT session.

## Red flags / escalation

New severe spine pain after minor load, suspected fracture, neurologic deficit.

## Practical workflow geriatri

### 1. Jangan menganggap decline sebagai “karena umur”

Pada Osteoporosis, tanyakan baseline sebelum masalah, onset decline, falls, medication, cognition, vision/hearing, nutrition, continence, social support dan environment. Acute decline selalu membutuhkan kemungkinan medical cause.

### 2. Pemeriksaan fungsi

Vitals termasuk orthostatic bila perlu → gait/device → 5xSTS/TUG → balance → strength → endurance → cognition screen sesuai indikasi → ADL/environment. Pilih tes secukupnya agar pasien tidak kelelahan sebelum latihan.

### 3. Differential contributor

acute delirium/illness, orthostatic hypotension, medication effect, vestibular/sensory deficit, neurologic event, fracture/pain, deconditioning/frailty.

### 4. Intervention

Gunakan multicomponent approach: strength terutama lower limb/power, balance/reactive stepping, gait, aerobic, task practice dan home safety. Untuk frail patient, mulai volume rendah tetapi jangan berhenti pada exercise sangat ringan selamanya jika kapasitas membaik.

## Evaluasi

Outcome harus menangkap perubahan independence, bukan angka test saja. Catat falls/near falls, device level, walking outside home dan caregiver assistance.

## SOAP

S: fall, dizziness, fatigue, confidence, ADL.

O: TUG/5xSTS/gait/vitals + task.

A: faktor risiko yang paling modifiable saat ini.

P: progression dan strategi safety berikutnya.

## Deep Dive: mekanisme sampai evaluasi

### Patofisiologi yang perlu benar-benar dipahami

Bone mineral and microarchitectural loss increase fragility fracture risk. Immobilization, age, hormonal/nutritional and medication factors contribute. Vertebral fractures can alter kyphosis and balance.

### Pemeriksaan terarah

Fracture history, fall risk, pain, posture, strength, balance and medical bone density/risk. Avoid loaded spinal flexion/twisting in high-risk vertebral fracture context. Screen acute fracture with new severe pain.

### Intervensi berdasarkan target impairment

Progressive resistance, weight-bearing impact appropriate to fracture risk, balance/fall prevention and postural extensor exercise. Teach safe lifting and activity. Medical anti-osteoporosis therapy/nutrition is coordinated with team.

### Evaluasi dan kriteria progression

Strength, balance, falls, functional loading and fracture occurrence; BMD changes require longer medical follow-up.

### Differential / kondisi yang jangan terlewat

Metastatic bone disease, osteomalacia and acute compression fracture.
