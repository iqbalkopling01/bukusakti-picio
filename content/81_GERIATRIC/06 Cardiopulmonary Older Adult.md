## Basis materi

Materi FTG Cardiopulmonal dan kardiorespirasi.

## Definisi

Aging menurunkan reserve cardiovascular/respiratory dan comorbidity lebih banyak sehingga deconditioning mudah terjadi.

## Patofisiologi dan hubungan sebab-akibat

Reduced maximal HR response, arterial stiffness, chest wall/lung changes, muscle loss dan disease combine to reduce exercise tolerance.

## Manifestasi dan impairment

Dyspnea, fatigue, slower walking, prolonged recovery.

## Pemeriksaan fisioterapi

Vitals, medication, 6MWT, Borg, SpO2, falls, strength, functional activity.

## ICF / problem list

Pisahkan impairment, activity limitation, participation restriction, faktor personal, dan faktor lingkungan. Pilih problem prioritas berdasarkan safety, tujuan pasien, dan modifiability.

## Intervensi

Aerobic interval/continuous, resistance, breathing, pacing, early mobilization post illness, monitor response.

## Progression dan evaluasi

6MWT, RPE, ADL, recovery.

## Red flags / escalation

Chest pain, syncope, unstable HF, severe desaturation.

## Practical workflow geriatri

### 1. Jangan menganggap decline sebagai “karena umur”

Pada Cardiopulmonary Problems in Older Adults, tanyakan baseline sebelum masalah, onset decline, falls, medication, cognition, vision/hearing, nutrition, continence, social support dan environment. Acute decline selalu membutuhkan kemungkinan medical cause.

### 2. Pemeriksaan fungsi

Vitals termasuk orthostatic bila perlu → gait/device → 5xSTS/TUG → balance → strength → endurance → cognition screen sesuai indikasi → ADL/environment. Pilih tes secukupnya agar pasien tidak kelelahan sebelum latihan.

### 3. Differential contributor

regional mechanical disorder, neurological disorder, vascular disorder, infection/inflammation, fracture/trauma, systemic disease.

### 4. Intervention

Gunakan multicomponent approach: strength terutama lower limb/power, balance/reactive stepping, gait, aerobic, task practice dan home safety. Untuk frail patient, mulai volume rendah tetapi jangan berhenti pada exercise sangat ringan selamanya jika kapasitas membaik.

## Evaluasi

Outcome harus menangkap perubahan independence, bukan angka test saja. Catat falls/near falls, device level, walking outside home dan caregiver assistance.

## SOAP

S: fall, dizziness, fatigue, confidence, ADL.

O: TUG/5xSTS/gait/vitals + task.

A: faktor risiko yang paling modifiable saat ini.

P: progression dan strategi safety berikutnya.

## Deep Dive: mekanisme sampai evaluasi

### Patofisiologi yang perlu benar-benar dipahami

Aging lowers maximal HR response, vascular compliance, respiratory elastic recoil and muscle reserve; disease adds heart failure/COPD/ischemia. Symptoms may present atypically.

### Pemeriksaan terarah

Vitals including orthostatic when needed, dyspnea/fatigue, edema, auscultation if relevant, exercise response, gait and strength. Medication and comorbidity strongly influence response.

### Intervensi berdasarkan target impairment

Graded aerobic and resistance training, breathing/pacing when pulmonary limitation, balance and functional conditioning. Increase workload gradually with longer warm-up/recovery when needed.

### Evaluasi dan kriteria progression

6MWT, Borg, HR/BP/SpO2, ADL and recovery time.

### Differential / kondisi yang jangan terlewat

Anemia, infection, medication, deconditioning and acute cardiac/pulmonary event.
