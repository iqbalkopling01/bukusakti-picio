## Basis materi

Materi aging pathological sensorium/vestibular, jatuh.

## Definisi

Age-related vestibular decline dan BPPV/other vestibular disorders meningkatkan imbalance risk.

## Patofisiologi dan hubungan sebab-akibat

Reduced vestibular hair cell/function, vision/proprioception decline menyebabkan sensory redundancy berkurang. Compensation lebih sulit saat satu input terganggu.

## Manifestasi dan impairment

Dizziness, unsteadiness, positional vertigo, fall, oscillopsia.

## Pemeriksaan fisioterapi

Timing/trigger, positional tests if BPPV, head impulse/gaze stability by trained clinician, balance eyes open/closed, gait head turn, fall risk.

## ICF / problem list

Pisahkan impairment, activity limitation, participation restriction, faktor personal, dan faktor lingkungan. Pilih problem prioritas berdasarkan safety, tujuan pasien, dan modifiability.

## Intervensi

Canalith reposition for BPPV, gaze stabilization/habituation for stable vestibular hypofunction, balance and gait, fall prevention.

## Progression dan evaluasi

Dizziness, positional test, dynamic balance, falls.

## Red flags / escalation

Central neurologic signs, new severe headache, acute hearing loss, inability stand.

## Practical workflow geriatri

### 1. Jangan menganggap decline sebagai “karena umur”

Pada Vestibular Dysfunction in Older Adults, tanyakan baseline sebelum masalah, onset decline, falls, medication, cognition, vision/hearing, nutrition, continence, social support dan environment. Acute decline selalu membutuhkan kemungkinan medical cause.

### 2. Pemeriksaan fungsi

Vitals termasuk orthostatic bila perlu → gait/device → 5xSTS/TUG → balance → strength → endurance → cognition screen sesuai indikasi → ADL/environment. Pilih tes secukupnya agar pasien tidak kelelahan sebelum latihan.

### 3. Differential contributor

regional mechanical disorder, neurological disorder, vascular disorder, infection/inflammation, fracture/trauma, systemic disease.

### 4. Intervention

Gunakan multicomponent approach: strength terutama lower limb/power, balance/reactive stepping, gait, aerobic, task practice dan home safety. Untuk frail patient, mulai volume rendah tetapi jangan berhenti pada exercise sangat ringan selamanya jika kapasitas membaik.

## Evaluasi

Outcome harus menangkap perubahan independence, bukan angka test saja. Catat falls/near falls, device level, walking outside home dan caregiver assistance.

## SOAP

S: fall, dizziness, fatigue, confidence, ADL.

O: TUG/5xSTS/gait/vitals + task.

A: faktor risiko yang paling modifiable saat ini.

P: progression dan strategi safety berikutnya.

## Deep Dive: mekanisme sampai evaluasi

### Patofisiologi yang perlu benar-benar dipahami

Age-related vestibular hair-cell/nerve decline, visual/proprioceptive changes and central integration reduce balance reserve. Older adults can also have specific BPPV or vestibular hypofunction.

### Pemeriksaan terarah

History vertigo vs imbalance, triggers, falls, hearing/neuro symptoms, orthostatic BP. Positional tests for BPPV when indicated, head impulse/gaze stability within competency, balance under sensory conditions and gait/turn.

### Intervensi berdasarkan target impairment

Repositioning for confirmed BPPV, gaze stabilization/adaptation for hypofunction, habituation when appropriate, balance and gait with sensory challenge, strength and fall prevention.

### Evaluasi dan kriteria progression

Dizziness frequency, positional test, gait/balance, falls and confidence.

### Differential / kondisi yang jangan terlewat

Central neurologic disease, orthostatic hypotension, medication, vision and neuropathy.
