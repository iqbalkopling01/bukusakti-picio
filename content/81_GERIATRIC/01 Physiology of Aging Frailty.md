## Basis materi

Materi FT Geriatri Aging, sensory-motor-cognitive-personal function.

## Definisi

Aging adalah perubahan fisiologis progresif yang meningkatkan heterogeneity dan menurunkan reserve pada berbagai sistem. Frailty adalah state vulnerability akibat reduced reserve multi-system.

## Patofisiologi dan hubungan sebab-akibat

Sarcopenia, reduced bone density, slower neural processing, sensory decline, cardiovascular/respiratory reserve reduction dan recovery lebih lambat meningkatkan risk disability. Frailty bukan age semata.

## Manifestasi dan impairment

Reduced strength/power, slower gait, fatigue, balance deficit, activity reduction.

## Pemeriksaan fisioterapi

Grip/strength, 5xSTS, gait speed, TUG, balance, endurance, cognition, nutrition/fall history, ADL.

## ICF / problem list

Pisahkan impairment, activity limitation, participation restriction, faktor personal, dan faktor lingkungan. Pilih problem prioritas berdasarkan safety, tujuan pasien, dan modifiability.

## Intervensi

Progressive resistance especially power component, balance, aerobic, functional task, protein/nutrition referral, fall prevention, social/activity engagement.

## Progression dan evaluasi

Gait speed, 5xSTS, TUG, 6MWT, falls, ADL participation.

## Red flags / escalation

Unexplained rapid decline, weight loss, recurrent syncope, acute confusion.

## Practical workflow geriatri

### 1. Jangan menganggap decline sebagai “karena umur”

Pada Physiology of Aging and Frailty, tanyakan baseline sebelum masalah, onset decline, falls, medication, cognition, vision/hearing, nutrition, continence, social support dan environment. Acute decline selalu membutuhkan kemungkinan medical cause.

### 2. Pemeriksaan fungsi

Vitals termasuk orthostatic bila perlu → gait/device → 5xSTS/TUG → balance → strength → endurance → cognition screen sesuai indikasi → ADL/environment. Pilih tes secukupnya agar pasien tidak kelelahan sebelum latihan.

### 3. Differential contributor

acute delirium/illness, orthostatic hypotension, medication effect, vestibular/sensory deficit, neurologic event, fracture/pain, deconditioning/frailty.

### 4. Intervention

Gunakan multicomponent approach: strength terutama lower limb/power, balance/reactive stepping, gait, aerobic, task practice dan home safety. Untuk frail patient, mulai volume rendah tetapi jangan berhenti pada exercise sangat ringan selamanya jika kapasitas membaik.

## Evaluasi

Outcome harus menangkap perubahan independence, bukan angka test saja. Catat falls/near falls, device level, walking outside home dan caregiver assistance.

## SOAP

S: fall, dizziness, fatigue, confidence, ADL.

O: TUG/5xSTS/gait/vitals + task.

A: faktor risiko yang paling modifiable saat ini.

P: progression dan strategi safety berikutnya.

## Deep Dive: mekanisme sampai evaluasi

### Patofisiologi yang perlu benar-benar dipahami

Aging reduces reserve across muscle, bone, sensory, neural, cardiac and respiratory systems. Frailty represents multisystem vulnerability and poor recovery from stressor, often overlapping sarcopenia, malnutrition and inactivity but not equivalent to chronological age.

### Pemeriksaan terarah

Baseline vs recent decline, falls, weight/nutrition, medications, cognition, hearing/vision and social support. Functional measures: gait speed, 5xSTS, grip/strength, TUG, balance, endurance and ADL. Orthostatic vitals when indicated.

### Intervensi berdasarkan target impairment

Multicomponent exercise with progressive resistance and power, balance/reactive stepping, aerobic conditioning and functional task. Start at tolerable dose but progressively challenge. Coordinate nutrition/protein and medical causes of decline.

### Evaluasi dan kriteria progression

Gait speed, 5xSTS/TUG, strength, 6MWT, falls, ADL and participation. Recovery time after sessions matters.

### Differential / kondisi yang jangan terlewat

Acute illness/delirium, heart failure, anemia, depression, medication effects and neurologic disease.
