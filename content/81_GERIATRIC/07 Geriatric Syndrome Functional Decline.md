## Basis materi

Materi FTG Sindrom Geriatri.

## Definisi

Geriatric syndrome adalah multifactorial problem seperti frailty, falls, immobility, incontinence, cognitive decline, pressure injury yang tidak berasal satu organ saja.

## Patofisiologi dan hubungan sebab-akibat

Multiple small deficits interact sehingga functional threshold terlewati. Bed rest singkat dapat menghasilkan disproportionate loss pada frail adult.

## Manifestasi dan impairment

Immobility, dependency, recurrent falls, fatigue, low reserve.

## Pemeriksaan fisioterapi

Comprehensive mobility, cognition, continence context, nutrition, skin, medication, environment, caregiver, goals.

## ICF / problem list

Pisahkan impairment, activity limitation, participation restriction, faktor personal, dan faktor lingkungan. Pilih problem prioritas berdasarkan safety, tujuan pasien, dan modifiability.

## Intervensi

Multicomponent exercise, early mobility, task practice, positioning, pressure relief, caregiver education, interdisciplinary referral.

## Progression dan evaluasi

ADL, mobility assistance, gait, strength, falls, participation.

## Red flags / escalation

Acute decline should be treated as possible illness, not “normal aging”.

## Practical workflow geriatri

### 1. Jangan menganggap decline sebagai “karena umur”

Pada Geriatric Syndromes and Functional Decline, tanyakan baseline sebelum masalah, onset decline, falls, medication, cognition, vision/hearing, nutrition, continence, social support dan environment. Acute decline selalu membutuhkan kemungkinan medical cause.

### 2. Pemeriksaan fungsi

Vitals termasuk orthostatic bila perlu → gait/device → 5xSTS/TUG → balance → strength → endurance → cognition screen sesuai indikasi → ADL/environment. Pilih tes secukupnya agar pasien tidak kelelahan sebelum latihan.

### 3. Differential contributor

acute delirium/illness, orthostatic hypotension, medication effect, vestibular/sensory deficit, neurologic event, fracture/pain, deconditioning/frailty.

### 4. Intervention

Gunakan multicomponent approach: strength terutama lower limb/power, balance/reactive stepping, gait, aerobic, task practice dan home safety. Untuk frail patient, mulai volume rendah tetapi jangan berhenti pada exercise sangat ringan selamanya jika kapasitas membaik.

## Evaluasi

Outcome harus menangkap perubahan independence, bukan angka test saja. Catat falls/near falls, device level, walking outside home dan caregiver assistance.

## SOAP

S: fall, dizziness, fatigue, confidence, ADL.

O: TUG/5xSTS/gait/vitals + task.

A: faktor risiko yang paling modifiable saat ini.

P: progression dan strategi safety berikutnya.

## Deep Dive: mekanisme sampai evaluasi

### Patofisiologi yang perlu benar-benar dipahami

Geriatric syndromes such as falls, frailty, immobility, incontinence and cognitive decline share multifactorial mechanisms. Acute hospitalization can rapidly reduce muscle and mobility.

### Pemeriksaan terarah

Compare pre-morbid baseline with current. Review cognition, nutrition, medications, continence, skin, strength, balance, gait, endurance, ADL and social support.

### Intervensi berdasarkan target impairment

Multicomponent rehabilitation, early mobility, strength/power, balance, ADL task, device and caregiver/environment planning. Address reversible medical contributors with team.

### Evaluasi dan kriteria progression

Assistance level, walking, ADL, discharge destination, falls and participation.

### Differential / kondisi yang jangan terlewat

Delirium, infection, cardiac/neurologic events, fracture and depression.
