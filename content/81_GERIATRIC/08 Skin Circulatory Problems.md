## Basis materi

Materi blood vessel/circulatory/skin disorders geriatri.

## Definisi

Venous, arterial, edema, skin fragility dan pressure injury lebih common dengan aging/comorbidity.

## Patofisiologi dan hubungan sebab-akibat

Reduced mobility dan calf pump contribute venous stasis. Arterial disease lowers perfusion. Pressure + shear over bony prominence damages tissue especially with sensory/nutrition issue.

## Manifestasi dan impairment

Edema, skin color/temp changes, ulcer, pain, reduced walking.

## Pemeriksaan fisioterapi

Skin inspection, pulses/circulation screening within scope, edema, sensation, mobility, pressure risk. Differentiate arterial vs venous pattern before compression.

## ICF / problem list

Pisahkan impairment, activity limitation, participation restriction, faktor personal, dan faktor lingkungan. Pilih problem prioritas berdasarkan safety, tujuan pasien, dan modifiability.

## Intervensi

Mobility/calf pump, elevation for selected venous edema, compression only when arterial status adequate and prescribed/within scope, pressure relief, positioning, exercise.

## Progression dan evaluasi

Edema, wound dimensions per wound team, walking, skin tolerance.

## Red flags / escalation

Acute limb ischemia, DVT, infected wound/sepsis, rapidly spreading skin change.

## Practical workflow geriatri

### 1. Jangan menganggap decline sebagai “karena umur”

Pada Skin and Circulatory Problems in Geriatrics, tanyakan baseline sebelum masalah, onset decline, falls, medication, cognition, vision/hearing, nutrition, continence, social support dan environment. Acute decline selalu membutuhkan kemungkinan medical cause.

### 2. Pemeriksaan fungsi

Vitals termasuk orthostatic bila perlu → gait/device → 5xSTS/TUG → balance → strength → endurance → cognition screen sesuai indikasi → ADL/environment. Pilih tes secukupnya agar pasien tidak kelelahan sebelum latihan.

### 3. Differential contributor

acute delirium/illness, orthostatic hypotension, medication effect, vestibular/sensory deficit, neurologic event, fracture/pain, deconditioning/frailty.

### 4. Intervention

Gunakan multicomponent approach: strength terutama lower limb/power, balance/reactive stepping, gait, aerobic, task practice dan home safety. Untuk frail patient, mulai volume rendah tetapi jangan berhenti pada exercise sangat ringan selamanya jika kapasitas membaik.

## Evaluasi

Outcome harus menangkap perubahan independence, bukan angka test saja. Catat falls/near falls, device level, walking outside home dan caregiver assistance.

## SOAP

S: fall, dizziness, fatigue, confidence, ADL.

O: TUG/5xSTS/gait/vitals + task.

A: faktor risiko yang paling modifiable saat ini.

P: progression dan strategi safety berikutnya.

## Deep Dive: mekanisme sampai evaluasi

### Patofisiologi yang perlu benar-benar dipahami

Aging skin is thinner and less resilient; vascular/edema problems and immobility increase risk wound/pressure injury. Arterial and venous disease have different loading/compression implications.

### Pemeriksaan terarah

Inspect skin, color, temperature, wound and pressure areas; edema distribution, pulses/capillary refill where within scope, pain pattern, sensation, mobility and pressure relief ability. Compression requires vascular appropriateness.

### Intervensi berdasarkan target impairment

Pressure redistribution, repositioning, mobility, calf pump exercise, elevation when appropriate, wound-care collaboration and compression only with proper assessment/prescription. Strength/gait improve circulation and independence.

### Evaluasi dan kriteria progression

Skin/wound status, edema, mobility and pressure-relief independence.

### Differential / kondisi yang jangan terlewat

DVT, cellulitis, critical limb ischemia, heart failure edema and neuropathic wound.
