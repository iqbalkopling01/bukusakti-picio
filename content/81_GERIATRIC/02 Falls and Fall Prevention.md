## Basis materi

Materi jatuh, vestibular dan fraktur, Berg/5xSTS/6MWT.

## Definisi

Fall adalah event tidak disengaja menuju lantai/level lebih rendah. Risiko biasanya multifactorial.

## Patofisiologi dan hubungan sebab-akibat

Weakness, reduced reaction, sensory/vestibular loss, medication, orthostatic hypotension, cognition, environment dan fear interact. Setelah fall, fear menurunkan activity dan mempercepat deconditioning.

## Manifestasi dan impairment

Falls/near falls, slow gait, difficulty turning, reduced balance, fear.

## Pemeriksaan fisioterapi

Fall history, orthostatic vitals, vision/hearing, feet, strength, 5xSTS, TUG, Berg, gait, medication review through team, home hazards.

## ICF / problem list

Pisahkan impairment, activity limitation, participation restriction, faktor personal, dan faktor lingkungan. Pilih problem prioritas berdasarkan safety, tujuan pasien, dan modifiability.

## Intervensi

Strength/power, static-dynamic-reactive balance, gait, dual-task selected, device, home modification, education getting up from floor.

## Progression dan evaluasi

Falls frequency, near falls, TUG, Berg, 5xSTS, confidence, community mobility.

## Red flags / escalation

Syncope, acute neuro event, fracture, cardiac symptoms require medical workup.

## Practical workflow geriatri

### 1. Jangan menganggap decline sebagai “karena umur”

Pada Falls and Fall Prevention, tanyakan baseline sebelum masalah, onset decline, falls, medication, cognition, vision/hearing, nutrition, continence, social support dan environment. Acute decline selalu membutuhkan kemungkinan medical cause.

### 2. Pemeriksaan fungsi

Vitals termasuk orthostatic bila perlu → gait/device → 5xSTS/TUG → balance → strength → endurance → cognition screen sesuai indikasi → ADL/environment. Pilih tes secukupnya agar pasien tidak kelelahan sebelum latihan.

### 3. Differential contributor

acute delirium/illness, orthostatic hypotension, medication effect, vestibular/sensory deficit, neurologic event, fracture/pain, deconditioning/frailty.

### 4. Intervention

Gunakan multicomponent approach: strength terutama lower limb/power, balance/reactive stepping, gait, aerobic, task practice dan home safety. Untuk frail patient, mulai volume rendah tetapi jangan berhenti pada exercise sangat ringan selamanya jika kapasitas membaik.

## Evaluasi

Outcome harus menangkap perubahan independence, bukan angka test saja. Catat falls/near falls, device level, walking outside home dan caregiver assistance.

## SOAP

S: fall, dizziness, fatigue, confidence, ADL.

O: TUG/5xSTS/gait/vitals + task.

A: faktor risiko yang paling modifiable saat ini.

P: progression dan strategi safety berikutnya.

## Deep Dive: mekanisme sampai evaluasi

### Patofisiologi yang perlu benar-benar dipahami

Falls arise from interaction of intrinsic deficits and environment: strength/power, balance, sensory/vestibular, cognition, orthostatic, medication, footwear and hazards. One test cannot identify all risk.

### Pemeriksaan terarah

Fall history including circumstances, injuries and fear; orthostatic BP, gait/device, TUG/5xSTS/Berg, vision/vestibular clues, cognition, feet/footwear and home hazard. Review medications with appropriate provider.

### Intervensi berdasarkan target impairment

Progressive strength/power, static-dynamic-reactive balance, gait/turn/stair, dual task when suitable, device optimization and home/environment modification. Educate floor-recovery strategy where safe.

### Evaluasi dan kriteria progression

Falls/near falls, confidence, TUG/5xSTS, gait speed, balance and community mobility.

### Differential / kondisi yang jangan terlewat

Syncope/arrhythmia, seizure, stroke/TIA, BPPV, neuropathy and acute medication/illness.
