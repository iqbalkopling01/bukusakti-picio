Materi kesehatan komunitas di Drive membedakan promotif, preventif, kuratif dan rehabilitatif. Fisioterapi dapat bekerja pada seluruh continuum terutama physical activity, fall prevention, ergonomic/activity education, chronic-disease exercise dan disability participation.

## Level tindakan

Individual: exercise prescription dan self-management. Family: caregiver training. Group: class balance, pulmonary/cardiac rehab, workplace exercise. Community: physical-activity program, accessibility, screening dan education.

## Prevention

Primary prevention sebelum penyakit/cedera, secondary early detection/risk modification, tertiary mengurangi disability/complication pada established condition.

## Evaluasi program

Reach/attendance, adherence, physical activity, functional outcome, injury/fall event dan participation. Program yang bagus harus feasible dan culturally/contextually appropriate.

## Contoh program

Fall-prevention class dapat menggabungkan strength, balance, home-safety education dan referral vision/medication. Workplace program dapat menggabungkan physical activity break, load management dan ergonomic task redesign. Community pulmonary program dapat memakai walking, resistance, breathing education dan self-management.

## Screening bukan diagnosis massal

Screening komunitas harus punya pathway tindak lanjut. Mengukur balance atau BP tanpa sistem referral bila abnormal membuat program tidak lengkap.
