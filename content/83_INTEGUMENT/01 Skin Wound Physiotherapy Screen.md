## Pemeriksaan

Inspeksi skin color, integrity, moisture, temperature, edema, wound location, dressing/drain, pressure areas dan surrounding tissue. Tanyakan pain, sensation, systemic symptom, wound history dan medical restrictions. Pada pasien immobile, periksa kemampuan pressure relief dan transfer.

## Red flags

Rapidly spreading erythema/swelling, disproportionate severe pain, fever/systemic toxicity, crepitus, foul drainage, wound dehiscence, ischemic color/cold limb atau acute DVT concern memerlukan medical escalation.

## Rehab role

Positioning, pressure redistribution, ROM/contracture prevention, edema management bila appropriate, progressive mobility, strength/endurance dan caregiver education. Wound dressing/debridement hanya sesuai scope dan setting credential.

## Evaluation

Wound status dari team, pain, edema, ROM, transfer/gait, skin tolerance terhadap device dan independence pressure relief.

## Wound description yang perlu dicatat

Location, approximate size/depth bila scope memungkinkan, tissue appearance yang dilaporkan team, exudate amount/type, odor, surrounding erythema/maceration, dressing, edema dan pain. Jangan melepas dressing hanya untuk assessment fisioterapi bila bukan bagian order/kompetensi.

## Pressure injury risk

Nilai mobility, sensation, nutrition risk, moisture/incontinence, device pressure dan ability to reposition. Program pressure relief harus realistis terhadap cognition, UE strength dan caregiver availability.

## Device and gait

Orthosis/prosthesis/wheelchair dapat membuat friction/pressure. Periksa skin sebelum dan setelah trial serta ajarkan self-check pada area dengan sensory loss.
