## Basis materi

Materi piriformis syndrome dan lumbar radiculopathy.

## Definisi

Istilah piriformis syndrome sering digunakan untuk sciatic-like symptoms terkait deep gluteal region, tetapi diagnosis harus membedakan lumbar root dan hip causes.

## Patofisiologi dan hubungan sebab-akibat

Sciatic nerve dapat mengalami irritation/compression di deep gluteal space pada sebagian pasien. Local muscle pain juga dapat refer tanpa true nerve lesion.

## Manifestasi dan impairment yang diharapkan

Buttock pain, posterior leg symptoms, provocation sitting/hip position, possible neural symptoms.

## Pemeriksaan fisioterapi

Lumbar neuro screen wajib: reflex, myotome, dermatome, SLR/slump. Hip ROM, palpation deep gluteal, resisted hip tests. Diagnosis exclusion penting.

## Problem list / ICF

Susun body structure-function impairment, activity limitation, participation restriction, serta faktor personal/lingkungan. Prioritaskan problem yang paling membatasi tujuan pasien dan paling modifiable oleh fisioterapi.

## Intervensi fisioterapi

Address identified contributor: load modification, hip strength/control, mobility, neural mobility bila signs, lumbar treatment bila source spinal. Hindari aggressive deep pressure bila neural irritability.

## Progression dan evaluasi

Sitting/walking tolerance, neural test, hip strength, symptom distribution.

## Red flags / escalation

Progressive neurologic deficit, bowel/bladder, vascular, trauma.

## Practical workflow neurologis

### 1. Tentukan localization sebelum latihan

Pada Piriformis Syndrome / Deep Gluteal Differential, tanyakan onset/progression dan fungsi yang hilang. Periksa mental status/communication bila relevan, cranial nerve bila central, tone, reflex, sensation, myotome/strength, coordination, balance, transfer dan gait. Tujuan awal adalah menjawab: lesi central atau peripheral, level/distribusi apa, dan impairment mana yang paling membatasi activity.

### 2. Safety scan

Cari perubahan neurologis baru, respiratory/autonomic issue, swallowing/aspiration concern, orthostatic intolerance, skin/pressure risk dan fall risk. Kondisi neurologis kronis tetap dapat mengalami acute medical event yang tidak boleh dianggap “bagian dari diagnosis lama”.

### 3. Movement analysis

Amati kualitas task, bukan hanya grade otot. Saat sit-to-stand/gait, catat alignment, weight shift, selective control, timing, tone interaction, sensory dependence, compensation dan assistance.

### 4. Differential contributor

regional mechanical disorder, neurological disorder, vascular disorder, infection/inflammation, fracture/trauma, systemic disease.

### 5. Intervention hierarchy

Pertama jaga ROM, skin, respiratory dan positioning bila ada risiko. Kedua pulihkan task dasar seperti bed mobility, sitting, transfer dan standing. Ketiga tingkatkan strength/endurance/balance. Keempat tambah speed, dual task dan community demand. Handling seperti Bobath atau PNF dipakai untuk membantu pasien aktif menyelesaikan task, bukan menggantikan repetition aktif.

## Goal dan evaluasi

Gunakan level bantuan sebagai outcome penting. Contoh: “sit-to-stand dari min assist menjadi supervision dalam 2 minggu” lebih klinis daripada “tone membaik”. Kombinasikan dengan 10MWT, TUG, Berg, 6MWT, GMFM atau scale lain sesuai populasi.

## SOAP neurologis

S: fatigue, falls, pain, dizziness, medication timing, confidence, caregiver report.

O: neurological sign yang relevan dan functional task yang sama.

A: apakah perubahan disebabkan motor recovery, compensation, cue, device, atau fatigue?

P: tulis target task sesi berikutnya dan level bantuan/cue yang akan dikurangi.

## Deep Dive: mekanisme sampai evaluasi

### Patofisiologi yang perlu benar-benar dipahami

Deep gluteal syndrome describes sciatic nerve irritation in deep gluteal space; "piriformis syndrome" is one possible contributor. Diagnosis is clinical and should not be made from buttock tenderness alone.

### Pemeriksaan terarah

History buttock/posterior-thigh symptoms, sitting and hip position. Perform lumbar/root screen first, then hip ROM/provocation, neural tension, palpation and selected deep-gluteal provocation. Check reflex/myotome/sensory deficits.

### Intervensi berdasarkan target impairment

Load modification, hip mobility/strength, neural mobility when appropriate, graded sitting/walking and movement strategy. Manual therapy can modulate symptoms. Persistent neurologic deficit requires further evaluation.

### Evaluasi dan kriteria progression

Sitting tolerance, neural symptoms, hip strength and functional task.

### Differential / kondisi yang jangan terlewat

Lumbar radiculopathy, hamstring syndrome, ischiofemoral impingement, hip joint pathology, SI-region pain and vascular causes.
