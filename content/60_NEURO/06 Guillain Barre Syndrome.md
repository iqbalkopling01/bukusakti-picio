## Basis materi

Materi GBS, neurophysiology.

## Definisi

GBS adalah acute immune-mediated peripheral polyneuropathy sering dengan ascending weakness dan areflexia.

## Patofisiologi dan hubungan sebab-akibat

Immune attack pada peripheral nerve myelin/axon menurunkan conduction. Respiratory dan autonomic nerves dapat terdampak. Recovery bergantung remyelination/axonal regeneration.

## Manifestasi dan impairment yang diharapkan

Symmetric ascending weakness, reduced/absent reflex, sensory symptoms, pain, fatigue, possible respiratory/autonomic instability.

## Pemeriksaan fisioterapi

Acute respiratory/vital monitoring, strength tanpa overfatiguing, reflex, sensation, ROM, functional ability. In recovery monitor fatigue dan overwork.

## Problem list / ICF

Susun body structure-function impairment, activity limitation, participation restriction, serta faktor personal/lingkungan. Prioritaskan problem yang paling membatasi tujuan pasien dan paling modifiable oleh fisioterapi.

## Intervensi fisioterapi

Acute ICU: positioning, ROM, respiratory support collaboration, prevent contracture/pressure. Recovery: gradual low-to-moderate strengthening, transfer/gait, endurance with substantial rest. Avoid aggressive exercise into fatigue.

## Progression dan evaluasi

Track MMT/dynamometry, transfer, gait, endurance, fatigue recovery. Progress slowly according to neurological recovery.

## Red flags / escalation

Rapid progression, respiratory difficulty, bulbar symptom, autonomic instability require urgent medical management.

## Practical workflow neurologis

### 1. Tentukan localization sebelum latihan

Pada Guillain-Barré Syndrome, tanyakan onset/progression dan fungsi yang hilang. Periksa mental status/communication bila relevan, cranial nerve bila central, tone, reflex, sensation, myotome/strength, coordination, balance, transfer dan gait. Tujuan awal adalah menjawab: lesi central atau peripheral, level/distribusi apa, dan impairment mana yang paling membatasi activity.

### 2. Safety scan

Cari perubahan neurologis baru, respiratory/autonomic issue, swallowing/aspiration concern, orthostatic intolerance, skin/pressure risk dan fall risk. Kondisi neurologis kronis tetap dapat mengalami acute medical event yang tidak boleh dianggap “bagian dari diagnosis lama”.

### 3. Movement analysis

Amati kualitas task, bukan hanya grade otot. Saat sit-to-stand/gait, catat alignment, weight shift, selective control, timing, tone interaction, sensory dependence, compensation dan assistance.

### 4. Differential contributor

acute medical deterioration, medication effect, infection/metabolic issue, peripheral musculoskeletal limitation, vestibular/visual contributor, cardiorespiratory deconditioning.

### 5. Intervention hierarchy

Pertama jaga ROM, skin, respiratory dan positioning bila ada risiko. Kedua pulihkan task dasar seperti bed mobility, sitting, transfer dan standing. Ketiga tingkatkan strength/endurance/balance. Keempat tambah speed, dual task dan community demand. Handling seperti Bobath atau PNF dipakai untuk membantu pasien aktif menyelesaikan task, bukan menggantikan repetition aktif.

## Goal dan evaluasi

Gunakan level bantuan sebagai outcome penting. Contoh: “sit-to-stand dari min assist menjadi supervision dalam 2 minggu” lebih klinis daripada “tone membaik”. Kombinasikan dengan 10MWT, TUG, Berg, 6MWT, GMFM atau scale lain sesuai populasi.

## SOAP neurologis

S: fatigue, falls, pain, dizziness, medication timing, confidence, caregiver report.

O: neurological sign yang relevan dan functional task yang sama.

A: apakah perubahan disebabkan motor recovery, compensation, cue, device, atau fatigue?

P: tulis target task sesi berikutnya dan level bantuan/cue yang akan dikurangi.

## Deep Dive: mekanisme sampai evaluasi

### Patofisiologi yang perlu benar-benar dipahami

Acute immune-mediated peripheral polyradiculoneuropathy causes demyelination and/or axonal injury, typically ascending weakness with areflexia and possible sensory/autonomic/respiratory involvement.

### Pemeriksaan terarah

Respiratory and autonomic safety are first. Review vital capacity/medical respiratory data, HR/BP instability, strength distribution, reflex loss, sensation, pain and mobility. Avoid maximal repeated testing that produces overwork fatigue.

### Intervensi berdasarkan target impairment

Acute: positioning, ROM, respiratory assistance per team, pressure prevention and gentle mobility. Recovery: graded activation, low-to-moderate load strength with rest, transfer/gait and endurance progression. Avoid high-fatigue overwork early.

### Evaluasi dan kriteria progression

Strength, assistance level, respiratory status, walking distance and fatigue/recovery. Worsening respiratory or autonomic signs are urgent.

### Differential / kondisi yang jangan terlewat

Spinal cord disease, myasthenic crisis, acute myopathy, transverse myelitis and critical illness neuropathy.
