## Basis materi

Materi Beginners Guide to Postural Management, Bobath, transfer.

## Definisi

Postural management adalah 24-hour approach untuk mendukung alignment, comfort, pressure management, function, respiration, dan prevention secondary deformity pada pasien dengan severe motor disability.

## Patofisiologi dan hubungan sebab-akibat

Weakness, abnormal tone, immobility, asymmetry, gravity dan prolonged position dapat memicu contracture, scoliosis, pressure injury, pain, dan respiratory restriction.

## Manifestasi dan impairment yang diharapkan

Difficulty maintain sitting/lying, asymmetry, pressure risk, contracture, pain, reduced function.

## Pemeriksaan fisioterapi

Assess lying/sitting alignment, skin, ROM, tone, respiratory, comfort, caregiver capacity, wheelchair/seating.

## Problem list / ICF

Susun body structure-function impairment, activity limitation, participation restriction, serta faktor personal/lingkungan. Prioritaskan problem yang paling membatasi tujuan pasien dan paling modifiable oleh fisioterapi.

## Intervensi fisioterapi

Positioning schedule, supported lying/sitting, seating adaptation, pressure relief, ROM, active movement, standing program bila indicated, caregiver education. Equipment melalui multidisciplinary assessment.

## Progression dan evaluasi

Skin integrity, comfort, ROM, sitting tolerance, function, caregiver ease, respiratory effect.

## Red flags / escalation

Skin breakdown, acute pain, respiratory compromise, equipment causing pressure.

## Practical workflow neurologis

### 1. Tentukan localization sebelum latihan

Pada Postural Management in Neurodisability, tanyakan onset/progression dan fungsi yang hilang. Periksa mental status/communication bila relevan, cranial nerve bila central, tone, reflex, sensation, myotome/strength, coordination, balance, transfer dan gait. Tujuan awal adalah menjawab: lesi central atau peripheral, level/distribusi apa, dan impairment mana yang paling membatasi activity.

### 2. Safety scan

Cari perubahan neurologis baru, respiratory/autonomic issue, swallowing/aspiration concern, orthostatic intolerance, skin/pressure risk dan fall risk. Kondisi neurologis kronis tetap dapat mengalami acute medical event yang tidak boleh dianggap “bagian dari diagnosis lama”.

### 3. Movement analysis

Amati kualitas task, bukan hanya grade otot. Saat sit-to-stand/gait, catat alignment, weight shift, selective control, timing, tone interaction, sensory dependence, compensation dan assistance.

### 4. Differential contributor

regional mechanical disorder, neurological disorder, vascular disorder, infection/inflammation, fracture/trauma, systemic disease.

### 5. Intervention hierarchy

Pertama jaga ROM, skin, respiratory dan positioning bila ada risiko. Kedua pulihkan task dasar seperti bed mobility, sitting, transfer dan standing. Ketiga tingkatkan strength/endurance/balance. Keempat tambah speed, dual task dan community demand. Handling seperti Bobath atau PNF dipakai untuk membantu pasien aktif menyelesaikan task, bukan menggantikan repetition aktif.

## Goal dan evaluasi

Gunakan level bantuan sebagai outcome penting. Contoh: “sit-to-stand dari min assist menjadi supervision dalam 2 minggu” lebih klinis daripada “tone membaik”. Kombinasikan dengan 10MWT, TUG, Berg, 6MWT, GMFM atau scale lain sesuai populasi.

## SOAP neurologis

S: fatigue, falls, pain, dizziness, medication timing, confidence, caregiver report.

O: neurological sign yang relevan dan functional task yang sama.

A: apakah perubahan disebabkan motor recovery, compensation, cue, device, atau fatigue?

P: tulis target task sesi berikutnya dan level bantuan/cue yang akan dikurangi.

## Deep Dive: mekanisme sampai evaluasi

### Patofisiologi yang perlu benar-benar dipahami

Long-term motor impairment can create asymmetrical loading, contracture, pain, pressure injury and respiratory compromise. Postural management aims to distribute pressure and support function across 24 hours, not create a rigid "ideal posture".

### Pemeriksaan terarah

Assess body shape, ROM/contracture, tone, skin, comfort, breathing, seating/lying, head/upper-limb function, transfers and caregiver handling. Observe posture over time and under fatigue.

### Intervensi berdasarkan target impairment

Individualized seating/lying supports, pressure redistribution, regular position change, ROM, strengthening where possible, standing program if appropriate and caregiver training. Equipment must be reviewed as body size and tone change.

### Evaluasi dan kriteria progression

Skin, pain, tolerance duration, symmetry, function such as feeding/upper-limb use and caregiver burden.

### Differential / kondisi yang jangan terlewat

Hip dislocation/scoliosis progression, pain source, equipment fit and acute medical issue.
