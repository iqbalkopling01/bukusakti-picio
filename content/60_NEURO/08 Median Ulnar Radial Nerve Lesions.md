## Basis materi

Materi Lesi N Medianus, Radialis, Ulnaris, upper extremity neurologic test.

## Definisi

Peripheral nerve lesion menghasilkan motor/sensory deficit sesuai nerve dan level lesion.

## Patofisiologi dan hubungan sebab-akibat

Compression, traction, laceration, ischemia atau systemic neuropathy mengganggu conduction. Neuropraxia mainly conduction block, axonotmesis axonal injury dengan connective framework preserved varying degree, neurotmesis severe discontinuity.

## Manifestasi dan impairment yang diharapkan

Median: thenar/precision grip dan sensory radial palm/fingers. Ulnar: intrinsic hand weakness, claw pattern, ulnar sensory. Radial: wrist/finger extension weakness/wrist drop, dorsal radial sensory. Pattern tergantung lesion level.

## Pemeriksaan fisioterapi

Map sensory, motor muscles, reflex where relevant, Tinel, nerve tension, grip/pinch, functional hand. Differentiate root lesion by myotome/dermatome/reflex and multi-nerve distribution.

## Problem list / ICF

Susun body structure-function impairment, activity limitation, participation restriction, serta faktor personal/lingkungan. Prioritaskan problem yang paling membatasi tujuan pasien dan paling modifiable oleh fisioterapi.

## Intervensi fisioterapi

Protect insensate skin, maintain ROM, tendon/nerve gliding selected, strengthening reinnervating muscles, splint/orthosis, task training. After repair follow surgical nerve protocol.

## Progression dan evaluasi

Sensory return, MMT/dynamometry, grip/pinch, dexterity, Tinel progression cautiously, function.

## Red flags / escalation

Rapid progressive weakness, traumatic laceration, severe nerve pain, vascular compromise.

## Practical workflow neurologis

### 1. Tentukan localization sebelum latihan

Pada Median, Ulnar, and Radial Nerve Lesions, tanyakan onset/progression dan fungsi yang hilang. Periksa mental status/communication bila relevan, cranial nerve bila central, tone, reflex, sensation, myotome/strength, coordination, balance, transfer dan gait. Tujuan awal adalah menjawab: lesi central atau peripheral, level/distribusi apa, dan impairment mana yang paling membatasi activity.

### 2. Safety scan

Cari perubahan neurologis baru, respiratory/autonomic issue, swallowing/aspiration concern, orthostatic intolerance, skin/pressure risk dan fall risk. Kondisi neurologis kronis tetap dapat mengalami acute medical event yang tidak boleh dianggap “bagian dari diagnosis lama”.

### 3. Movement analysis

Amati kualitas task, bukan hanya grade otot. Saat sit-to-stand/gait, catat alignment, weight shift, selective control, timing, tone interaction, sensory dependence, compensation dan assistance.

### 4. Differential contributor

regional mechanical disorder, neurological disorder, vascular disorder, infection/inflammation, fracture/trauma, systemic disease.

### 5. Intervention hierarchy

Pertama jaga ROM, skin, respiratory dan positioning bila ada risiko. Kedua pulihkan task dasar seperti bed mobility, sitting, transfer dan standing. Ketiga tingkatkan strength/endurance/balance. Keempat tambah speed, dual task dan community demand. Handling seperti Bobath atau PNF dipakai untuk membantu pasien aktif menyelesaikan task, bukan menggantikan repetition aktif.

## Goal dan evaluasi

Gunakan level bantuan sebagai outcome penting. Contoh: “sit-to-stand dari min assist menjadi supervision dalam 2 minggu” lebih klinis daripada “tone membaik”. Kombinasikan dengan 10MWT, TUG, Berg, 6MWT, GMFM atau scale lain sesuai populasi.

## SOAP neurologis

S: fatigue, falls, pain, dizziness, medication timing, confidence, caregiver report.

O: neurological sign yang relevan dan functional task yang sama.

A: apakah perubahan disebabkan motor recovery, compensation, cue, device, atau fatigue?

P: tulis target task sesi berikutnya dan level bantuan/cue yang akan dikurangi.

## Deep Dive: mekanisme sampai evaluasi

### Patofisiologi yang perlu benar-benar dipahami

Peripheral nerve lesion can produce neurapraxic conduction block, axonal injury or more severe disruption. Deficit follows nerve-specific motor and sensory distribution rather than dermatomal root alone.

### Pemeriksaan terarah

Map sensory territory, nerve-specific muscle strength, reflex if relevant, Tinel/provocation, neurodynamic test and functional hand task. Compare with cervical myotomes/reflex to distinguish root. Check muscle atrophy and autonomic/trophic change.

### Intervensi berdasarkan target impairment

Remove/compressing load, ergonomic modification, maintain ROM, nerve-gliding when mechanosensitivity rather than severe irritability, progressive strengthening as reinnervation/capacity allows, splinting and task practice. Severe motor loss/traumatic injury needs medical/nerve evaluation.

### Evaluasi dan kriteria progression

Sensory map, MMT/dynamometry, grip/pinch, dexterity/task and symptom response. Recovery can be slow with axonal injury.

### Differential / kondisi yang jangan terlewat

Cervical radiculopathy, brachial plexus lesion, tendon injury and central neurologic disease.
