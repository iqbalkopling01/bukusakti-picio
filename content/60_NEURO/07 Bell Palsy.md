## Basis materi

Materi Bell palsy dan cranial nerve.

## Definisi

Acute peripheral facial nerve palsy, biasanya idiopathic, menyebabkan unilateral LMN facial weakness.

## Patofisiologi dan hubungan sebab-akibat

Inflammation/edema facial nerve dalam facial canal diduga mengganggu conduction. Karena lesion peripheral CN VII, upper dan lower facial muscles sisi yang sama dapat lemah.

## Manifestasi dan impairment yang diharapkan

Facial asymmetry, inability close eye, reduced forehead movement, drooling, altered taste/ear symptoms pada sebagian.

## Pemeriksaan fisioterapi

CN VII motor exam, eye closure, facial movement, synkinesis, function eating/speech. Screen central stroke signs: forehead sparing with other neuro deficit changes differential.

## Problem list / ICF

Susun body structure-function impairment, activity limitation, participation restriction, serta faktor personal/lingkungan. Prioritaskan problem yang paling membatasi tujuan pasien dan paling modifiable oleh fisioterapi.

## Intervensi fisioterapi

Eye protection adalah prioritas medis/edukasi bila closure incomplete. Facial movement training gentle, mirror feedback, avoid excessive force yang dapat memperburuk maladaptive patterns. Electrical stimulation controversial dan harus mengikuti protocol/dosen.

## Progression dan evaluasi

Photographic/standard facial grading, eye closure, symmetry, synkinesis, patient function.

## Red flags / escalation

Other acute neuro deficits, severe headache, bilateral palsy, recurrent palsy, vesicular ear rash/hearing symptoms require medical assessment.

## Practical workflow neurologis

### 1. Tentukan localization sebelum latihan

Pada Bell Palsy, tanyakan onset/progression dan fungsi yang hilang. Periksa mental status/communication bila relevan, cranial nerve bila central, tone, reflex, sensation, myotome/strength, coordination, balance, transfer dan gait. Tujuan awal adalah menjawab: lesi central atau peripheral, level/distribusi apa, dan impairment mana yang paling membatasi activity.

### 2. Safety scan

Cari perubahan neurologis baru, respiratory/autonomic issue, swallowing/aspiration concern, orthostatic intolerance, skin/pressure risk dan fall risk. Kondisi neurologis kronis tetap dapat mengalami acute medical event yang tidak boleh dianggap “bagian dari diagnosis lama”.

### 3. Movement analysis

Amati kualitas task, bukan hanya grade otot. Saat sit-to-stand/gait, catat alignment, weight shift, selective control, timing, tone interaction, sensory dependence, compensation dan assistance.

### 4. Differential contributor

acute medical deterioration, medication effect, infection/metabolic issue, peripheral musculoskeletal limitation, vestibular/visual contributor, cardiorespiratory deconditioning.

### 5. Intervention hierarchy

Pertama jaga ROM, skin, respiratory dan positioning bila ada risiko. Kedua pulihkan task dasar seperti bed mobility, sitting, transfer dan standing. Ketiga tingkatkan strength/endurance/balance. Keempat tambah speed, dual task dan community demand. Handling seperti Bobath atau PNF dipakai untuk membantu pasien aktif menyelesaikan task, bukan menggantikan repetition aktif.

## Goal dan evaluasi

Gunakan level bantuan sebagai outcome penting. Contoh: “sit-to-stand dari min assist menjadi supervision dalam 2 minggu” lebih klinis daripada “tone membaik”. Kombinasikan dengan 10MWT, TUG, Berg, 6MWT, GMFM atau scale lain sesuai populasi.

## SOAP neurologis

S: fatigue, falls, pain, dizziness, medication timing, confidence, caregiver report.

O: neurological sign yang relevan dan functional task yang sama.

A: apakah perubahan disebabkan motor recovery, compensation, cue, device, atau fatigue?

P: tulis target task sesi berikutnya dan level bantuan/cue yang akan dikurangi.

## Deep Dive: mekanisme sampai evaluasi

### Patofisiologi yang perlu benar-benar dipahami

Acute peripheral facial nerve dysfunction causes unilateral lower motor neuron facial weakness. Inflammation/edema within facial canal is a common proposed mechanism; medical diagnosis excludes central causes.

### Pemeriksaan terarah

Observe forehead, eye closure, smile, lip seal and symmetry at rest/movement. Screen other cranial/neuro signs because forehead sparing or limb deficit suggests central lesion. Ask ear pain, taste, hearing, eye dryness and onset.

### Intervensi berdasarkan target impairment

Eye protection is critical when closure incomplete and requires medical guidance. Gentle facial movement/neuromuscular retraining and symmetry practice may be used; avoid aggressive repetitive maximal facial exercise that increases unwanted synkinesis. Coordinate medical treatment.

### Evaluasi dan kriteria progression

Photo/video standardized facial movements, functional eye/mouth closure and facial grading scale if used. Monitor synkinesis.

### Differential / kondisi yang jangan terlewat

Stroke, Ramsay Hunt, tumor, trauma and other cranial neuropathy.
