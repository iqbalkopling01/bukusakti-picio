## Basis materi

Materi SCI 1, postural management, transfer/ambulasi, konferensi kasus SCI AIS D NLI T9 akibat compression fracture/spondylitis TB.

## Definisi

SCI adalah kerusakan spinal cord yang menghasilkan motor, sensory, dan autonomic dysfunction di bawah level lesion. Classification menggunakan neurological level dan AIS menurut ISNCSCI.

## Patofisiologi dan hubungan sebab-akibat

Primary injury merusak cord secara langsung. Secondary cascade meliputi ischemia, edema, inflammation dan cellular damage. Complete/incomplete status menentukan preserved sacral function. Lesion level menentukan muscle groups dan autonomic risk. Pada kasus spondylitis TB, vertebral destruction/compression dapat menyebabkan cord injury.

## Manifestasi dan impairment yang diharapkan

Weakness/paralysis, sensory loss, altered reflex/tone, bladder-bowel/sexual dysfunction, orthostatic issue, pressure injury risk, respiratory impairment pada high lesion, balance/gait deficit pada incomplete SCI.

## Pemeriksaan fisioterapi

ISNCSCI sensory/motor examination oleh trained examiner, neurological level/AIS. ROM, tone, skin, respiratory, orthostatic tolerance, sitting balance, transfer, wheelchair, gait potential, strength below level, pain. Monitor autonomic dysreflexia risk terutama higher lesions.

## Problem list / ICF

Susun body structure-function impairment, activity limitation, participation restriction, serta faktor personal/lingkungan. Prioritaskan problem yang paling membatasi tujuan pasien dan paling modifiable oleh fisioterapi.

## Intervensi fisioterapi

Positioning/pressure relief, ROM prevent contracture, respiratory care bila needed, strengthening preserved muscles, bed mobility, transfer, wheelchair skills. In incomplete AIS D, emphasize standing/gait, lower-limb strength, balance, endurance, task repetition. Manage orthostatic tolerance. Education skin/bladder-bowel with team.

## Progression dan evaluasi

Reassess neurological status if change, strength, transfer assistance, sitting/standing balance, walking device/distance, 10MWT/6MWT if ambulatory, independence.

## Red flags / escalation

New ascending weakness, acute autonomic dysreflexia symptoms, respiratory decline, pressure wound infection, DVT, spinal instability/infection, sudden neurologic deterioration.

## Practical workflow neurologis

### 1. Tentukan localization sebelum latihan

Pada Spinal Cord Injury, tanyakan onset/progression dan fungsi yang hilang. Periksa mental status/communication bila relevan, cranial nerve bila central, tone, reflex, sensation, myotome/strength, coordination, balance, transfer dan gait. Tujuan awal adalah menjawab: lesi central atau peripheral, level/distribusi apa, dan impairment mana yang paling membatasi activity.

### 2. Safety scan

Cari perubahan neurologis baru, respiratory/autonomic issue, swallowing/aspiration concern, orthostatic intolerance, skin/pressure risk dan fall risk. Kondisi neurologis kronis tetap dapat mengalami acute medical event yang tidak boleh dianggap “bagian dari diagnosis lama”.

### 3. Movement analysis

Amati kualitas task, bukan hanya grade otot. Saat sit-to-stand/gait, catat alignment, weight shift, selective control, timing, tone interaction, sensory dependence, compensation dan assistance.

### 4. Differential contributor

acute medical deterioration, medication effect, infection/metabolic issue, peripheral musculoskeletal limitation, vestibular/visual contributor, cardiorespiratory deconditioning.

### 5. Intervention hierarchy

Pertama jaga ROM, skin, respiratory dan positioning bila ada risiko. Kedua pulihkan task dasar seperti bed mobility, sitting, transfer dan standing. Ketiga tingkatkan strength/endurance/balance. Keempat tambah speed, dual task dan community demand. Handling seperti Bobath atau PNF dipakai untuk membantu pasien aktif menyelesaikan task, bukan menggantikan repetition aktif.

## Goal dan evaluasi

Gunakan level bantuan sebagai outcome penting. Contoh: “sit-to-stand dari min assist menjadi supervision dalam 2 minggu” lebih klinis daripada “tone membaik”. Kombinasikan dengan 10MWT, TUG, Berg, 6MWT, GMFM atau scale lain sesuai populasi.

## SOAP neurologis

S: fatigue, falls, pain, dizziness, medication timing, confidence, caregiver report.

O: neurological sign yang relevan dan functional task yang sama.

A: apakah perubahan disebabkan motor recovery, compensation, cue, device, atau fatigue?

P: tulis target task sesi berikutnya dan level bantuan/cue yang akan dikurangi.

## Deep Dive: mekanisme sampai evaluasi

### Patofisiologi yang perlu benar-benar dipahami

Primary mechanical/compressive injury disrupts spinal cord tissue; secondary ischemia, edema and inflammation expand injury. Lesion level determines motor/sensory/autonomic consequences. Sacral sparing determines completeness in ISNCSCI. In spondylitis TB, vertebral infection and collapse can compress the cord.

### Pemeriksaan terarah

Use ISNCSCI/ASIA when trained, then ROM, MMT/preserved muscles, tone/MAS, skin, respiratory, orthostatic tolerance, FIST/sitting, transfer, wheelchair and gait potential. Use SCIM III for independence. Check bowel/bladder/autonomic history and pressure injury risk.

### Intervensi berdasarkan target impairment

Pressure relief and positioning, ROM, respiratory care when indicated, strengthening preserved muscles, progressive resistance UE for transfer, trunk/sitting balance, bed mobility, transfer, wheelchair, standing/gait for incomplete injury, aerobic conditioning and self-management education.

### Evaluasi dan kriteria progression

Serial ISNCSCI when appropriate, SCIM, FIST, MMT/MAS, transfer assistance, walking speed/endurance and device. The Drive case demonstrates SCIM 44→56→69 and FIST 30→46→54 as multi-level recovery markers.

### Differential / kondisi yang jangan terlewat

Spinal instability/compression, infection progression, autonomic dysreflexia, DVT, pressure wound, syrinx/new neurologic decline.
