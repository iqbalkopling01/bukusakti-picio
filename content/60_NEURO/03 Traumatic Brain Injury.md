## Basis materi

Materi TBI, neurosains, postural management.

## Definisi

TBI adalah brain dysfunction akibat external mechanical force dengan severity dan pattern yang bervariasi.

## Patofisiologi dan hubungan sebab-akibat

Primary injury dapat berupa contusion, hemorrhage, diffuse axonal injury. Secondary injury melalui edema, ischemia, inflammation, intracranial pressure changes. Frontal/temporal/diffuse injury memengaruhi cognition, behavior dan motor control.

## Manifestasi dan impairment yang diharapkan

Altered consciousness, memory, attention, behavior, weakness, tone, coordination, balance, gait, vestibular symptoms.

## Pemeriksaan fisioterapi

Medical stability, level consciousness, orientation, cognition, cranial nerve, motor/sensory, tone, coordination, balance, gait, vestibular screen, fatigue. Use functional outcome appropriate setting.

## Problem list / ICF

Susun body structure-function impairment, activity limitation, participation restriction, serta faktor personal/lingkungan. Prioritaskan problem yang paling membatasi tujuan pasien dan paling modifiable oleh fisioterapi.

## Intervensi fisioterapi

Low-stimulation/structured environment bila overstimulated, positioning, ROM, early mobility, balance/gait, task practice, aerobic graded, vestibular rehab, cognitive-motor integration with team.

## Progression dan evaluasi

Track assistance, balance, gait, endurance, cognition/task tolerance, symptom provocation. Progress complexity cautiously because fatigue can be prominent.

## Red flags / escalation

Worsening consciousness, severe headache/vomiting, new focal deficit, seizure, CSF leak signs require medical escalation.

## Practical workflow neurologis

### 1. Tentukan localization sebelum latihan

Pada Traumatic Brain Injury, tanyakan onset/progression dan fungsi yang hilang. Periksa mental status/communication bila relevan, cranial nerve bila central, tone, reflex, sensation, myotome/strength, coordination, balance, transfer dan gait. Tujuan awal adalah menjawab: lesi central atau peripheral, level/distribusi apa, dan impairment mana yang paling membatasi activity.

### 2. Safety scan

Cari perubahan neurologis baru, respiratory/autonomic issue, swallowing/aspiration concern, orthostatic intolerance, skin/pressure risk dan fall risk. Kondisi neurologis kronis tetap dapat mengalami acute medical event yang tidak boleh dianggap “bagian dari diagnosis lama”.

### 3. Movement analysis

Amati kualitas task, bukan hanya grade otot. Saat sit-to-stand/gait, catat alignment, weight shift, selective control, timing, tone interaction, sensory dependence, compensation dan assistance.

### 4. Differential contributor

acute medical deterioration, medication effect, infection/metabolic issue, peripheral musculoskeletal limitation, vestibular/visual contributor, cardiorespiratory deconditioning.

### 5. Intervention hierarchy

Pertama jaga ROM, skin, respiratory dan positioning bila ada risiko. Kedua pulihkan task dasar seperti bed mobility, sitting, transfer dan standing. Ketiga tingkatkan strength/endurance/balance. Keempat tambah speed, dual task dan community demand. Handling seperti Bobath atau PNF dipakai untuk membantu pasien aktif menyelesaikan task, bukan menggantikan repetition aktif.

## Goal dan evaluasi

Gunakan level bantuan sebagai outcome penting. Contoh: “sit-to-stand dari min assist menjadi supervision dalam 2 minggu” lebih klinis daripada “tone membaik”. Kombinasikan dengan 10MWT, TUG, Berg, 6MWT, GMFM atau scale lain sesuai populasi.

## SOAP neurologis

S: fatigue, falls, pain, dizziness, medication timing, confidence, caregiver report.

O: neurological sign yang relevan dan functional task yang sama.

A: apakah perubahan disebabkan motor recovery, compensation, cue, device, atau fatigue?

P: tulis target task sesi berikutnya dan level bantuan/cue yang akan dikurangi.

## Deep Dive: mekanisme sampai evaluasi

### Patofisiologi yang perlu benar-benar dipahami

TBI produces primary focal/diffuse mechanical injury followed by secondary metabolic, ischemic, inflammatory and intracranial-pressure effects. Motor, balance, cognition, behavior, vision/vestibular and autonomic symptoms can coexist.

### Pemeriksaan terarah

Review consciousness/cognitive status, medical restrictions, headache/dizziness, cranial/vision/vestibular, tone/strength, coordination, balance, gait and dual-task. Monitor exertional symptom and behavior. Use GCS/medical scales from team and functional measures appropriate to stage.

### Intervensi berdasarkan target impairment

Early positioning/mobility when stable, prevent contracture, progressive sitting/standing/gait, balance/vestibular rehab when indicated, aerobic reconditioning, dual-task/cognitive-motor progression and family education. Control environmental stimulation based on tolerance.

### Evaluasi dan kriteria progression

Level of assistance, balance/gait, symptom response to exertion, participation and cognitive safety. Sudden neurologic deterioration requires urgent medical evaluation.

### Differential / kondisi yang jangan terlewat

Stroke, cervical injury, peripheral vestibular disorder, medication effect and post-traumatic headache/neck pain.
