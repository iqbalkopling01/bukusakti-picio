## Basis materi

Materi Parkinson smt 5, Fisioterapi pada Parkinson, geriatri Parkinson, Modified Parkinson Activity Scale.

## Definisi

Parkinson disease adalah neurodegenerative disorder dengan loss dopaminergic neurons substantia nigra dan dysfunction basal ganglia circuits.

## Patofisiologi dan hubungan sebab-akibat

Dopamine depletion mengubah direct/indirect basal ganglia pathway sehingga initiation dan scaling movement terganggu. Automatic movement menurun. Nonmotor autonomic, sleep, cognition dan mood features juga dapat terjadi.

## Manifestasi dan impairment yang diharapkan

Bradykinesia, rigidity, resting tremor, postural instability, reduced arm swing, shuffling, freezing, hypophonia, stooped posture.

## Pemeriksaan fisioterapi

Medication timing, falls/freezing, gait, transfers, turning, balance, ROM, strength, endurance. Assess cue response. Outcomes: TUG, gait speed, 6MWT, Berg, MPAS, freezing questionnaire bila available.

## Problem list / ICF

Susun body structure-function impairment, activity limitation, participation restriction, serta faktor personal/lingkungan. Prioritaskan problem yang paling membatasi tujuan pasien dan paling modifiable oleh fisioterapi.

## Intervensi fisioterapi

Large-amplitude movement practice, external visual/auditory cue, gait/turning/freezing strategies, strength, aerobic, balance/reactive stepping, dual-task progression, flexibility. Train bed mobility and sit-to-stand. Education exercise consistency.

## Progression dan evaluasi

Compare on/off medication state consistently. Track falls/freezing, gait speed, step length, TUG, endurance, transfer.

## Red flags / escalation

Sudden acute change may not be Parkinson progression; screen infection, stroke, medication issue, orthostatic hypotension, falls injury.

## Practical workflow neurologis

### 1. Tentukan localization sebelum latihan

Pada Parkinson Disease, tanyakan onset/progression dan fungsi yang hilang. Periksa mental status/communication bila relevan, cranial nerve bila central, tone, reflex, sensation, myotome/strength, coordination, balance, transfer dan gait. Tujuan awal adalah menjawab: lesi central atau peripheral, level/distribusi apa, dan impairment mana yang paling membatasi activity.

### 2. Safety scan

Cari perubahan neurologis baru, respiratory/autonomic issue, swallowing/aspiration concern, orthostatic intolerance, skin/pressure risk dan fall risk. Kondisi neurologis kronis tetap dapat mengalami acute medical event yang tidak boleh dianggap “bagian dari diagnosis lama”.

### 3. Movement analysis

Amati kualitas task, bukan hanya grade otot. Saat sit-to-stand/gait, catat alignment, weight shift, selective control, timing, tone interaction, sensory dependence, compensation dan assistance.

### 4. Differential contributor

acute medical deterioration, medication effect, infection/metabolic issue, peripheral musculoskeletal limitation, vestibular/visual contributor, cardiorespiratory deconditioning.

### 5. Intervention hierarchy

Pertama jaga ROM, skin, respiratory dan positioning bila ada risiko. Kedua pulihkan task dasar seperti bed mobility, sitting, transfer dan standing. Ketiga tingkatkan strength/endurance/balance. Keempat tambah speed, dual task dan community demand. Handling seperti Bobath atau PNF dipakai untuk membantu pasien aktif menyelesaikan task, bukan menggantikan repetition aktif.

## Goal dan evaluasi

Gunakan level bantuan sebagai outcome penting. Contoh: “sit-to-stand dari min assist menjadi supervision dalam 2 minggu” lebih klinis daripada “tone membaik”. Kombinasikan dengan 10MWT, TUG, Berg, 6MWT, GMFM atau scale lain sesuai populasi.

## SOAP neurologis

S: fatigue, falls, pain, dizziness, medication timing, confidence, caregiver report.

O: neurological sign yang relevan dan functional task yang sama.

A: apakah perubahan disebabkan motor recovery, compensation, cue, device, atau fatigue?

P: tulis target task sesi berikutnya dan level bantuan/cue yang akan dikurangi.

## Deep Dive: mekanisme sampai evaluasi

### Patofisiologi yang perlu benar-benar dipahami

Degeneration of dopaminergic neurons in substantia nigra alters basal ganglia circuits, reducing automatic scaling/initiation of movement. Bradykinesia, rigidity and postural instability affect gait; nonmotor/autonomic symptoms also matter.

### Pemeriksaan terarah

Note medication ON/OFF timing. Examine bradykinesia, rigidity, posture, amplitude, turning, sit-to-stand, freezing triggers, balance, dual-task and gait. Check falls and orthostatic BP. MPAS/TUG/10MWT/Berg or disease-specific measures may be used.

### Intervensi berdasarkan target impairment

Large-amplitude and task-specific practice, external cueing, gait/turn/freezing strategies, progressive strength and power, aerobic exercise, balance/reactive stepping and transfer training. Educate about medication timing and home safety. Cueing should improve task, then be varied to build adaptability.

### Evaluasi dan kriteria progression

Gait speed/step length, turn time/steps, freezing/falls, TUG/MPAS, sit-to-stand, endurance and participation. Compare at similar medication state.

### Differential / kondisi yang jangan terlewat

Atypical parkinsonism, stroke, normal pressure hydrocephalus, medication adverse effects and peripheral/vestibular contributors.
