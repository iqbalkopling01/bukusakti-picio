## Basis materi

Materi meralgia, peripheral nerve.

## Definisi

Entrapment/irritation lateral femoral cutaneous nerve yang bersifat sensory.

## Patofisiologi dan hubungan sebab-akibat

Compression dekat inguinal ligament dapat dipengaruhi tight garment, obesity, pregnancy, trauma, surgery. Karena nerve sensory, true motor weakness bukan feature utama.

## Manifestasi dan impairment yang diharapkan

Burning/numbness anterolateral thigh tanpa motor deficit.

## Pemeriksaan fisioterapi

Sensory map, hip/lumbar screen, motor/reflex normal expected, assess aggravating compression. Differentiate L2-L3 radiculopathy/femoral neuropathy.

## Problem list / ICF

Susun body structure-function impairment, activity limitation, participation restriction, serta faktor personal/lingkungan. Prioritaskan problem yang paling membatasi tujuan pasien dan paling modifiable oleh fisioterapi.

## Intervensi fisioterapi

Reduce compression, activity/garment modification, general mobility/weight management context, neural mobility gentle bila appropriate. Persistent cases medical management.

## Progression dan evaluasi

Symptom area/intensity, standing/walking tolerance.

## Red flags / escalation

Motor weakness/reflex loss suggests alternative diagnosis; systemic progressive neuro needs review.

## Practical workflow neurologis

### 1. Tentukan localization sebelum latihan

Pada Meralgia Paresthetica, tanyakan onset/progression dan fungsi yang hilang. Periksa mental status/communication bila relevan, cranial nerve bila central, tone, reflex, sensation, myotome/strength, coordination, balance, transfer dan gait. Tujuan awal adalah menjawab: lesi central atau peripheral, level/distribusi apa, dan impairment mana yang paling membatasi activity.

### 2. Safety scan

Cari perubahan neurologis baru, respiratory/autonomic issue, swallowing/aspiration concern, orthostatic intolerance, skin/pressure risk dan fall risk. Kondisi neurologis kronis tetap dapat mengalami acute medical event yang tidak boleh dianggap “bagian dari diagnosis lama”.

### 3. Movement analysis

Amati kualitas task, bukan hanya grade otot. Saat sit-to-stand/gait, catat alignment, weight shift, selective control, timing, tone interaction, sensory dependence, compensation dan assistance.

### 4. Differential contributor

regional mechanical disorder, neurological disorder, vascular disorder, infection/inflammation, fracture/trauma, systemic disease.

### 5. Intervention hierarchy

Pertama jaga ROM, skin, respiratory dan positioning bila ada risiko. Kedua pulihkan task dasar seperti bed mobility, sitting, transfer dan standing. Ketiga tingkatkan strength/endurance/balance. Keempat tambah speed, dual task dan community demand. Handling seperti Bobath atau PNF dipakai untuk membantu pasien aktif menyelesaikan task, bukan menggantikan repetition aktif.

## Goal dan evaluasi

Gunakan level bantuan sebagai outcome penting. Contoh: “sit-to-stand dari min assist menjadi supervision dalam 2 minggu” lebih klinis daripada “tone membaik”. Kombinasikan dengan 10MWT, TUG, Berg, 6MWT, GMFM atau scale lain sesuai populasi.

## SOAP neurologis

S: fatigue, falls, pain, dizziness, medication timing, confidence, caregiver report.

O: neurological sign yang relevan dan functional task yang sama.

A: apakah perubahan disebabkan motor recovery, compensation, cue, device, atau fatigue?

P: tulis target task sesi berikutnya dan level bantuan/cue yang akan dikurangi.

## Deep Dive: mekanisme sampai evaluasi

### Patofisiologi yang perlu benar-benar dipahami

Lateral femoral cutaneous nerve entrapment is purely sensory, often near inguinal ligament, causing anterolateral thigh dysesthesia without motor weakness.

### Pemeriksaan terarah

Map sensory symptoms and provoking factors such as standing, tight garments, obesity/pregnancy. Motor/reflex examination should be normal; abnormal quadriceps strength/reflex suggests root/femoral nerve differential. Lumbar/hip screen is important.

### Intervensi berdasarkan target impairment

Reduce external compression, modify load/posture, address mobility if relevant, graded activity and medical management for persistent symptoms. Do not prescribe motor strengthening as if the sensory nerve innervated muscle.

### Evaluasi dan kriteria progression

Symptom area/intensity and standing/walking tolerance.

### Differential / kondisi yang jangan terlewat

L2–L3 radiculopathy, femoral neuropathy, hip pathology and local soft-tissue pain.
