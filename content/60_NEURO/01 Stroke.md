## Basis materi

Materi acute ischemic stroke, ppt stroke, PNF stroke, Bobath, neuromuscular assessment.

## Definisi

Stroke adalah acute neurological dysfunction akibat gangguan vascular otak, ischemic atau hemorrhagic, yang menyebabkan focal/global deficit sesuai area.

## Patofisiologi dan hubungan sebab-akibat

Ischemia mengurangi perfusi sehingga neuron kehilangan oxygen/glucose dan terjadi energy failure, excitotoxicity dan cell death. Hemorrhage menyebabkan bleeding, mass effect, dan secondary injury. Lesion corticospinal/sensory/cerebellar/cortical network menghasilkan pola impairment berbeda. Recovery melibatkan resolution acute injury, compensation, dan neuroplasticity.

## Manifestasi dan impairment yang diharapkan

Hemiparesis, sensory loss, altered tone, facial weakness, aphasia, neglect, visual field loss, balance/gait dysfunction, dysphagia, cognition depending lesion.

## Pemeriksaan fisioterapi

Acute safety/medical status. Cranial nerve, cognition/communication, tone, ROM, strength/selective control, sensation, coordination, sitting/standing balance, transfer, gait. Use outcome sesuai stage: Fugl-Meyer bila available, Berg, TUG, 10MWT, 6MWT, functional independence measures.

## Problem list / ICF

Susun body structure-function impairment, activity limitation, participation restriction, serta faktor personal/lingkungan. Prioritaskan problem yang paling membatasi tujuan pasien dan paling modifiable oleh fisioterapi.

## Intervensi fisioterapi

Early mobilization sesuai medical stability. Task-specific repetition: bed mobility, reach, sit-to-stand, transfer, gait. Strengthening tidak dilarang hanya karena stroke; dose sesuai capacity. Balance, aerobic conditioning, sensory-motor practice, upper-limb task. PNF/Bobath principles sebagai tools, bukan pengganti task practice. Manage shoulder positioning/pain dan contracture risk.

## Progression dan evaluasi

Track assistance level, gait speed/endurance, balance, arm function, strength, spasticity impact, ADL participation. Progress repetitions, task complexity, speed, environment, dual task.

## Red flags / escalation

New acute neuro deficit, sudden severe headache, chest instability, DVT, aspiration concern, autonomic/medical instability.

## Practical workflow neurologis

### 1. Tentukan localization sebelum latihan

Pada Stroke, tanyakan onset/progression dan fungsi yang hilang. Periksa mental status/communication bila relevan, cranial nerve bila central, tone, reflex, sensation, myotome/strength, coordination, balance, transfer dan gait. Tujuan awal adalah menjawab: lesi central atau peripheral, level/distribusi apa, dan impairment mana yang paling membatasi activity.

### 2. Safety scan

Cari perubahan neurologis baru, respiratory/autonomic issue, swallowing/aspiration concern, orthostatic intolerance, skin/pressure risk dan fall risk. Kondisi neurologis kronis tetap dapat mengalami acute medical event yang tidak boleh dianggap “bagian dari diagnosis lama”.

### 3. Movement analysis

Amati kualitas task, bukan hanya grade otot. Saat sit-to-stand/gait, catat alignment, weight shift, selective control, timing, tone interaction, sensory dependence, compensation dan assistance.

### 4. Differential contributor

acute medical deterioration, medication effect, infection/metabolic issue, peripheral musculoskeletal limitation, vestibular/visual contributor, cardiorespiratory deconditioning.

### 5. Intervention hierarchy

Pertama jaga ROM, skin, respiratory dan positioning bila ada risiko. Kedua pulihkan task dasar seperti bed mobility, sitting, transfer dan standing. Ketiga tingkatkan strength/endurance/balance. Keempat tambah speed, dual task dan community demand. Handling seperti Bobath atau PNF dipakai untuk membantu pasien aktif menyelesaikan task, bukan menggantikan repetition aktif.

## Goal dan evaluasi

Gunakan level bantuan sebagai outcome penting. Contoh: “sit-to-stand dari min assist menjadi supervision dalam 2 minggu” lebih klinis daripada “tone membaik”. Kombinasikan dengan 10MWT, TUG, Berg, 6MWT, GMFM atau scale lain sesuai populasi.

## SOAP neurologis

S: fatigue, falls, pain, dizziness, medication timing, confidence, caregiver report.

O: neurological sign yang relevan dan functional task yang sama.

A: apakah perubahan disebabkan motor recovery, compensation, cue, device, atau fatigue?

P: tulis target task sesi berikutnya dan level bantuan/cue yang akan dikurangi.

## Deep Dive: mekanisme sampai evaluasi

### Patofisiologi yang perlu benar-benar dipahami

Ischemic stroke causes focal perfusion failure, ATP depletion, ionic imbalance, excitotoxicity and infarction; hemorrhagic stroke adds bleeding, mass effect and secondary injury. Deficit depends on vascular territory and network: corticospinal lesion produces weakness/selective-control loss; somatosensory lesion alters feedback; cerebellar lesion alters coordination; cortical lesion can create aphasia, neglect, apraxia or visual field loss.

### Pemeriksaan terarah

First establish medical stability and communication/cognition. Examine cranial/visual fields if relevant, tone/reflex, selective motor control and strength, sensation/proprioception, coordination, trunk/sitting, transfer, standing and gait. Check neglect and perceptual issue that affect safety. Use standardized measures appropriate to stage; document assistance and device.

### Intervensi berdasarkan target impairment

High-repetition task-specific practice, progressive strength, aerobic conditioning, balance and gait, upper-limb reach/grasp practice, sensory-motor training and environmental challenge. Positioning/ROM prevent secondary pain/contracture. PNF/Bobath can provide cue/facilitation but active practice and dosage drive learning. Manage shoulder pain with support and active scapulohumeral control.

### Evaluasi dan kriteria progression

Assistance level, gait speed/6MWT, balance, upper-limb task, strength/selective control and participation. Retest in comparable medication/fatigue conditions.

### Differential / kondisi yang jangan terlewat

Recurrent stroke, seizure, DVT, cardiac limitation, vestibular disorder, peripheral neuropathy and MSK pain superimposed on stroke.
