## Basis materi

Materi multiple sclerosis, neuromuscular.

## Definisi

MS adalah immune-mediated demyelinating disease CNS dengan lesion disseminated dalam space/time.

## Patofisiologi dan hubungan sebab-akibat

Inflammation merusak myelin dan axon sehingga conduction melambat/blok. Lesion location bervariasi sehingga symptoms multifocal. Heat sensitivity dan fatigue sering relevan.

## Manifestasi dan impairment yang diharapkan

Fatigue, weakness, sensory change, optic/visual issue, spasticity, ataxia, balance/gait, bladder, cognition.

## Pemeriksaan fisioterapi

Fatigue pattern, relapse status, temperature sensitivity, strength, tone, sensation, coordination, balance, gait, endurance.

## Problem list / ICF

Susun body structure-function impairment, activity limitation, participation restriction, serta faktor personal/lingkungan. Prioritaskan problem yang paling membatasi tujuan pasien dan paling modifiable oleh fisioterapi.

## Intervensi fisioterapi

Energy management tanpa excessive inactivity, aerobic/strength training individualized, balance/gait, stretching spastic muscle, cooling strategy bila heat sensitive, task-specific. Avoid overwork that causes prolonged symptom exacerbation.

## Progression dan evaluasi

Track fatigue, gait speed/endurance, balance, strength, participation. Distinguish temporary symptom worsening from true relapse requiring medical review.

## Red flags / escalation

New neurologic symptoms lasting/persistent suggesting relapse, infection, acute visual/neurologic decline.

## Practical workflow neurologis

### 1. Tentukan localization sebelum latihan

Pada Multiple Sclerosis, tanyakan onset/progression dan fungsi yang hilang. Periksa mental status/communication bila relevan, cranial nerve bila central, tone, reflex, sensation, myotome/strength, coordination, balance, transfer dan gait. Tujuan awal adalah menjawab: lesi central atau peripheral, level/distribusi apa, dan impairment mana yang paling membatasi activity.

### 2. Safety scan

Cari perubahan neurologis baru, respiratory/autonomic issue, swallowing/aspiration concern, orthostatic intolerance, skin/pressure risk dan fall risk. Kondisi neurologis kronis tetap dapat mengalami acute medical event yang tidak boleh dianggap “bagian dari diagnosis lama”.

### 3. Movement analysis

Amati kualitas task, bukan hanya grade otot. Saat sit-to-stand/gait, catat alignment, weight shift, selective control, timing, tone interaction, sensory dependence, compensation dan assistance.

### 4. Differential contributor

acute medical deterioration, medication effect, infection/metabolic issue, peripheral musculoskeletal limitation, vestibular/visual contributor, cardiorespiratory deconditioning.

### 5. Intervention hierarchy

Pertama jaga ROM, skin, respiratory dan positioning bila ada risiko. Kedua pulihkan task dasar seperti bed mobility, sitting, transfer dan standing. Ketiga tingkatkan strength/endurance/balance. Keempat tambah speed, dual task dan community demand. Handling seperti Bobath atau PNF dipakai untuk membantu pasien aktif menyelesaikan task, bukan menggantikan repetition aktif.

## Goal dan evaluasi

Gunakan level bantuan sebagai outcome penting. Contoh: “sit-to-stand dari min assist menjadi supervision dalam 2 minggu” lebih klinis daripada “tone membaik”. Kombinasikan dengan 10MWT, TUG, Berg, 6MWT, GMFM atau scale lain sesuai populasi.

## SOAP neurologis

S: fatigue, falls, pain, dizziness, medication timing, confidence, caregiver report.

O: neurological sign yang relevan dan functional task yang sama.

A: apakah perubahan disebabkan motor recovery, compensation, cue, device, atau fatigue?

P: tulis target task sesi berikutnya dan level bantuan/cue yang akan dikurangi.

## Deep Dive: mekanisme sampai evaluasi

### Patofisiologi yang perlu benar-benar dipahami

Immune-mediated CNS demyelination and neuroaxonal injury create lesions disseminated in space/time. Conduction impairment can worsen with heat/fatigue; symptoms vary widely including weakness, sensory change, ataxia, spasticity and visual/autonomic issues.

### Pemeriksaan terarah

History relapse/stability, fatigue, heat sensitivity, falls, vision and bladder. Examine strength, tone, sensation, coordination, balance, gait and endurance. Avoid testing to exhaustion. Distinguish relapse/new neurologic deficit from ordinary fatigue.

### Intervensi berdasarkan target impairment

Progressive resistance and aerobic exercise are generally useful in stable disease, with pacing, cooling and interval strategies. Balance/gait and task practice, flexibility where needed, energy conservation and assistive device. During relapse modify intensity and coordinate medically.

### Evaluasi dan kriteria progression

Fatigue scale/subjective recovery, gait speed, 6MWT, balance, strength and participation. Monitor delayed recovery/heat response.

### Differential / kondisi yang jangan terlewat

Relapse, infection, medication effect, deconditioning and MSK pain.
