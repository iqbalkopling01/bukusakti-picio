## Basis kasus anonim

Tugas individu penatalaksanaan fisioterapi post-CABG di Drive yang membahas anamnesis, physical examination, outcome, ICF dan intervention context.

## Masalah awal yang dicontohkan

Post-sternotomy pain, difficult cough, dyspnea, weakness, neck/shoulder discomfort, shallow thoracic breathing dan dependence pada change position. Ini menunjukkan post-op CABG bukan hanya cardiac problem; pain, respiratory mechanics, secretion dan deconditioning saling berinteraksi.

## Pemeriksaan

Vital signs, oxygen/device/lines, inspection wound/posture/breathing, palpation accessory muscle/spasm/chest expansion, auscultation lung sounds, chest expansion measurement, pain NRS, Borg dyspnea, ICU Mobility Scale, Barthel Index, 6MWT bila fase sudah sesuai, dan incentive spirometry/volume feedback pada contoh sumber.

## Clinical reasoning akut

Sebelum mobilization, cek hemodynamic stability, arrhythmia, oxygenation, lines/tubes dan medical restrictions. Pain dapat membatasi deep inspiration dan cough, meningkatkan risiko secretion retention/atelectatic pattern. Bed rest mempercepat deconditioning sehingga early mobility diperlukan saat stabil.

## Intervensi

Breathing exercise, supported cough/huff, positioning, shoulder/thoracic mobility sesuai sternal/wound precaution, bed mobility, sitting, standing dan progressive ambulation. Exercise intensity ditentukan oleh vitals, symptom, Borg/RPE dan phase cardiac rehabilitation.

## Evaluasi

Oxygen requirement, RR/SpO2, dyspnea, auscultation/secretions, chest expansion, pain, mobility scale, ADL independence dan walking endurance. Catat adverse signs seperti hypotension, arrhythmia, angina-like symptom atau excessive desaturation.
