## Tujuan potensial

Sensory cue, symptom modulation, edema support, atau facilitation/inhibition concept sesuai materi. Efek tidak boleh dilebih-lebihkan dan harus dievaluasi secara objektif.

## Sebelum aplikasi

Skin clean/dry, screen allergy, wound, fragile skin. Pilih direction, tension, dan anchor berdasarkan target teknik.

## Setelah aplikasi

Periksa irritation, itching, blister, atau discomfort. Edukasi cara melepas tape secara gentle.

## Clinical test

Ulangi functional task setelah tape. Bila tidak ada perubahan bermakna dan tidak ada alasan lain, jangan pertahankan hanya karena “sudah ditempel”.

## Clinical Deep Dive

## Kinesiotaping practical

Tape is an adjunct that may provide sensory cue, short-term pain modulation or support depending application. Evidence and mechanism vary by condition.

### Before taping

Check skin integrity, allergy history, sensation and goal. Clean/dry skin. Round tape corners. Avoid excessive stretch over fragile skin.

### Application logic

Muscle/facilitation-inhibition terminology differs across schools and evidence is inconsistent. Instead define functional goal: cue scapular position, reduce perceived pain, support swelling management, or allow sport exposure. Use minimal tension necessary.

### After

Check itching, burning, blistering, color change. Remove if reaction. Reassess the task immediately. If no effect, do not continue because tape "harus dipakai beberapa kali".
