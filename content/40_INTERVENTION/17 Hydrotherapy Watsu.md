## Properti air

Buoyancy mengurangi effective weight bearing. Hydrostatic pressure memengaruhi circulation dan edema. Viscosity memberi resistance. Temperature memengaruhi comfort dan physiologic response.

## Hydrotherapy

Dapat digunakan untuk ROM, gait, balance, strengthening, aerobic exercise, atau relaxation dengan load darat yang lebih rendah.

## Progression

Ubah water depth, speed, surface area, turbulence, floatation device, direction, dan task complexity.

## Watsu

Pendekatan aquatic yang menggunakan support, gentle movement, stretch, dan relaxation. Pelaksanaan memerlukan training dan screening khusus.

## Contraindication

Open/infected wound yang tidak dapat dikelola, unstable cardiac/respiratory status, uncontrolled seizure tertentu, incontinence/communicable condition sesuai facility policy, dan masalah safety lain.

## Clinical Deep Dive

## Hydrotherapy / Watsu practical

Water buoyancy reduces effective weight bearing, viscosity memberi resistance multi-direction, hydrostatic pressure membantu edema/respiratory mechanics tertentu, warm water meningkatkan comfort.

### Candidates

Painful weight-bearing, weakness, balance training with reduced fall consequence, neurologic movement, general conditioning. Screen wound, infection, continence, unstable cardiac/respiratory, uncontrolled seizure, thermoregulation and pool access/safety.

### Exercise progression

Deeper water → more unloading. Increase speed/surface area to increase resistance. Progress walking, squat, step, balance, arm exercise and aerobic circuit.

### Watsu

Passive/supportive aquatic movement and stretching with therapist support. Treat as complementary/relaxation/mobility technique; patient active exercise is still needed for strength/function goals.

### Outcome

Land-based function must improve; pool performance alone is not enough.
