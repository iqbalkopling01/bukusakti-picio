## Tentukan limiting factor

Pain, weakness, contracture, spasticity, balance, sensation, endurance, fear, foot clearance, stance stability, or coordination.

## Pre-gait

Bed mobility, sitting balance, sit-to-stand, standing tolerance, weight shift, stepping, single-limb support, dan trunk control.

## Parallel bars

Cocok untuk awal bila pasien membutuhkan support tinggi. Terapis memberi cue weight shift, step length, foot placement, dan knee control.

## Assistive device

Pilih walker, crutch, cane, atau device lain berdasarkan balance, upper-limb capacity, weight-bearing status, cognition, dan environment.

## Common targets

Foot clearance, heel contact, knee control stance, hip extension, step symmetry, cadence, turning, obstacle negotiation.

## Cue

Gunakan verbal, visual floor target, mirror, tactile cue, metronome, atau external cue sesuai diagnosis. Pada Parkinson, external cue dapat membantu freezing/bradykinesia pada sebagian pasien.

## Progression

More support → less support. Flat indoor → turns → obstacle → uneven surface → community. Slow controlled → variable speed. Single task → dual task bila aman.

## Outcome

10MWT, TUG, 6MWT, assistance level, device, gait deviation, falls/near falls.

## Clinical Deep Dive

## Gait training operasional

### 1. Identifikasi limiting phase

Gunakan observational gait by phase. Contoh: foot slap loading response → dorsiflexor eccentric control; pelvic drop mid-stance → hip abductor demand; knee hyperextension → plantarflexor/quad/sensory strategy.

### 2. Pilih task

Part practice dapat dipakai untuk komponen sulit, tetapi selalu kembali ke whole gait. Options: weight shift, step initiation, treadmill, overground, obstacle, turn, stair, variable speed.

### 3. Cue

Verbal, visual floor markers, metronome, tactile cue atau device. Gunakan cue minimal yang memberi perubahan, lalu kurangi agar carryover meningkat.

### 4. Device

Device dipilih untuk safety dan efficiency. Progress device hanya jika balance/force/control mendukung, bukan karena pasien "harus lepas alat".

### 5. Dose

Neuro gait recovery membutuhkan repetition tinggi. Akumulasi walking time/distance sambil menjaga safety. Interval dapat meningkatkan total volume.

### Reassessment

10MWT, 6MWT, assistance/device, step symmetry, turn dan fatigue.
