## Tujuan

Mengurangi pain, meningkatkan accessory motion/ROM, atau memodulasi symptom agar exercise/function lebih mudah. Manual therapy bukan tujuan akhir sendiri.

## Assessment sebelum teknik

Pain irritability, AROM/PROM, end-feel, accessory motion, stability, neurological status, red flags, dan response to movement.

## Kaltenborn

Grade I loosen: distraction kecil untuk mengurangi compressive force dan menilai irritability.

Grade II tighten: mengambil slack jaringan sekitar joint.

Grade III stretch: distraction/glide cukup besar untuk memberi stretch capsule/periarticular tissue.

## Maitland

Grade I: small amplitude awal range.

Grade II: large amplitude dalam range tanpa mencapai limit.

Grade III: large amplitude menuju limit/resistance.

Grade IV: small amplitude di akhir range/resistance.

Grade V: high velocity low amplitude thrust, hanya untuk klinisi dengan kompetensi, screening, dan kewenangan sesuai.

## Pemilihan arah

Gunakan anatomy, treatment plane, accessory restriction, symptom response, dan convex-concave reasoning. Jangan mengaplikasikan aturan mekanik secara buta.

## Reassessment

Setelah teknik, ulangi comparable sign: ROM, pain movement, functional task, atau accessory motion. Bila tidak ada benefit bermakna, ubah strategi.

## Contraindication/precaution

Fracture, instability, acute infection, malignancy di area, severe osteoporosis, neurological/vascular red flag, dan kondisi lain memerlukan screening dan modifikasi/avoidance.

## Clinical Deep Dive

## Manual therapy dan joint mobilization operasional

### Assessment sebelum mobilization

Tentukan comparable sign, AROM/PROM, end-feel, joint play dan irritability. Manual technique harus punya hypothesis: pain modulation, joint mobility atau movement facilitation.

### Kaltenborn

Grade I loosen mengurangi compressive/cohesive force tanpa significant tissue tension. Grade II tighten mengambil slack. Grade III stretch memberi tissue stretch. Traction/glide arah mengikuti treatment plane dan arthrokinematic goal.

### Maitland

Grade I small amplitude beginning range, II large amplitude within range, III large amplitude into resistance, IV small amplitude at end range, V high-velocity low-amplitude thrust untuk practitioner trained dan setelah safety screen.

### Convex-concave

Gunakan sebagai biomechanical guide, bukan aturan absolut yang menggantikan symptom response. Pada convex moving on concave, roll dan glide berlawanan; concave moving on convex, searah.

### Dosis

Duration/oscillation/set bervariasi. Gunakan brief trial lalu reassess comparable sign. Jika pain/ROM/function tidak berubah, jangan terus menambah teknik hanya karena "grade belum cukup".

### Contraindication/precaution

Fracture/instability, infection, malignancy, severe osteoporosis, acute inflammatory joint, neurovascular compromise dan condition-specific precautions. Manipulation membutuhkan kompetensi khusus dan screening lebih ketat.
