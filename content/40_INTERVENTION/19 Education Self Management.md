## Isi edukasi

Pemahaman kondisi, prognosis realistis, activity modification, pacing, home exercise, fall prevention, breathing strategy, device use, tissue healing, return to activity, dan warning signs sesuai diagnosis.

## Teach-back

Minta pasien menjelaskan kembali instruksi dengan bahasa sendiri atau mendemonstrasikan latihan. Ini lebih baik daripada hanya bertanya “sudah paham?”.

## Home program

Sedikit latihan yang jelas lebih mudah dipatuhi daripada daftar panjang. Tulis frequency, dose, cue, stop rule, dan progression.

## Self-monitoring

Ajarkan pasien mencatat pain, fatigue, dyspnea, swelling, activity tolerance, atau symptom yang relevan. Self-management harus memberi kontrol, bukan membuat pasien takut bergerak.

## Clinical Deep Dive

## Education and self-management

Education is an intervention only when it changes behavior, understanding or safety. Avoid long lectures.

### Structure

1. Elicit patient belief and goal.
2. Explain mechanism in language relevant to task.
3. Give 1–3 actionable instructions.
4. Demonstrate and use teach-back.
5. Agree on monitoring and progression.

### Home exercise

Keep program small enough to be done. Include exercise name, setup, dose, symptom rule and progression. Use pictures/video if helpful. Ask barriers such as time, equipment, pain fear or caregiver support.

### Self-monitor

Pain/swelling 24-hour response, dyspnea/Borg, fall/near-fall, skin, fatigue or disease-specific signs. Tell patient what is expected and what requires contact/referral.
