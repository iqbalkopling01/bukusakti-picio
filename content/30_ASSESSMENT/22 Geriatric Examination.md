## Comprehensive approach

Pada older adult, masalah sering multifactorial. Gabungkan mobility, balance, strength, endurance, cognition, medication, sensory, fall history, ADL, environment, dan social support.

## Fall history

Tanyakan jumlah jatuh, near-fall, situasi, dizziness, loss of consciousness, footwear, environmental obstacle, assistive device, dan fear of falling.

## Five Times Sit to Stand

Gunakan kursi standar dan instruksi konsisten. Ukur waktu menyelesaikan lima sit-to-stand. Catat penggunaan tangan dan kualitas gerak.

## Timed Up and Go

Dari duduk, berdiri, berjalan jarak protokol, berbalik, kembali dan duduk. Catat waktu, device, assistance, turning strategy.

## Berg Balance Scale

14 tasks functional balance. Gunakan scoring manual resmi. Cocok untuk tracking balance tetapi perhatikan ceiling/floor effect pada populasi tertentu.

## 6MWT

Menilai submaximal walking endurance. Gunakan protokol konsisten dan monitor symptom/vitals.

## MMSE

Screening cognitive domains. Interpretasi dipengaruhi education, language, sensory impairment, dan konteks. Bukan diagnosis dementia tunggal.

## Clock Drawing Test

Screen visuospatial/executive/cognitive function dengan metode scoring yang dipilih secara konsisten.

## Parkinson-specific

Modified Parkinson Activity Scale dapat dipakai untuk activity yang relevan pada Parkinson sesuai materi.

## Sensory and vestibular

Screen vision, hearing, proprioception, dizziness, vestibular symptoms, dan foot sensation bila relevan.

## Practical Detail

## Comprehensive but efficient geriatric screen

Baseline function before current illness → falls → medication → cognition → vision/hearing → nutrition → orthostatic symptom → strength/power → balance/gait → endurance → ADL/environment.

Do not exhaust frail patient with all tests before treatment. Choose a small battery such as 5xSTS + TUG/gait speed + balance + ADL, then add cognition/vestibular/cardio as indicated.

A sudden decline over days is not normal aging and needs medical cause consideration.
