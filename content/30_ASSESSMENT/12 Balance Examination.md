## Komponen

Balance bergantung pada sensory orientation, postural alignment, anticipatory adjustment, reactive response, limits of stability, strength, motor control, dan cognition.

## Static balance

Mulai dari base of support lebar. Progress ke feet together, semi-tandem, tandem, dan single-leg sesuai keamanan. Ubah visual input dengan eyes closed bila aman.

## Romberg

Pasien berdiri feet together. Bandingkan eyes open dan closed. Peningkatan sway yang sangat nyata saat mata ditutup dapat mendukung ketergantungan visual atau gangguan proprioceptive, tetapi interpretasi harus menyeluruh.

## Functional reach

Ukur seberapa jauh pasien dapat reach ke depan tanpa melangkah atau kehilangan balance sesuai protokol alat.

## Reactive balance

Nilai respons terhadap perturbation ringan bila aman. Amati ankle, hip, dan stepping strategy.

## Dynamic balance

Gunakan turning, reaching, stepping over obstacle, tandem walk, stair, atau outcome measure seperti Berg Balance Scale sesuai populasi.

## Safety

Posisi terapis harus guarding tanpa membatasi gerak. Gunakan gait belt bila sesuai setting. Jangan memberikan challenge yang melebihi kemampuan tanpa proteksi.

## Dokumentasi

Catat kondisi visual, surface, base of support, assistive device, bantuan, durasi, dan strategi yang dipakai.

## Practical Detail

## Balance domains

Static steady-state, anticipatory postural adjustment, reactive balance and dynamic gait balance. A patient may be good in one and poor in another.

### Clinical progression test

Sitting → standing wide BOS → narrow → semi-tandem/tandem → single leg; eyes open/closed; reach; turn; step; perturbation. Only progress if guarding and safety adequate.

### Sensory organization

Firm vs compliant surface and eyes open/closed can reveal visual/somatosensory dependence, but this is a clinical challenge not a formal sensory-organization test unless using standardized equipment.

Use Berg/PBS, FIST, TUG or other instrument according to population.
