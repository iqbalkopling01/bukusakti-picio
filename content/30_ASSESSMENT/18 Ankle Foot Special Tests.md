## Anterior Drawer Ankle

Stabilkan tibia, ankle sedikit plantarflex, tarik calcaneus/talus anterior. Increased translation atau soft end-feel dapat mendukung ATFL injury.

## Talar Tilt

Berikan inversion atau eversion stress sesuai ligament target. Excess tilt/pain dibanding sisi mendukung lateral atau medial ligament involvement.

## Squeeze Test

Compress tibia-fibula di proximal/mid calf. Distal syndesmosis pain dapat mendukung high ankle sprain hypothesis.

## External Rotation Stress

Stabilkan leg dan berikan external rotation pada foot/ankle sesuai protokol. Distal syndesmotic pain dapat mendukung syndesmosis involvement.

## Thompson Test

Pasien prone dengan feet melewati edge. Squeeze calf. Normalnya terjadi plantarflexion. Tidak adanya plantarflexion meningkatkan kecurigaan Achilles rupture.

## Windlass Test

Dorsiflex great toe saat weight bearing atau non-weight bearing sesuai protokol. Reproduction plantar fascia pain/tension dapat mendukung plantar fascia involvement.

## Navicular Drop

Bandingkan navicular height dari subtalar neutral ke relaxed stance. Digunakan sebagai salah satu gambaran foot posture, bukan diagnosis sendiri.

## Practical Detail

## Ligament

Anterior drawer biases ATFL; talar tilt assesses lateral ligament complex depending direction. Perform after acute pain/swelling allow reliable test.

## Syndesmosis

Squeeze and external-rotation stress can reproduce distal syndesmotic pain. High ankle sprain often has longer recovery; mechanism and tenderness are important.

## Achilles

Thompson calf squeeze: absent/reduced plantarflexion suggests Achilles rupture and requires medical referral. Compare sides.

## Fracture screen

Use validated Ottawa Ankle/Foot Rules where applicable rather than palpating indefinitely.
