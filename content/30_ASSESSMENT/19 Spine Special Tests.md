## Cervical Spurling

Cervical extension, side-bending/rotation ke sisi gejala dengan axial compression secara terkontrol sesuai protokol. Reproduction radicular arm symptoms lebih bermakna daripada local neck pain.

## Cervical Distraction

Supine atau sitting. Berikan gentle traction. Reduction radicular symptoms dapat mendukung cervical radiculopathy hypothesis.

## Upper Limb Neurodynamic

Gunakan [[30_ASSESSMENT/14 Neurodynamic Tests]]. Kombinasikan dengan Spurling, distraction, cervical rotation ROM, myotome, dermatome, dan reflex.

## Straight Leg Raise

Untuk lumbar radicular hypothesis, gunakan SLR dengan structural differentiation. Lihat [[30_ASSESSMENT/14 Neurodynamic Tests]].

## Slump

Menambah neural load melalui spinal flexion, knee extension, ankle position. Interpretasi membutuhkan perubahan dengan structural differentiation.

## Femoral Nerve Stretch

Prone knee bend atau variant hip extension untuk upper lumbar/femoral nerve system.

## Lumbar quadrant / extension-rotation

Extension dengan side-bending/rotation dapat memprovokasi posterior element atau foraminal symptoms, tetapi tidak spesifik untuk satu struktur.

## SIJ provocation concept

Gunakan cluster provocation bila diajarkan, bukan alignment palpation tunggal. Tes seperti distraction, compression, thigh thrust, sacral thrust dapat dipakai sesuai kompetensi dan protokol.

## Red flags spine

Progressive neurological deficit, saddle anesthesia, bladder-bowel dysfunction baru, major trauma, suspected fracture/infection/malignancy memerlukan escalation.

## Practical Detail

## Cervical

Spurling: extension/side-flexion/axial compression variant to reproduce radicular arm symptom. Distraction reduction of symptom supports root compression hypothesis. ULTT and reflex/myotome complete cluster. Hoffmann/clonus/gait are myelopathy screen, not radiculopathy tests.

## Lumbar

SLR/slump for lower-lumbar neural mechanosensitivity, femoral nerve stretch for upper lumbar. Repeated movement can assess directional response/centralization. Prone instability or segmental tests have limited standalone value.

Red flags and neuro deficit override special-test cluster.
