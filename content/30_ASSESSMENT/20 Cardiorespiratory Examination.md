## Subjective

Dyspnea, cough, sputum, wheeze, chest pain, orthopnea, paroxysmal nocturnal dyspnea, fatigue, exercise tolerance, smoking, infection history, cardiac history, surgery, medication, edema, syncope.

## Observation

Respiratory rate, depth, rhythm, accessory muscle use, chest symmetry, posture, cyanosis, clubbing bila ada, edema, jugular appearance sesuai kompetensi, work of breathing.

## Palpation

Chest expansion dapat dibandingkan kanan-kiri. Tactile fremitus dan tracheal position hanya dilakukan bila termasuk kompetensi yang diajarkan.

## Percussion

Digunakan untuk membandingkan resonance area thorax. Dullness atau hyperresonance harus diinterpretasikan bersama auscultation dan medical findings.

## Auscultation

Dengarkan area simetris anterior, lateral, posterior. Identifikasi normal breath sound dan added sound seperti crackles atau wheeze. Catat lokasi dan fase respirasi.

## Cough dan sputum

Nilai effective/ineffective cough, dry/productive, sputum amount, color, viscosity, dan kemampuan clearance.

## Dyspnea

Gunakan Borg scale atau mMRC sesuai kebutuhan. Gabungkan dengan RR, SpO2, HR, activity.

## Functional capacity

6MWT: ukur jarak selama 6 menit mengikuti protokol standar setting. Catat HR, SpO2, dyspnea/fatigue, assistive device, oxygen bila digunakan, dan reason for stopping.

## Cardiac screening

HR/rhythm, BP, peripheral edema, symptom response, exercise tolerance. Chest pain, syncope, acute severe dyspnea, atau unstable vitals membutuhkan medical escalation.

## Reassessment

Gunakan outcome yang relevan: walking distance, dyspnea, SpO2 response, RR recovery, cough effectiveness, sputum clearance, functional task.

## Practical Detail

## Full bedside sequence

General stability/vitals → inspection → palpation → percussion → auscultation → cough/sputum → chest expansion → functional challenge/outcome. Tidak semua pasien membutuhkan semua langkah.

Inspection: oxygen/device, pattern, accessory muscle, color, posture. Palpation: expansion, fremitus. Percussion: resonance/dull/hyperresonance. Auscultation: location, inspiratory/expiratory phase and change after cough. Then Borg/mMRC/6MWT or bedside mobility according to stability.
