## Valgus stress elbow

Elbow sedikit flexed. Terapkan valgus force sambil stabilisasi humerus. Pain/laxity medial dapat mendukung UCL involvement.

## Varus stress elbow

Berikan varus force. Pain/laxity lateral dapat mendukung lateral collateral complex involvement.

## Cozen test

Elbow flexed atau posisi sesuai protokol, forearm pronated. Pasien melakukan resisted wrist extension dan radial deviation. Lateral epicondyle pain dapat mendukung lateral elbow tendinopathy pattern.

## Mill test

Passive stretch extensor group melalui elbow extension, forearm pronation, wrist flexion. Familiar lateral epicondyle pain dapat mendukung tendinopathy pattern.

## Maudsley test

Resisted middle finger extension. Lateral elbow pain dapat memprovokasi extensor mechanism.

## Tinel cubital tunnel

Percuss ringan area ulnar nerve sekitar cubital tunnel. Tingling pada distribution ulnar dapat mendukung nerve irritability.

## Finkelstein / Eichhoff concept

Thumb dimasukkan ke palm lalu wrist diarahkan ulnar deviation secara hati-hati sesuai teknik. Pain di first dorsal compartment mendukung De Quervain pattern. Hindari force berlebihan karena dapat provokasi pada orang tanpa kelainan.

## Phalen

Wrist full flexion dipertahankan sampai waktu protokol. Paresthesia median distribution mendukung carpal tunnel syndrome.

## Tinel carpal tunnel

Percuss ringan median nerve di carpal tunnel. Tingling ke thumb-index-middle/radial ring finger dapat mendukung nerve irritability.

## Durkan compression

Tekanan langsung pada carpal tunnel selama durasi protokol. Reproduksi median distribution symptoms mendukung CTS.

## Froment sign

Pasien menjepit kertas antara thumb dan index. Flexion IP thumb sebagai kompensasi dapat menunjukkan weakness adductor pollicis karena ulnar nerve dysfunction.

## Allen test

Digunakan untuk screening collateral circulation hand sebelum prosedur tertentu. Compression radial dan ulnar artery dilakukan sesuai protokol lalu satu sisi dilepas dan reperfusion diamati. Ikuti SOP karena interpretasi visual dapat bervariasi.

## Practical Detail

## Selection by hypothesis

Lateral elbow: resisted wrist extension/middle finger and stretch can reproduce extensor tendon pain, but cervical/radial nerve screen matters. Carpal tunnel: Phalen/Tinel/compression are supportive; median sensory/motor and cervical/root screen strengthen localization. De Quervain: Finkelstein/Eichhoff with first-compartment palpation. Ulnar collateral elbow in athlete: valgus stress/moving valgus under trained exam.

For hand tendon/ligament injury, compare active vs passive motion and isolated tendon function before provocative special tests.
