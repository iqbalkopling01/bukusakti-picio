## Prinsip

Pemeriksaan anak harus mempertimbangkan usia koreksi, developmental stage, state regulation, caregiver report, play, dan environment. Observasi spontaneous movement sering sama pentingnya dengan test formal.

## History

Pregnancy/birth history, gestational age, neonatal complication, feeding, sleep, seizure, cardiac/respiratory condition, milestone, regression, school/play participation, family goals.

## Observation

Posture, symmetry, spontaneous movement, antigravity control, head/trunk control, reaching, rolling, sitting, crawling, kneeling, standing, gait sesuai usia.

## Tone dan reflex

Nilai tone, primitive reflex persistence/integration, postural reactions, dan selective control sesuai developmental context.

## ROM dan musculoskeletal screening

Perhatikan contracture, hypermobility, deformity, torticollis, foot alignment, hip stability sesuai indikasi.

## Outcome measures yang ada di materi Drive

### AIMS

Alberta Infant Motor Scale menilai gross motor infant melalui observasi prone, supine, sitting, standing. Gunakan manual/scoring sheet resmi.

### HINE

Hammersmith Infant Neurological Examination menilai cranial nerve, posture, movement, tone, reflex/reaction dan aspek neurologis infant. Ikuti form dan umur yang sesuai.

### GMFM

Gross Motor Function Measure menilai lying/rolling, sitting, crawling/kneeling, standing, walking-running-jumping. Banyak digunakan pada CP dan kondisi motorik anak.

### SATCo

Segmental Assessment of Trunk Control menilai control trunk dari level atas ke bawah dengan support yang diturunkan bertahap.

### TCMS

Trunk Control Measurement Scale menilai static sitting balance, dynamic sitting balance/selective movement, dan reaching/dynamic control sesuai versi.

### PDMS-2

Peabody Developmental Motor Scales mengukur domain gross dan fine motor sesuai kelompok umur serta prosedur standar.

### PEDI-CAT

Menilai function/participation melalui domain yang relevan menggunakan item berbasis caregiver/report dan computer-adaptive format bila tersedia.

### PBS

Pediatric Balance Scale merupakan adaptasi balance tasks untuk anak.

### HCRS

Head Control Rating Scale digunakan pada laporan kasus untuk mendokumentasikan head control.

### Beighton Score

Screen generalized joint hypermobility dengan item hyperextension tertentu dan trunk flexion. Gunakan cutoff sesuai usia/konteks guideline.

## Dokumentasi

Catat level bantuan, quality movement, compensation, fatigue, attention, motivation, dan caregiver handling. Jangan hanya mencatat “bisa/tidak bisa”.

## Practical Detail

## Developmental exam sequence

Caregiver history → observe spontaneous movement before handling → state/attention → head/trunk → symmetry/tone → ROM/alignment → antigravity strength → transitions → balance/gait → age/diagnosis-specific outcome.

Corrected age matters in prematurity. Regression, feeding/respiratory decline or loss of antigravity movement is red flag.

Specific tools are not interchangeable. AIMS = infant gross motor observation; HINE = neurologic infant; GMFM = gross motor change; SATCo = segmental trunk; TCMS = sitting trunk; PDMS = developmental motor profile; PEDI-CAT = functional daily activity/participation.
