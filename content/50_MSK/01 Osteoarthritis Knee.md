## Basis materi

Materi OA Knee semester 5, Joint Degenerative, Osteoarthritis dan Degenerative Arthropathy, strengthening, gait, dan materi outcome geriatri.

## Definisi

Osteoarthritis adalah gangguan sendi degeneratif yang melibatkan cartilage, subchondral bone, synovium, capsule, dan struktur periarticular. Gejala klinis tidak selalu berbanding lurus dengan gambaran radiologi.

## Patofisiologi dan hubungan sebab-akibat

Degenerasi cartilage mengubah distribusi load. Subchondral bone dan synovium dapat ikut mengalami perubahan. Nyeri dan effusion menurunkan activation quadriceps. Pasien mengurangi loading sehingga strength dan aerobic capacity turun. Reduced extension atau flexion kemudian mengubah gait dan meningkatkan demand pada sendi lain. Siklus pain → avoidance → weakness → reduced function dapat mempertahankan disability meskipun struktur tidak berubah cepat.

## Manifestasi dan impairment yang diharapkan

Pain saat weight bearing, morning stiffness relatif singkat, crepitus, limited ROM terutama extension/flexion, quadriceps weakness, reduced walking tolerance, difficulty stair/squat/sit-to-stand. Varus/valgus alignment dapat muncul tetapi tidak otomatis menentukan severity.

## Pemeriksaan fisioterapi

Subjective: load-related pain, stiffness, swelling, giving way, walking/stair tolerance, sleep, activity goals. Objective: observation alignment dan gait, effusion, tenderness joint line, AROM/PROM, extension deficit, patellar mobility bila relevan, quadriceps/hip strength, sit-to-stand, balance. Special tests tidak dipakai untuk “membuktikan OA”; diagnosis medis sering berdasarkan kombinasi usia, symptoms, exam, imaging bila perlu. Outcome: NPRS, ROM, 5xSTS, TUG, 6MWT atau questionnaire knee bila tersedia.

## Problem list / ICF

Susun body structure-function impairment, activity limitation, participation restriction, serta faktor personal/lingkungan. Prioritaskan problem yang paling membatasi tujuan pasien dan paling modifiable oleh fisioterapi.

## Intervensi fisioterapi

Education tentang load management tanpa fear. Strengthening quadriceps, hip abductors/extensors, calf sesuai deficit. Aerobic exercise low-impact atau walking sesuai toleransi. Mobility untuk extension/flexion bila terbatas. Balance dan gait training bila instability/fall risk. Manual therapy dapat dipakai sebagai adjunct jika membantu ROM/pain, lalu selalu diikuti active exercise. Weight management dan physical activity dibahas dalam tim bila relevan.

## Progression dan evaluasi

Reassess pain saat task, knee ROM, quadriceps performance, 5xSTS, walking/stair tolerance. Progress resistance dan task complexity bila 24-hour response stabil. Setelah flare, kurangi sementara volume/intensity tetapi hindari immobilization berkepanjangan.

## Red flags / escalation

Acute hot swollen joint dengan systemic symptoms, unexplained rapid deterioration, suspected fracture, DVT, atau severe night/systemic features memerlukan medical evaluation.

## Practical workflow saat pasien datang

### 1. Triage 2–3 menit

Untuk Knee Osteoarthritis, jangan langsung melakukan special test. Pastikan mekanisme dan symptom behavior masuk pola yang masuk akal. Tanyakan trauma, onset, irritability, kemampuan weight bearing atau menggunakan anggota gerak, night/systemic symptoms, neurologic symptoms, dan tujuan aktivitas. Lakukan red-flag screen sebelum loading agresif.

### 2. Comparable sign

Pilih satu sampai tiga gerakan atau task yang mereproduksi masalah secara aman, misalnya squat, reach, stair, gait, grip, shoulder elevation, atau sport movement. Comparable sign ini akan dipakai untuk melihat apakah pemeriksaan dan intervensi benar-benar mengubah fungsi.

### 3. Scan objektif

Urutan yang efisien: observation → AROM → PROM bila perlu → resisted/muscle performance → palpation terarah → neurological screen jika ada radiation/paresthesia → special test berdasarkan hipotesis → functional test. Jangan melakukan semua special test yang dihafal. Pilih tes yang dapat membedakan hipotesis.

### 4. Differential yang harus tetap di kepala

fracture, meniscal injury, ligament injury, patellofemoral pain, hip referral, lumbar/neural referral, inflammatory/infective joint.

### 5. Setelah tes

Rangkum temuan menjadi pola: pain-dominant, stiffness-dominant, weakness/capacity-dominant, instability, neural, atau mixed. Pilih intervensi yang menargetkan pola tersebut.

## Contoh goal dan progression

Short-term goal harus terukur, misalnya “dalam 2 minggu pasien meningkatkan knee extension dari -10° menjadi -3° dan melakukan sit-to-stand 5 kali tanpa peningkatan nyeri > baseline keesokan hari.” Long-term goal berbasis aktivitas, misalnya kembali berjalan 30 menit, naik tangga, mengangkat barang, atau kembali latihan.

Progression umumnya bergerak dari controlled low-load → full ROM/load → higher resistance/endurance → speed/power → complex functional/sport task. Jika condition berupa fracture/post-op, progression tetap tunduk pada biological healing dan precaution.

## Contoh evaluasi SOAP singkat

S: pasien melaporkan perubahan nyeri, stiffness, confidence, dan activity tolerance sejak sesi sebelumnya.

O: ulangi comparable sign dan minimal satu ukuran objektif seperti ROM, force, gait, sit-to-stand, hop, atau task. Catat intervensi dan dosis aktual.

A: jelaskan apakah kapasitas meningkat, gejala stabil, atau loading terlalu tinggi. Hubungkan perubahan dengan goal.

P: pertahankan, progress, regress, atau ubah intervensi. Tentukan apa yang akan diukur sesi berikutnya.

## Deep Dive: mekanisme sampai evaluasi

### Patofisiologi yang perlu benar-benar dipahami

Cartilage matrix degradation and altered chondrocyte homeostasis reduce the ability to distribute compressive load. Subchondral bone remodeling, osteophyte formation, synovitis and capsular/periarticular change contribute to pain and stiffness. Pain/effusion can inhibit quadriceps activation. Reduced activity then lowers muscle capacity and increases relative demand during stairs, sit-to-stand and walking. Radiographic severity and pain can be discordant, so function must be examined directly.

### Pemeriksaan terarah

Quantify irritability and 24-hour response. Inspect varus/valgus, swelling and gait. Measure AROM/PROM with emphasis on extension loss, flexion, end-feel and pain location. Check effusion, joint-line/patellofemoral tenderness, patellar mobility when relevant, quadriceps and hip strength, calf capacity, balance, sit-to-stand and stairs. Screen hip/lumbar referral and inflammatory/systemic features. Use pain scale plus 5xSTS/TUG/6MWT or knee-specific PROM when available.

### Intervensi berdasarkan target impairment

Education focuses on graded loading rather than avoidance. Build quadriceps and hip strength with isometric or lower-load options during irritability, then progressive resistance through tolerated ROM. Add aerobic work, step/sit-to-stand practice, mobility for extension/flexion, balance and gait. Manual therapy can temporarily improve pain/ROM in selected patients but should feed into active loading. Adjust exercise volume when effusion or next-day pain rises meaningfully.

### Evaluasi dan kriteria progression

Recheck the same provocative task, ROM, effusion, force/repetition capacity and walking/stair tolerance. Improvement should be expressed as function and load tolerance, not only pain at rest. If symptoms flare, compare 24-hour response and modify volume/intensity before abandoning exercise.

### Differential / kondisi yang jangan terlewat

Meniscal lesion, ligament injury, patellofemoral pain, inflammatory arthritis, crystal arthropathy, referred hip/lumbar pain, fracture, infection and DVT depending presentation.
