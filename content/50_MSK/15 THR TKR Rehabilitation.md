## Basis materi

Materi FT B THR/TKR, studi kasus TKR & THR, OA, gait, strengthening.

## Definisi

Total joint replacement mengganti articular surfaces hip atau knee untuk severe joint disease. Rehab dipengaruhi surgical approach, precautions, comorbidity, pre-op function, dan complication.

## Patofisiologi dan hubungan sebab-akibat

Surgery menimbulkan tissue trauma, inflammation, pain, edema, temporary muscle inhibition dan mobility loss. Early mobilization mencegah deconditioning dan complication. Loading progresif memulihkan strength/function.

## Manifestasi dan impairment yang diharapkan

Post-op pain/swelling, reduced ROM, weakness, gait/device dependence, difficulty transfer/stair.

## Pemeriksaan fisioterapi

Check surgical order, weight-bearing status, hip precaution approach-specific bila THR, wound, DVT signs, vitals. ROM, quadriceps/hip activation, transfer, gait, stair, functional outcomes.

## Problem list / ICF

Susun body structure-function impairment, activity limitation, participation restriction, serta faktor personal/lingkungan. Prioritaskan problem yang paling membatasi tujuan pasien dan paling modifiable oleh fisioterapi.

## Intervensi fisioterapi

Early: breathing/circulation exercise, bed mobility, sit-to-stand, gait device, ROM allowed, quad/glute activation. Progress resistance, gait distance, stair, balance. TKR fokus extension/flexion recovery dan quadriceps. THR fokus hip strength/control sesuai approach precautions.

## Progression dan evaluasi

Track wound, edema, pain, ROM, strength, device level, 5xSTS/TUG/walking. Progress device only when gait safe, not hanya karena waktu.

## Red flags / escalation

DVT/PE signs, wound infection, dislocation signs, fracture, uncontrolled pain, fever, neurovascular deficit.

## Practical workflow saat pasien datang

### 1. Triage 2–3 menit

Untuk THR and TKR Rehabilitation, jangan langsung melakukan special test. Pastikan mekanisme dan symptom behavior masuk pola yang masuk akal. Tanyakan trauma, onset, irritability, kemampuan weight bearing atau menggunakan anggota gerak, night/systemic symptoms, neurologic symptoms, dan tujuan aktivitas. Lakukan red-flag screen sebelum loading agresif.

### 2. Comparable sign

Pilih satu sampai tiga gerakan atau task yang mereproduksi masalah secara aman, misalnya squat, reach, stair, gait, grip, shoulder elevation, atau sport movement. Comparable sign ini akan dipakai untuk melihat apakah pemeriksaan dan intervensi benar-benar mengubah fungsi.

### 3. Scan objektif

Urutan yang efisien: observation → AROM → PROM bila perlu → resisted/muscle performance → palpation terarah → neurological screen jika ada radiation/paresthesia → special test berdasarkan hipotesis → functional test. Jangan melakukan semua special test yang dihafal. Pilih tes yang dapat membedakan hipotesis.

### 4. Differential yang harus tetap di kepala

fracture, meniscal injury, ligament injury, patellofemoral pain, hip referral, lumbar/neural referral, inflammatory/infective joint.

### 5. Setelah tes

Rangkum temuan menjadi pola: pain-dominant, stiffness-dominant, weakness/capacity-dominant, instability, neural, atau mixed. Pilih intervensi yang menargetkan pola tersebut.

## Contoh goal dan progression

Short-term goal harus terukur, misalnya “dalam 2 minggu pasien meningkatkan knee extension dari -10° menjadi -3° dan melakukan sit-to-stand 5 kali tanpa peningkatan nyeri > baseline keesokan hari.” Long-term goal berbasis aktivitas, misalnya kembali berjalan 30 menit, naik tangga, mengangkat barang, atau kembali latihan.

Progression umumnya bergerak dari controlled low-load → full ROM/load → higher resistance/endurance → speed/power → complex functional/sport task. Jika condition berupa fracture/post-op, progression tetap tunduk pada biological healing dan precaution.

## Contoh evaluasi SOAP singkat

S: pasien melaporkan perubahan nyeri, stiffness, confidence, dan activity tolerance sejak sesi sebelumnya.

O: ulangi comparable sign dan minimal satu ukuran objektif seperti ROM, force, gait, sit-to-stand, hop, atau task. Catat intervensi dan dosis aktual.

A: jelaskan apakah kapasitas meningkat, gejala stabil, atau loading terlalu tinggi. Hubungkan perubahan dengan goal.

P: pertahankan, progress, regress, atau ubah intervensi. Tentukan apa yang akan diukur sesi berikutnya.

## Deep Dive: mekanisme sampai evaluasi

### Patofisiologi yang perlu benar-benar dipahami

Arthroplasty replaces damaged joint surfaces, often after advanced OA/RA/trauma. Post-operative impairment reflects surgical trauma, pain/effusion, inhibition, pre-op deconditioning and movement restrictions rather than the implant alone.

### Pemeriksaan terarah

Confirm surgical approach, weight-bearing, wound and surgeon precautions. TKR: extension/flexion, swelling, quadriceps activation/SLR lag, distal neurovascular, gait. THR: hip ROM within precautions, abductor/extensor strength, leg-length perception, transfer and gait. Screen DVT/infection/dislocation.

### Intervensi berdasarkan target impairment

Early mobility, circulation/ankle pumping, ROM within protocol, quadriceps/gluteal activation, transfer/gait device and progressive strength. Progress to stairs, endurance and community tasks. Modality can support symptom management but active rehab is primary.

### Evaluasi dan kriteria progression

Wound, pain, swelling, ROM, strength, assistance/device, gait speed/distance, stairs and patient-reported function. Progress based on healing and function.

### Differential / kondisi yang jangan terlewat

DVT/PE, infection, prosthetic dislocation, fracture, nerve injury, loosening later.
