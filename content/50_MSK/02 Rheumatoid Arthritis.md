## Basis materi

Materi Rheumatoid Arthritis semester 5, patofisiologi inflammation/immunity, exercise, modalitas.

## Definisi

RA adalah penyakit inflamasi autoimun sistemik yang terutama menyerang synovial joints dan dapat menimbulkan deformity serta manifestasi ekstra-artikular.

## Patofisiologi dan hubungan sebab-akibat

Autoimmune synovitis menyebabkan proliferasi jaringan inflamasi, effusion, pain, dan kerusakan cartilage/bone bila berlanjut. Systemic inflammation menyebabkan fatigue dan deconditioning. Pada cervical spine, ligamentous involvement dapat meningkatkan risiko instability pada sebagian pasien, sehingga screening penting sebelum manual therapy cervical.

## Manifestasi dan impairment yang diharapkan

Symmetric small-joint pain/swelling, morning stiffness, fatigue, reduced grip, deformity pada penyakit lanjut, reduced ROM dan strength. Flare dan remission dapat berganti.

## Pemeriksaan fisioterapi

History flare, medication, fatigue, systemic symptom, cervical symptom. Inspect swelling/deformity. ROM gentle, functional grip, strength submaximal bila joint inflamed. Assess ADL dan endurance. Hindari aggressive stress pada acutely inflamed joint.

## Problem list / ICF

Susun body structure-function impairment, activity limitation, participation restriction, serta faktor personal/lingkungan. Prioritaskan problem yang paling membatasi tujuan pasien dan paling modifiable oleh fisioterapi.

## Intervensi fisioterapi

Saat flare: education, protection, gentle pain-free ROM, positioning, energy conservation, low-load activity. Saat disease controlled: progressive strengthening, aerobic exercise, hand function, balance, flexibility. Splint/assistive device melalui kolaborasi bila perlu. Manual therapy hanya dengan screening ketat dan bukan pada joint unstable/actively inflamed.

## Progression dan evaluasi

Track swollen/painful joint behavior, ROM, grip/function, fatigue, walking tolerance. Progress perlahan dan pertimbangkan systemic fatigue serta medication effect.

## Red flags / escalation

New neurologic signs dengan neck pain, severe systemic flare, fever/infection, chest symptoms, atau rapidly progressive joint swelling memerlukan medical review.

## Practical workflow saat pasien datang

### 1. Triage 2–3 menit

Untuk Rheumatoid Arthritis, jangan langsung melakukan special test. Pastikan mekanisme dan symptom behavior masuk pola yang masuk akal. Tanyakan trauma, onset, irritability, kemampuan weight bearing atau menggunakan anggota gerak, night/systemic symptoms, neurologic symptoms, dan tujuan aktivitas. Lakukan red-flag screen sebelum loading agresif.

### 2. Comparable sign

Pilih satu sampai tiga gerakan atau task yang mereproduksi masalah secara aman, misalnya squat, reach, stair, gait, grip, shoulder elevation, atau sport movement. Comparable sign ini akan dipakai untuk melihat apakah pemeriksaan dan intervensi benar-benar mengubah fungsi.

### 3. Scan objektif

Urutan yang efisien: observation → AROM → PROM bila perlu → resisted/muscle performance → palpation terarah → neurological screen jika ada radiation/paresthesia → special test berdasarkan hipotesis → functional test. Jangan melakukan semua special test yang dihafal. Pilih tes yang dapat membedakan hipotesis.

### 4. Differential yang harus tetap di kepala

regional mechanical disorder, neurological disorder, vascular disorder, infection/inflammation, fracture/trauma, systemic disease.

### 5. Setelah tes

Rangkum temuan menjadi pola: pain-dominant, stiffness-dominant, weakness/capacity-dominant, instability, neural, atau mixed. Pilih intervensi yang menargetkan pola tersebut.

## Contoh goal dan progression

Short-term goal harus terukur, misalnya “dalam 2 minggu pasien meningkatkan knee extension dari -10° menjadi -3° dan melakukan sit-to-stand 5 kali tanpa peningkatan nyeri > baseline keesokan hari.” Long-term goal berbasis aktivitas, misalnya kembali berjalan 30 menit, naik tangga, mengangkat barang, atau kembali latihan.

Progression umumnya bergerak dari controlled low-load → full ROM/load → higher resistance/endurance → speed/power → complex functional/sport task. Jika condition berupa fracture/post-op, progression tetap tunduk pada biological healing dan precaution.

## Contoh evaluasi SOAP singkat

S: pasien melaporkan perubahan nyeri, stiffness, confidence, dan activity tolerance sejak sesi sebelumnya.

O: ulangi comparable sign dan minimal satu ukuran objektif seperti ROM, force, gait, sit-to-stand, hop, atau task. Catat intervensi dan dosis aktual.

A: jelaskan apakah kapasitas meningkat, gejala stabil, atau loading terlalu tinggi. Hubungkan perubahan dengan goal.

P: pertahankan, progress, regress, atau ubah intervensi. Tentukan apa yang akan diukur sesi berikutnya.

## Deep Dive: mekanisme sampai evaluasi

### Patofisiologi yang perlu benar-benar dipahami

Autoimmune synovial inflammation drives persistent synovitis, pannus formation and progressive cartilage/bone erosion. Systemic inflammation, pain, fatigue and corticosteroid exposure can reduce muscle/bone capacity. Joint instability/deformity may emerge over time. Cervical involvement, especially upper cervical instability, is clinically important in established RA.

### Pemeriksaan terarah

History disease activity, flare pattern, medication, fatigue and systemic symptoms. Inspect swelling/deformity, palpate warmth/tenderness, assess functional ROM and strength without repeatedly provoking inflamed joints. Screen cervical symptoms before aggressive cervical manual therapy. Track grip, sit-to-stand, gait, ADL and fatigue. Refer acute hot joint/systemic infection concern.

### Intervensi berdasarkan target impairment

During active flare use joint protection, comfortable ROM, low-load muscle activation, pacing and aerobic activity below flare threshold. During stable disease use progressive strength and aerobic exercise, hand/functional training, balance and education. Avoid aggressive mobilization of acutely inflamed/unstable joints. Coordinate with rheumatology because disease-modifying therapy controls the underlying inflammatory process.

### Evaluasi dan kriteria progression

Monitor pain/swelling, morning stiffness duration, fatigue, functional strength, walking/ADL and flare frequency. A good rehab response is improved capacity without sustained increase in joint swelling/systemic fatigue.

### Differential / kondisi yang jangan terlewat

OA, septic arthritis, gout/pseudogout, connective tissue disease, medication adverse effect and cervical myelopathy/instability.
