## Basis materi

Materi FRAKTUR, Fraktur Ekstremitas, injury-inflammation-healing, laporan THR/TKR dan praktikum MSK.

## Definisi

Fracture adalah terputusnya kontinuitas tulang akibat trauma, overload berulang, atau pathological weakening.

## Patofisiologi dan hubungan sebab-akibat

Setelah fracture terbentuk hematoma dan inflammatory phase. Reparative phase membentuk soft lalu hard callus. Remodeling mengorganisasi tulang mengikuti mechanical demand. Rate dipengaruhi lokasi, stability, blood supply, usia, nutrition, comorbidity, dan fixation. Immobilization menyebabkan joint stiffness, muscle atrophy, edema, dan deconditioning.

## Manifestasi dan impairment yang diharapkan

Pain, swelling, loss of function, restricted weight bearing, stiffness setelah immobilization, weakness, altered gait.

## Pemeriksaan fisioterapi

Pastikan medical diagnosis dan weight-bearing/precaution. Inspect wound/fixation, edema, circulation/sensation, ROM joint terkait, strength sesuai fase, gait/device, functional task. Jangan melakukan stress test pada fracture yang belum healed.

## Problem list / ICF

Susun body structure-function impairment, activity limitation, participation restriction, serta faktor personal/lingkungan. Prioritaskan problem yang paling membatasi tujuan pasien dan paling modifiable oleh fisioterapi.

## Intervensi fisioterapi

Early: edema management, distal/proximal ROM yang diizinkan, isometric activation, breathing/mobility, education protection. Setelah stability/healing memungkinkan: progressive ROM, strengthening, weight bearing, proprioception, gait, task. Scar management setelah wound closed bila relevan.

## Progression dan evaluasi

Evaluasi pain, edema, ROM, strength, weight-bearing tolerance, gait assistance, functional independence. Progress loading berdasarkan healing status, order medis, dan symptom response.

## Red flags / escalation

Increasing pain/swelling, wound infection, neurovascular compromise, compartment syndrome signs, loss of fixation, new deformity, suspected DVT.

## Practical workflow saat pasien datang

### 1. Triage 2–3 menit

Untuk Fracture and Bone Healing, jangan langsung melakukan special test. Pastikan mekanisme dan symptom behavior masuk pola yang masuk akal. Tanyakan trauma, onset, irritability, kemampuan weight bearing atau menggunakan anggota gerak, night/systemic symptoms, neurologic symptoms, dan tujuan aktivitas. Lakukan red-flag screen sebelum loading agresif.

### 2. Comparable sign

Pilih satu sampai tiga gerakan atau task yang mereproduksi masalah secara aman, misalnya squat, reach, stair, gait, grip, shoulder elevation, atau sport movement. Comparable sign ini akan dipakai untuk melihat apakah pemeriksaan dan intervensi benar-benar mengubah fungsi.

### 3. Scan objektif

Urutan yang efisien: observation → AROM → PROM bila perlu → resisted/muscle performance → palpation terarah → neurological screen jika ada radiation/paresthesia → special test berdasarkan hipotesis → functional test. Jangan melakukan semua special test yang dihafal. Pilih tes yang dapat membedakan hipotesis.

### 4. Differential yang harus tetap di kepala

regional mechanical disorder, neurological disorder, vascular disorder, infection/inflammation, fracture/trauma, systemic disease.

### 5. Setelah tes

Rangkum temuan menjadi pola: pain-dominant, stiffness-dominant, weakness/capacity-dominant, instability, neural, atau mixed. Pilih intervensi yang menargetkan pola tersebut.

## Contoh goal dan progression

Short-term goal harus terukur, misalnya “dalam 2 minggu pasien meningkatkan knee extension dari -10° menjadi -3° dan melakukan sit-to-stand 5 kali tanpa peningkatan nyeri > baseline keesokan hari.” Long-term goal berbasis aktivitas, misalnya kembali berjalan 30 menit, naik tangga, mengangkat barang, atau kembali latihan.

Progression umumnya bergerak dari controlled low-load → full ROM/load → higher resistance/endurance → speed/power → complex functional/sport task. Jika condition berupa fracture/post-op, progression tetap tunduk pada biological healing dan precaution.

## Contoh evaluasi SOAP singkat

S: pasien melaporkan perubahan nyeri, stiffness, confidence, dan activity tolerance sejak sesi sebelumnya.

O: ulangi comparable sign dan minimal satu ukuran objektif seperti ROM, force, gait, sit-to-stand, hop, atau task. Catat intervensi dan dosis aktual.

A: jelaskan apakah kapasitas meningkat, gejala stabil, atau loading terlalu tinggi. Hubungkan perubahan dengan goal.

P: pertahankan, progress, regress, atau ubah intervensi. Tentukan apa yang akan diukur sesi berikutnya.

## Deep Dive: mekanisme sampai evaluasi

### Patofisiologi yang perlu benar-benar dipahami

Fracture disrupts bone continuity and local vasculature. Healing progresses through hematoma/inflammation, soft then hard callus and remodeling. Mechanical stability, blood supply, fracture type, age, nutrition, smoking, infection and fixation affect healing. Immobilization also creates secondary stiffness, weakness, edema and deconditioning.

### Pemeriksaan terarah

Confirm medical diagnosis, fixation, weight-bearing and movement restrictions. Inspect wound/cast, edema, color, temperature and distal neurovascular status. Measure ROM of adjacent joints, pain, muscle activation and functional mobility. Avoid stress tests over an unstable/unhealed fracture. Screen compartment syndrome, infection, DVT and loss of reduction signs.

### Intervensi berdasarkan target impairment

Early phase priorities are protection, edema control, safe mobility, adjacent-joint ROM and isometric activation where permitted. Progress loading according to surgeon/radiographic/clinical criteria. Later phases restore full ROM, strength, proprioception, gait and task-specific capacity. Loading must respect healing biology rather than arbitrary exercise progression.

### Evaluasi dan kriteria progression

Reassess pain/edema, ROM, strength, weight-bearing tolerance, gait/device, functional task and medical healing status. Sudden worsening after improvement requires re-evaluation.

### Differential / kondisi yang jangan terlewat

Soft-tissue injury, occult fracture, infection, complex regional pain syndrome, DVT and neurovascular injury.
