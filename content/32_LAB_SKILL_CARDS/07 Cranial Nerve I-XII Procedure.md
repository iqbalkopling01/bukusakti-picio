## I Olfactory

Tanyakan perubahan smell. Bila perlu tes satu nostril per time dengan familiar non-irritating scent. Jangan gunakan ammonia karena menstimulasi trigeminal.

## II Optic

Visual acuity dengan chart bila tersedia, visual field confrontation, pupil afferent pathway bersama CN III. Fundoscopy di luar scope banyak setting fisioterapi kecuali terlatih.

## III, IV, VI Oculomotor/Trochlear/Abducens

Inspect ptosis dan pupil. Minta pasien mengikuti target dengan mata tanpa gerak kepala melalui H-pattern; catat diplopia, nystagmus dan movement restriction. Pupil light reflex melibatkan II afferent dan III efferent. New painful ophthalmoplegia = medical concern.

## V Trigeminal

Sensory light touch pada V1 forehead, V2 cheek, V3 jaw. Motor: clench jaw dan palpate masseter/temporalis. Corneal reflex jarang dilakukan rutin oleh fisioterapis karena risiko/comfort dan scope.

## VII Facial

Observe symmetry. Ask raise eyebrows, close eyes tightly, smile/show teeth, puff cheeks. Complete peripheral weakness affects forehead and lower face; central pattern may spare forehead, but do not diagnose stroke from this alone.

## VIII Vestibulocochlear

Screen hearing by conversation/finger rub. Vestibular function by symptom history, nystagmus, head impulse/positional tests if trained. Sudden hearing loss with vertigo needs medical evaluation.

## IX and X Glossopharyngeal/Vagus

Voice quality, cough, swallowing history and palate elevation on "ah" can be screened. Gag reflex is uncomfortable and not routinely required. Aspiration concern requires swallowing specialist/medical pathway.

## XI Accessory

Shoulder shrug against resistance (trapezius) and head rotation against resistance (SCM), compare sides.

## XII Hypoglossal

Protrude tongue; observe deviation, atrophy/fasciculation, speech/articulation. Tongue typically deviates toward weak side in LMN lesion, but central patterns require full context.

## Documentation

Record specific abnormal movement/function, not simply "CN abnormal". Acute new cranial deficit with limb weakness, speech or severe headache is urgent stroke/neurologic screen.
