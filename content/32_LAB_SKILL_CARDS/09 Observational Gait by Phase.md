## Cara merekam observasi

Gunakan walkway aman. Lihat depan-belakang-samping. Pertama natural pace tanpa terlalu banyak cue, lalu ulangi bila perlu fast pace, turn, obstacle atau stairs. Catat device, footwear, speed, step length, cadence, BOS dan symmetry.

## Initial contact

Expected: hip flexed, knee near extension, ankle near neutral, heel contact in many adults. Deviations: forefoot contact dapat berasal plantarflexor contracture/spasticity, pain, habit; flat foot dapat berasal dorsiflexor weakness/control.

## Loading response

Knee flexion absorbs shock, ankle plantarflexion controlled eccentrically by dorsiflexors, hip extensors stabilize trunk. Knee collapse can reflect quadriceps weakness/pain; foot slap suggests dorsiflexor control deficit.

## Mid stance

Body progresses over stable foot; ankle dorsiflexes, hip extends. Contralateral pelvic drop suggests hip abductor demand/control issue. Early heel rise may reflect calf tightness or forward progression strategy.

## Terminal stance

Heel rise, hip extension, forefoot loading. Limited hip extension or calf push-off may shorten contralateral step. Painful MTP can reduce terminal stance.

## Pre-swing

Rapid unloading with knee flexion and plantarflexion. Reduced knee flexion can set up swing clearance problems.

## Initial/mid swing

Hip/knee flexion and ankle dorsiflexion provide clearance. Foot drop → steppage; stiff knee → circumduction/hip hiking; adductor/spastic pattern → scissoring.

## Terminal swing

Knee extends under hamstring control and limb prepares for contact. Poor control can produce abrupt knee extension or short step.

## Trunk and pelvis

Duchenne lean toward stance can reduce hip abductor moment. Forward trunk over knee can help weak quadriceps during stance. Excessive pelvic rotation may compensate restricted hip.

## Neurologic patterns

Parkinson: short steps, reduced arm swing, festination/freezing. Cerebellar: wide variable gait. Sensory ataxia: high-stepping/stamping, visual dependence. Hemiparetic: asymmetry with stiff-knee/circumduction/equinovarus depending impairment.

## Reassessment

Use same speed/device and record video if consented. Link each observed deviation to a confirmatory impairment test before treating it.
