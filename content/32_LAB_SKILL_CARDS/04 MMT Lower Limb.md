## Hip flexion

Sitting, hip flexed, knee relaxed. Stabilkan pelvis/trunk. Resistance diberikan distal thigh menuju extension. Watch trunk lean or hip external rotation compensation.

## Hip extension

Prone with knee flexed or extended depending gluteus maximus vs hamstring contribution. Stabilkan pelvis. Resistance posterior distal thigh toward flexion. Lumbar extension/anterior pelvic tilt adalah kompensasi umum.

## Hip abduction

Side-lying, lower limb flexed for stability, tested limb neutral/slight extension. Pelvis stabilized. Resistance lateral distal thigh toward adduction. Hip flexion/TFL substitution and pelvic hiking are common.

## Hip adduction

Side-lying with upper leg supported out of way; tested lower leg adducts upward. Resistance medial distal thigh. Ensure trunk/pelvis remain stable.

## Hip rotation

Sitting, hip/knee 90°. For internal rotation, distal leg moves outward; resistance near ankle inward. External rotation opposite. Stabilize femur and pelvis.

## Knee extension

Sitting. Quadriceps extends knee. Resistance anterior distal tibia toward flexion. Note extension lag and pain. For post-TKR/ACL, do not test maximal resistance if protocol/tissue status does not allow.

## Knee flexion

Prone or sitting. Hamstrings flex knee. Resistance posterior distal leg toward extension. Foot rotation can bias medial/lateral hamstrings but routine clinical MMT often tests group.

## Ankle dorsiflexion

Tibialis anterior: dorsiflexion with inversion. Resistance over medial/dorsal forefoot toward plantarflexion/eversion. Foot drop requires root/peroneal nerve screen.

## Plantarflexion

Weight-bearing single-leg heel raises are more functional than non-weight-bearing MMT for grades above 3. Document repetitions, height and quality. Non-weight-bearing resistance can be used when unable to stand.

## Inversion/eversion

Tibialis posterior inversion+plantarflexion; fibularis longus/brevis eversion with plantarflexion component. Stabilize tibia and resist forefoot in opposite direction.

## Great-toe extension

Extensor hallucis longus is useful L5 myotome. Stabilize foot and resist proximal phalanx downward while patient extends great toe.

## Kesalahan umum

Resistance terlalu mendadak, posisi leverage berbeda antar sisi, tidak menstabilkan pelvis, menganggap pain inhibition sebagai neurologic weakness, dan memberi grade 5 tanpa benar-benar menantang muscle.
