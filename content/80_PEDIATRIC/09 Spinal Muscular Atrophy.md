## Basis materi

Materi SMA D4 Vokasi.

## Definisi

SMA adalah genetic motor neuron disorder dengan degeneration anterior horn cells dan progressive weakness.

## Patofisiologi dan hubungan sebab-akibat

SMN protein deficiency menyebabkan loss lower motor neurons. Denervation menghasilkan weakness, atrophy dan reduced reflex. Severity bergantung type dan treatment era.

## Manifestasi dan impairment

Symmetric proximal weakness, hypotonia, reduced reflex, respiratory weakness, contracture/scoliosis risk.

## Pemeriksaan fisioterapi

Strength/function, respiratory, ROM, posture, seating, mobility, fatigue. Use disease-specific functional scales if available.

## ICF / problem list

Pisahkan impairment, activity limitation, participation restriction, faktor personal, dan faktor lingkungan. Pilih problem prioritas berdasarkan safety, tujuan pasien, dan modifiability.

## Intervensi

Positioning, ROM, submaximal strengthening/activity, respiratory care, seating/assistive technology, standing/mobility according ability. Avoid overfatigue.

## Progression dan evaluasi

Function scale, ROM, respiratory, independence, participation.

## Red flags / escalation

Respiratory distress, swallowing decline, acute infection, sudden function change.

## Practical workflow pediatri

### 1. Mulai dari caregiver dan spontaneous behavior

Untuk Spinal Muscular Atrophy, wawancara caregiver tentang pregnancy/birth, diagnosis, feeding, sleep, seizure, milestone, regression, school/play dan tujuan keluarga. Jangan memulai dengan memaksa anak melakukan test formal ketika state atau engagement belum sesuai.

### 2. Observasi natural

Amati spontaneous movement, symmetry, head/trunk control, reaching, transition, floor mobility, standing dan gait sesuai usia. Catat kualitas dan strategi, bukan hanya milestone “bisa/tidak”.

### 3. Pemeriksaan terstruktur

Tone/reflex → ROM/alignment → strength/antigravity control → balance → gross/fine motor sesuai scope → outcome measure yang sesuai usia. Pada kondisi multisistem, screen vision, hearing, cardiac/respiratory dan feeding issue.

### 4. Differential contributor

regional mechanical disorder, neurological disorder, vascular disorder, infection/inflammation, fracture/trauma, systemic disease.

### 5. Intervensi

Pilih 2–4 task yang dekat dengan developmental level dan goal keluarga. Gunakan play, repetition, positioning, environmental setup dan caregiver practice. Manual facilitation hanya sebanyak yang diperlukan agar anak berhasil lalu dikurangi.

## Evaluasi

Gabungkan outcome formal dan perubahan nyata di rumah. Contoh: GMFM naik tetapi anak tetap tidak mampu transfer dari lantai di rumah berarti goal participation belum tercapai.

## SOAP pediatri

S: caregiver report tentang kemampuan baru, fatigue, falls, feeding, home practice.

O: video/observation task, level assistance, outcome item tertentu.

A: apakah anak memperoleh skill baru, hanya melakukan kompensasi, atau terbatas oleh tone/ROM/strength/attention?

P: satu progression task dan satu home activity yang realistis.

## Deep Dive: mekanisme sampai evaluasi

### Patofisiologi yang perlu benar-benar dipahami

Survival motor neuron deficiency causes degeneration of anterior horn motor neurons, producing progressive proximal weakness, hypotonia/areflexia and respiratory/bulbar involvement depending type. Disease-modifying therapies have changed natural history.

### Pemeriksaan terarah

Function, antigravity movement, ROM, contracture, scoliosis, respiratory/cough, feeding and fatigue. Avoid repeated maximal exertion. Use disease-specific functional scales from multidisciplinary team when available.

### Intervensi berdasarkan target impairment

Positioning, contracture prevention, supported active movement, low-fatigue strengthening/activity, respiratory support/cough assistance and equipment. Coordinate with neuromuscular team and current disease-modifying treatment.

### Evaluasi dan kriteria progression

Function/assistance, ROM, respiratory status, fatigue and participation. New respiratory/bulbar decline is urgent.

### Differential / kondisi yang jangan terlewat

DMD, congenital myopathy, peripheral neuropathy and CP hypotonic type.
