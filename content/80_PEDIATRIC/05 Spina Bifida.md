## Basis materi

Materi Spina Bifida pediatri.

## Definisi

Neural tube defect dengan severity dari occult defect hingga myelomeningocele yang menimbulkan neurological deficits sesuai level.

## Patofisiologi dan hubungan sebab-akibat

Incomplete closure neural tube menyebabkan spinal cord/nerve root abnormality. Level lesion menentukan motor/sensory loss lower limb, bladder/bowel dysfunction, orthopedic deformity dan mobility potential. Hydrocephalus/Chiari may coexist.

## Manifestasi dan impairment

Weakness/paralysis below level, sensory loss, foot/hip deformity, scoliosis, skin risk, bladder-bowel, mobility device needs.

## Pemeriksaan fisioterapi

Motor level, strength, sensation/skin, ROM/contracture, hip/knee/foot alignment, sitting balance, transfer, gait potential, device/orthosis, endurance.

## ICF / problem list

Pisahkan impairment, activity limitation, participation restriction, faktor personal, dan faktor lingkungan. Pilih problem prioritas berdasarkan safety, tujuan pasien, dan modifiability.

## Intervensi

Positioning, ROM, strength available muscles, transfer, wheelchair, gait with orthosis/device if feasible, skin protection, standing program, endurance, independence.

## Progression dan evaluasi

Skin, ROM, device fit, transfer independence, walking/rolling mobility, participation.

## Red flags / escalation

Skin wound/infection, shunt malfunction signs, new neuro loss, tethered cord suspicion, autonomic/systemic issue.

## Practical workflow pediatri

### 1. Mulai dari caregiver dan spontaneous behavior

Untuk Spina Bifida, wawancara caregiver tentang pregnancy/birth, diagnosis, feeding, sleep, seizure, milestone, regression, school/play dan tujuan keluarga. Jangan memulai dengan memaksa anak melakukan test formal ketika state atau engagement belum sesuai.

### 2. Observasi natural

Amati spontaneous movement, symmetry, head/trunk control, reaching, transition, floor mobility, standing dan gait sesuai usia. Catat kualitas dan strategi, bukan hanya milestone “bisa/tidak”.

### 3. Pemeriksaan terstruktur

Tone/reflex → ROM/alignment → strength/antigravity control → balance → gross/fine motor sesuai scope → outcome measure yang sesuai usia. Pada kondisi multisistem, screen vision, hearing, cardiac/respiratory dan feeding issue.

### 4. Differential contributor

vision/hearing contributor, orthopedic deformity, neurologic/genetic condition, cardiorespiratory comorbidity, pain/illness causing regression, environmental opportunity/task issue.

### 5. Intervensi

Pilih 2–4 task yang dekat dengan developmental level dan goal keluarga. Gunakan play, repetition, positioning, environmental setup dan caregiver practice. Manual facilitation hanya sebanyak yang diperlukan agar anak berhasil lalu dikurangi.

## Evaluasi

Gabungkan outcome formal dan perubahan nyata di rumah. Contoh: GMFM naik tetapi anak tetap tidak mampu transfer dari lantai di rumah berarti goal participation belum tercapai.

## SOAP pediatri

S: caregiver report tentang kemampuan baru, fatigue, falls, feeding, home practice.

O: video/observation task, level assistance, outcome item tertentu.

A: apakah anak memperoleh skill baru, hanya melakukan kompensasi, atau terbatas oleh tone/ROM/strength/attention?

P: satu progression task dan satu home activity yang realistis.

## Deep Dive: mekanisme sampai evaluasi

### Patofisiologi yang perlu benar-benar dipahami

Neural tube defect can damage spinal cord/roots at variable level, producing motor/sensory loss, bladder/bowel dysfunction, orthopedic deformity and hydrocephalus/Chiari-associated issues. Functional potential depends strongly on lesion level.

### Pemeriksaan terarah

Map motor and sensory level, ROM/contracture, hip/knee/foot alignment, skin/pressure, orthosis, transfers, wheelchair/gait and endurance. Ask shunt history, bowel/bladder and latex allergy. Inspect skin in insensate areas.

### Intervensi berdasarkan target impairment

Prevent contracture/pressure injury, strengthen innervated muscles, transfers/wheelchair, standing/gait with orthoses where appropriate, endurance and self-care education. Respect sensory loss during heat/modalities and footwear.

### Evaluasi dan kriteria progression

Skin integrity, ROM, strength, device/orthosis, transfer and community mobility. New weakness, headache/vomiting/shunt concern needs medical review.

### Differential / kondisi yang jangan terlewat

Tethered cord progression, shunt malfunction, orthopedic deformity and pressure injury.
