## Basis materi

Materi ASD ADHD pediatri.

## Definisi

ASD adalah neurodevelopmental condition dengan differences social communication dan restricted/repetitive behavior/sensory features. ADHD melibatkan persistent inattention/hyperactivity-impulsivity pattern.

## Patofisiologi dan hubungan sebab-akibat

Neural development differences memengaruhi sensory processing, motor planning, attention dan learning pada sebagian anak. Fisioterapi tidak “mengobati” core diagnosis, tetapi menangani motor/physical activity issues bila ada.

## Manifestasi dan impairment

Motor coordination difficulties, balance, low physical activity, sensory response, attention to task, participation barriers pada sebagian.

## Pemeriksaan fisioterapi

Family goals, gross motor, coordination, balance, gait, physical fitness, sensory triggers, communication preference.

## ICF / problem list

Pisahkan impairment, activity limitation, participation restriction, faktor personal, dan faktor lingkungan. Pilih problem prioritas berdasarkan safety, tujuan pasien, dan modifiability.

## Intervensi

Structured predictable task, visual cue, play, short blocks, motor skill, balance, physical activity, environmental modification. Collaborate OT/speech/psychology/medical team.

## Progression dan evaluasi

Participation, motor skill, physical activity, caregiver goal.

## Red flags / escalation

Regression, seizure, serious behavior safety or medical issue managed multidisciplinary.

## Practical workflow pediatri

### 1. Mulai dari caregiver dan spontaneous behavior

Untuk ASD and ADHD in Physiotherapy Context, wawancara caregiver tentang pregnancy/birth, diagnosis, feeding, sleep, seizure, milestone, regression, school/play dan tujuan keluarga. Jangan memulai dengan memaksa anak melakukan test formal ketika state atau engagement belum sesuai.

### 2. Observasi natural

Amati spontaneous movement, symmetry, head/trunk control, reaching, transition, floor mobility, standing dan gait sesuai usia. Catat kualitas dan strategi, bukan hanya milestone “bisa/tidak”.

### 3. Pemeriksaan terstruktur

Tone/reflex → ROM/alignment → strength/antigravity control → balance → gross/fine motor sesuai scope → outcome measure yang sesuai usia. Pada kondisi multisistem, screen vision, hearing, cardiac/respiratory dan feeding issue.

### 4. Differential contributor

vision/hearing contributor, orthopedic deformity, neurologic/genetic condition, cardiorespiratory comorbidity, pain/illness causing regression, environmental opportunity/task issue.

### 5. Intervensi

Pilih 2–4 task yang dekat dengan developmental level dan goal keluarga. Gunakan play, repetition, positioning, environmental setup dan caregiver practice. Manual facilitation hanya sebanyak yang diperlukan agar anak berhasil lalu dikurangi.

## Evaluasi

Gabungkan outcome formal dan perubahan nyata di rumah. Contoh: GMFM naik tetapi anak tetap tidak mampu transfer dari lantai di rumah berarti goal participation belum tercapai.

## SOAP pediatri

S: caregiver report tentang kemampuan baru, fatigue, falls, feeding, home practice.

O: video/observation task, level assistance, outcome item tertentu.

A: apakah anak memperoleh skill baru, hanya melakukan kompensasi, atau terbatas oleh tone/ROM/strength/attention?

P: satu progression task dan satu home activity yang realistis.

## Deep Dive: mekanisme sampai evaluasi

### Patofisiologi yang perlu benar-benar dipahami

ASD involves neurodevelopmental differences in social communication/behavior with variable motor, sensory and coordination features. ADHD involves attention/impulsivity/hyperactivity that can affect motor learning and participation; neither diagnosis automatically means a specific motor impairment.

### Pemeriksaan terarah

Family/school goal, sensory/behavioral context, motor skill, balance/coordination, physical activity and participation. Use short instructions and observation in natural task. Screen coexisting developmental coordination or hypotonia only if present.

### Intervensi berdasarkan target impairment

Task-oriented motor learning, physical activity, balance/coordination when impaired, environmental structure, predictable routines and caregiver/teacher collaboration. Adapt communication and sensory load.

### Evaluasi dan kriteria progression

Participation, task success, physical activity, motor skill and caregiver/school-reported function.

### Differential / kondisi yang jangan terlewat

Developmental coordination disorder, anxiety, sensory/vision/hearing problem and intellectual disability.
