## Basis materi

Materi CTEV PES PLANUS.

## Definisi

CTEV adalah congenital clubfoot dengan complex deformity. Pes planus menggambarkan reduced medial longitudinal arch dan dapat flexible atau rigid.

## Patofisiologi dan hubungan sebab-akibat

CTEV melibatkan bone/joint/soft tissue alignment sejak lahir dan biasanya ditangani serial casting/manipulation ± surgery sesuai pathway. Flexible flatfoot pada anak sering developmental variant dan tidak selalu symptomatic.

## Manifestasi dan impairment

CTEV: equinus, varus, adductus/cavus pattern. Pes planus: low arch, hindfoot valgus, fatigue/pain pada symptomatic cases.

## Pemeriksaan fisioterapi

Foot alignment, ROM, skin, gait, strength, pain, footwear, previous casting/surgery. Differentiate flexible vs rigid flatfoot.

## ICF / problem list

Pisahkan impairment, activity limitation, participation restriction, faktor personal, dan faktor lingkungan. Pilih problem prioritas berdasarkan safety, tujuan pasien, dan modifiability.

## Intervensi

CTEV PT mengikuti orthopedic correction protocol, ROM/strength/gait after correction. Symptomatic flexible flatfoot: calf/foot strength, balance, footwear/orthosis selected, activity. Avoid treating asymptomatic flexible flatfoot solely based on appearance.

## Progression dan evaluasi

Foot ROM/alignment, gait, pain, function, shoe tolerance.

## Red flags / escalation

Rigid painful flatfoot, neuro signs, recurrence CTEV, skin issue, acute trauma.

## Practical workflow pediatri

### 1. Mulai dari caregiver dan spontaneous behavior

Untuk CTEV and Pes Planus, wawancara caregiver tentang pregnancy/birth, diagnosis, feeding, sleep, seizure, milestone, regression, school/play dan tujuan keluarga. Jangan memulai dengan memaksa anak melakukan test formal ketika state atau engagement belum sesuai.

### 2. Observasi natural

Amati spontaneous movement, symmetry, head/trunk control, reaching, transition, floor mobility, standing dan gait sesuai usia. Catat kualitas dan strategi, bukan hanya milestone “bisa/tidak”.

### 3. Pemeriksaan terstruktur

Tone/reflex → ROM/alignment → strength/antigravity control → balance → gross/fine motor sesuai scope → outcome measure yang sesuai usia. Pada kondisi multisistem, screen vision, hearing, cardiac/respiratory dan feeding issue.

### 4. Differential contributor

fracture, syndesmosis injury, Achilles injury, peroneal/fibular nerve issue, tendon injury, inflammatory/infective condition.

### 5. Intervensi

Pilih 2–4 task yang dekat dengan developmental level dan goal keluarga. Gunakan play, repetition, positioning, environmental setup dan caregiver practice. Manual facilitation hanya sebanyak yang diperlukan agar anak berhasil lalu dikurangi.

## Evaluasi

Gabungkan outcome formal dan perubahan nyata di rumah. Contoh: GMFM naik tetapi anak tetap tidak mampu transfer dari lantai di rumah berarti goal participation belum tercapai.

## SOAP pediatri

S: caregiver report tentang kemampuan baru, fatigue, falls, feeding, home practice.

O: video/observation task, level assistance, outcome item tertentu.

A: apakah anak memperoleh skill baru, hanya melakukan kompensasi, atau terbatas oleh tone/ROM/strength/attention?

P: satu progression task dan satu home activity yang realistis.

## Deep Dive: mekanisme sampai evaluasi

### Patofisiologi yang perlu benar-benar dipahami

CTEV combines hindfoot equinus/varus, forefoot adductus and cavus in varying severity. Treatment often uses serial casting/manipulation and bracing early. Pediatric flexible pes planus may be physiologic; symptoms and rigidity matter more than footprint alone.

### Pemeriksaan terarah

For CTEV review treatment history, brace adherence, foot alignment, passive correctability, ROM, calf size, gait and relapse signs. Pes planus: assess flexible vs rigid, heel rise, pain, footwear and proximal mechanics.

### Intervensi berdasarkan target impairment

CTEV physiotherapy supports stretching/ROM and motor/gait after orthopedic treatment, brace education and strengthening. Symptomatic flexible pes planus may use activity/footwear advice, calf/foot/hip strength and orthoses when indicated; asymptomatic flexible flatfoot often needs reassurance.

### Evaluasi dan kriteria progression

Foot alignment, dorsiflexion, gait, pain, function and brace adherence. Rigid painful flatfoot needs medical/orthopedic evaluation.

### Differential / kondisi yang jangan terlewat

Tarsal coalition, neuromuscular foot deformity, vertical talus and recurrence CTEV.
