## Basis materi

Materi Tortikolis pediatri.

## Definisi

Infant head tilt satu sisi dan rotation berlawanan, sering terkait SCM shortening/imbalance.

## Patofisiologi dan hubungan sebab-akibat

Muscle tightness dan preferred position menghasilkan asymmetric sensory-motor experience. Persistent asymmetry dapat berkaitan plagiocephaly dan delayed symmetrical motor skill.

## Manifestasi dan impairment

Head tilt, limited cervical rotation/sideflexion, preferred gaze, cranial asymmetry.

## Pemeriksaan fisioterapi

Observe resting posture, active/passive cervical ROM, SCM palpation, head shape/CVAI if used, vision/neuro screen, gross motor symmetry.

## ICF / problem list

Pisahkan impairment, activity limitation, participation restriction, faktor personal, dan faktor lingkungan. Pilih problem prioritas berdasarkan safety, tujuan pasien, dan modifiability.

## Intervensi

Caregiver positioning, tummy time, active rotation toward limited side, gentle stretching, environmental setup, symmetrical play.

## Progression dan evaluasi

ROM, midline head control, time in preferred posture, head shape, AIMS if development concern.

## Red flags / escalation

Atypical torticollis, neurologic/ocular signs, severe pain, vertebral anomaly suspicion.

## Practical workflow pediatri

### 1. Mulai dari caregiver dan spontaneous behavior

Untuk Congenital Muscular Torticollis, wawancara caregiver tentang pregnancy/birth, diagnosis, feeding, sleep, seizure, milestone, regression, school/play dan tujuan keluarga. Jangan memulai dengan memaksa anak melakukan test formal ketika state atau engagement belum sesuai.

### 2. Observasi natural

Amati spontaneous movement, symmetry, head/trunk control, reaching, transition, floor mobility, standing dan gait sesuai usia. Catat kualitas dan strategi, bukan hanya milestone “bisa/tidak”.

### 3. Pemeriksaan terstruktur

Tone/reflex → ROM/alignment → strength/antigravity control → balance → gross/fine motor sesuai scope → outcome measure yang sesuai usia. Pada kondisi multisistem, screen vision, hearing, cardiac/respiratory dan feeding issue.

### 4. Differential contributor

vision/hearing contributor, orthopedic deformity, neurologic/genetic condition, cardiorespiratory comorbidity, pain/illness causing regression, environmental opportunity/task issue.

### 5. Intervensi

Pilih 2–4 task yang dekat dengan developmental level dan goal keluarga. Gunakan play, repetition, positioning, environmental setup dan caregiver practice. Manual facilitation hanya sebanyak yang diperlukan agar anak berhasil lalu dikurangi.

## Evaluasi

Gabungkan outcome formal dan perubahan nyata di rumah. Contoh: GMFM naik tetapi anak tetap tidak mampu transfer dari lantai di rumah berarti goal participation belum tercapai.

## SOAP pediatri

S: caregiver report tentang kemampuan baru, fatigue, falls, feeding, home practice.

O: video/observation task, level assistance, outcome item tertentu.

A: apakah anak memperoleh skill baru, hanya melakukan kompensasi, atau terbatas oleh tone/ROM/strength/attention?

P: satu progression task dan satu home activity yang realistis.

## Deep Dive: mekanisme sampai evaluasi

### Patofisiologi yang perlu benar-benar dipahami

Congenital muscular torticollis often involves sternocleidomastoid shortening/imbalance, producing head tilt to affected side and rotation opposite. Persistent asymmetry can contribute to plagiocephaly and asymmetric motor experience.

### Pemeriksaan terarah

Head posture, cervical PROM/AROM rotation/lateral flexion, SCM palpation, craniofacial symmetry, trunk/hip screen, visual tracking and developmental milestone. Red flags include neurologic/ocular cause, bony anomaly or pain/late onset.

### Intervensi berdasarkan target impairment

Caregiver positioning, tummy time, active rotation toward limited side, gentle stretching, symmetrical handling/feeding/play and trunk strengthening. More severe refractory cases need medical/orthotic/surgical review.

### Evaluasi dan kriteria progression

Cervical ROM, head midline time, head righting, plagiocephaly measure if used and developmental symmetry.

### Differential / kondisi yang jangan terlewat

Ocular torticollis, cervical vertebral anomaly, neurologic dystonia and acute painful torticollis.
