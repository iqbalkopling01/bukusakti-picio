## Basis materi

Materi lesi pleksus brachialis pediatri.

## Definisi

Injury plexus brachialis saat birth dapat menyebabkan weakness/sensory deficit upper limb sesuai roots/trunks affected.

## Patofisiologi dan hubungan sebab-akibat

Traction injury ranges neuropraxia hingga rupture/avulsion. Denervated muscles tidak menghasilkan force normal dan imbalance dapat menyebabkan shoulder contracture/deformity.

## Manifestasi dan impairment

Erb pattern C5-C6: shoulder abduction/external rotation dan elbow flexion weakness. More extensive lesion affects hand.

## Pemeriksaan fisioterapi

Observe spontaneous arm use, ROM, strength by active movement scale appropriate infant, sensation, scapula/shoulder alignment, contracture, biceps recovery timeline.

## ICF / problem list

Pisahkan impairment, activity limitation, participation restriction, faktor personal, dan faktor lingkungan. Pilih problem prioritas berdasarkan safety, tujuan pasien, dan modifiability.

## Intervensi

Gentle PROM prevent contracture, positioning, sensory input, facilitate active reach/grasp, bilateral play, strengthening as recovery, caregiver handling. Surgical referral timeline managed specialist.

## Progression dan evaluasi

Serial active movement, ROM, functional use, contracture.

## Red flags / escalation

Horner signs, absent recovery, progressive contracture, pain/trauma need specialist follow-up.

## Practical workflow pediatri

### 1. Mulai dari caregiver dan spontaneous behavior

Untuk Brachial Plexus Birth Injury, wawancara caregiver tentang pregnancy/birth, diagnosis, feeding, sleep, seizure, milestone, regression, school/play dan tujuan keluarga. Jangan memulai dengan memaksa anak melakukan test formal ketika state atau engagement belum sesuai.

### 2. Observasi natural

Amati spontaneous movement, symmetry, head/trunk control, reaching, transition, floor mobility, standing dan gait sesuai usia. Catat kualitas dan strategi, bukan hanya milestone “bisa/tidak”.

### 3. Pemeriksaan terstruktur

Tone/reflex → ROM/alignment → strength/antigravity control → balance → gross/fine motor sesuai scope → outcome measure yang sesuai usia. Pada kondisi multisistem, screen vision, hearing, cardiac/respiratory dan feeding issue.

### 4. Differential contributor

vision/hearing contributor, orthopedic deformity, neurologic/genetic condition, cardiorespiratory comorbidity, pain/illness causing regression, environmental opportunity/task issue.

### 5. Intervensi

Pilih 2–4 task yang dekat dengan developmental level dan goal keluarga. Gunakan play, repetition, positioning, environmental setup dan caregiver practice. Manual facilitation hanya sebanyak yang diperlukan agar anak berhasil lalu dikurangi.

## Evaluasi

Gabungkan outcome formal dan perubahan nyata di rumah. Contoh: GMFM naik tetapi anak tetap tidak mampu transfer dari lantai di rumah berarti goal participation belum tercapai.

## SOAP pediatri

S: caregiver report tentang kemampuan baru, fatigue, falls, feeding, home practice.

O: video/observation task, level assistance, outcome item tertentu.

A: apakah anak memperoleh skill baru, hanya melakukan kompensasi, atau terbatas oleh tone/ROM/strength/attention?

P: satu progression task dan satu home activity yang realistis.

## Deep Dive: mekanisme sampai evaluasi

### Patofisiologi yang perlu benar-benar dipahami

Traction/compression around birth can injure upper, lower or total brachial plexus, ranging neurapraxia to avulsion. Muscle imbalance during growth can create secondary shoulder contracture/deformity.

### Pemeriksaan terarah

Observe spontaneous arm movement, Moro/asymmetry, active vs passive ROM, muscle activation by root/nerve distribution, scapular/shoulder alignment and hand function. Avoid forcing weak shoulder. Track recovery trajectory and surgical referral criteria with specialist.

### Intervensi berdasarkan target impairment

Gentle passive ROM to prevent contracture, facilitate active movement in gravity-reduced positions, sensory play, bilateral task and progressive strength as reinnervation occurs. Educate caregiver positioning and handling.

### Evaluasi dan kriteria progression

Active movement recovery, ROM, functional hand-to-mouth/reach and symmetry. Plateau/severe deficits require specialist follow-up.

### Differential / kondisi yang jangan terlewat

Fracture clavicle/humerus, central neurologic injury and congenital limb disorder.
