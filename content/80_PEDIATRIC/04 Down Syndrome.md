## Basis materi

Materi Down Syndrome dan laporan konferensi kasus NDT yang telah dianonimkan.

## Definisi

Down Syndrome adalah chromosomal condition paling sering trisomy 21 yang memengaruhi banyak sistem dan perkembangan.

## Patofisiologi dan hubungan sebab-akibat

Gene dosage imbalance akibat extra chromosome 21 mengubah proliferasi, diferensiasi dan development sejak embryogenesis. Musculoskeletal features dapat termasuk hypotonia dan ligamentous laxity; cardiac, respiratory, hearing, vision dan cognitive development juga dapat terdampak. Motor milestone sering terlambat karena combination hypotonia, ligament laxity, altered postural control dan learning.

## Manifestasi dan impairment

Hypotonia, joint hypermobility, delayed head/trunk control, delayed sitting/standing/gait, flat foot, reduced balance, possible cardiac/respiratory comorbidity.

## Pemeriksaan fisioterapi

History cardiac/respiratory/hearing/vision, cervical atlantoaxial precaution, observation milestone. Dalam laporan klinik digunakan PBS, SATCo, Beighton Score, HCRS, GMFM. Assess strength, balance, trunk/head control, gait, participation.

## ICF / problem list

Pisahkan impairment, activity limitation, participation restriction, faktor personal, dan faktor lingkungan. Pilih problem prioritas berdasarkan safety, tujuan pasien, dan modifiability.

## Intervensi

NDT/task facilitation untuk head/trunk control, reaching, sitting, kneeling, quadruped, crawling, sit-to-stand; strengthening, balance, gait, play. Laporan kasus mencantumkan grasp training, head control, corrective sitting, reaching, kneeling, weight shifting, bridging, quadruped, crawling, sit-to-stand. Program tetap individualized.

## Progression dan evaluasi

Gunakan GMFM/HCRS atau measure yang dipilih secara serial, serta goal caregiver. Reassess quality dan independence, bukan skor saja.

## Red flags / escalation

Neck pain/new neurologic sign karena concern cervical instability, cardiac symptoms, severe respiratory issue, regression.

## Practical workflow pediatri

### 1. Mulai dari caregiver dan spontaneous behavior

Untuk Down Syndrome, wawancara caregiver tentang pregnancy/birth, diagnosis, feeding, sleep, seizure, milestone, regression, school/play dan tujuan keluarga. Jangan memulai dengan memaksa anak melakukan test formal ketika state atau engagement belum sesuai.

### 2. Observasi natural

Amati spontaneous movement, symmetry, head/trunk control, reaching, transition, floor mobility, standing dan gait sesuai usia. Catat kualitas dan strategi, bukan hanya milestone “bisa/tidak”.

### 3. Pemeriksaan terstruktur

Tone/reflex → ROM/alignment → strength/antigravity control → balance → gross/fine motor sesuai scope → outcome measure yang sesuai usia. Pada kondisi multisistem, screen vision, hearing, cardiac/respiratory dan feeding issue.

### 4. Differential contributor

vision/hearing contributor, orthopedic deformity, neurologic/genetic condition, cardiorespiratory comorbidity, pain/illness causing regression, environmental opportunity/task issue.

### 5. Intervensi

Pilih 2–4 task yang dekat dengan developmental level dan goal keluarga. Gunakan play, repetition, positioning, environmental setup dan caregiver practice. Manual facilitation hanya sebanyak yang diperlukan agar anak berhasil lalu dikurangi.

## Evaluasi

Gabungkan outcome formal dan perubahan nyata di rumah. Contoh: GMFM naik tetapi anak tetap tidak mampu transfer dari lantai di rumah berarti goal participation belum tercapai.

## SOAP pediatri

S: caregiver report tentang kemampuan baru, fatigue, falls, feeding, home practice.

O: video/observation task, level assistance, outcome item tertentu.

A: apakah anak memperoleh skill baru, hanya melakukan kompensasi, atau terbatas oleh tone/ROM/strength/attention?

P: satu progression task dan satu home activity yang realistis.

## Deep Dive: mekanisme sampai evaluasi

### Patofisiologi yang perlu benar-benar dipahami

Trisomy 21 changes gene dosage and multisystem development. Hypotonia, ligamentous laxity, altered postural control and potential cardiac/respiratory/sensory comorbidity contribute to delayed motor milestones. Atlantoaxial instability is a clinical precaution when symptomatic or medically identified.

### Pemeriksaan terarah

Caregiver/medical history including heart, respiratory, hearing/vision and sleep; observe head/trunk, transitions, standing/gait. Assess hypermobility (e.g. Beighton as appropriate), SATCo, PBS, HCRS and GMFM according to problem. Do not equate hypotonia with muscle weakness; test functional force separately.

### Intervensi berdasarkan target impairment

Task-oriented NDT/facilitation when helpful, strength, balance, reaching, transitions, kneeling/quadruped, sit-to-stand, gait and play. Provide sufficient stability without over-bracing all movement. Caregiver home practice should be simple and functional.

### Evaluasi dan kriteria progression

GMFM/HCRS/SATCo/PBS plus independence in real-life transitions and play. Monitor fatigue and cardiorespiratory response.

### Differential / kondisi yang jangan terlewat

CP/other genetic condition, hearing/vision-limited development, cervical neurologic signs and cardiac/respiratory limitation.
