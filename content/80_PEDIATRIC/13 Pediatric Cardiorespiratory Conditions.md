## Basis materi

Materi fisioterapi pada jantung dan respirasi anak.

## Definisi

Mencakup asthma, bronchitis/bronchopneumonia, congenital heart disease dan kondisi lain dengan kebutuhan assessment/intervensi sesuai usia.

## Patofisiologi dan hubungan sebab-akibat

Anak memiliki airway lebih kecil, respiratory reserve berbeda, dan developmental/feeding demands. Secretions atau airway narrowing meningkatkan work of breathing lebih cepat. Cardiac defects dapat mengubah oxygenation dan fatigue.

## Manifestasi dan impairment

Cough, wheeze, secretion, dyspnea, feeding/play fatigue, desaturation, developmental impact.

## Pemeriksaan fisioterapi

Observe work breathing, RR, HR, SpO2, auscultation, cough, sputum, feeding/play tolerance, developmental ability.

## ICF / problem list

Pisahkan impairment, activity limitation, participation restriction, faktor personal, dan faktor lingkungan. Pilih problem prioritas berdasarkan safety, tujuan pasien, dan modifiability.

## Intervensi

Age-appropriate airway clearance, positioning, play-based mobilization, breathing instruction pada anak cukup besar, endurance, caregiver education. Cardiac intensity according cardiology.

## Progression dan evaluasi

SpO2, RR, secretion, cough, play/activity tolerance.

## Red flags / escalation

Retraction severe, cyanosis, apnea, feeding distress, altered consciousness require urgent medical care.

## Practical workflow pediatri

### 1. Mulai dari caregiver dan spontaneous behavior

Untuk Pediatric Cardiorespiratory Conditions, wawancara caregiver tentang pregnancy/birth, diagnosis, feeding, sleep, seizure, milestone, regression, school/play dan tujuan keluarga. Jangan memulai dengan memaksa anak melakukan test formal ketika state atau engagement belum sesuai.

### 2. Observasi natural

Amati spontaneous movement, symmetry, head/trunk control, reaching, transition, floor mobility, standing dan gait sesuai usia. Catat kualitas dan strategi, bukan hanya milestone “bisa/tidak”.

### 3. Pemeriksaan terstruktur

Tone/reflex → ROM/alignment → strength/antigravity control → balance → gross/fine motor sesuai scope → outcome measure yang sesuai usia. Pada kondisi multisistem, screen vision, hearing, cardiac/respiratory dan feeding issue.

### 4. Differential contributor

regional mechanical disorder, neurological disorder, vascular disorder, infection/inflammation, fracture/trauma, systemic disease.

### 5. Intervensi

Pilih 2–4 task yang dekat dengan developmental level dan goal keluarga. Gunakan play, repetition, positioning, environmental setup dan caregiver practice. Manual facilitation hanya sebanyak yang diperlukan agar anak berhasil lalu dikurangi.

## Evaluasi

Gabungkan outcome formal dan perubahan nyata di rumah. Contoh: GMFM naik tetapi anak tetap tidak mampu transfer dari lantai di rumah berarti goal participation belum tercapai.

## SOAP pediatri

S: caregiver report tentang kemampuan baru, fatigue, falls, feeding, home practice.

O: video/observation task, level assistance, outcome item tertentu.

A: apakah anak memperoleh skill baru, hanya melakukan kompensasi, atau terbatas oleh tone/ROM/strength/attention?

P: satu progression task dan satu home activity yang realistis.

## Deep Dive: mekanisme sampai evaluasi

### Patofisiologi yang perlu benar-benar dipahami

Children with asthma, pneumonia, congenital heart disease or chronic lung disease have age-specific airway size, breathing pattern and developmental demands. Respiratory disease can reduce feeding/play and motor participation.

### Pemeriksaan terarah

Vitals by age, work of breathing, SpO2 baseline, cough/sputum, auscultation, chest movement, feeding/sleep and activity/play. Ask cardiac diagnosis/oxygen baseline where relevant.

### Intervensi berdasarkan target impairment

Positioning, breathing/play-based expansion, airway clearance only when indicated, early mobility and graded activity. For asthma, exercise with disease control/action plan. For CHD, individual cardiology parameters.

### Evaluasi dan kriteria progression

RR/SpO2, work of breathing, cough, play/activity and caregiver-reported recovery.

### Differential / kondisi yang jangan terlewat

Foreign-body aspiration, cardiac failure, pneumothorax and severe infection.
