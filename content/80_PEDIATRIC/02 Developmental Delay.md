## Basis materi

Materi gangguan pertumbuhan perkembangan, optimalisasi tumbuh kembang.

## Definisi

Delay adalah keterlambatan mencapai skill pada satu atau lebih domain dibanding expectation umur, dengan sebab sangat beragam.

## Patofisiologi dan hubungan sebab-akibat

Etiology dapat prematurity, genetic, neurologic, musculoskeletal, sensory, environmental, systemic disease. Delay merupakan description function, bukan etiologic diagnosis.

## Manifestasi dan impairment

Late milestone, low movement variability, asymmetry, low endurance, difficulty transition, participation limitation.

## Pemeriksaan fisioterapi

Birth/history, corrected age, milestone, neurologic/musculoskeletal screen, vision/hearing, tone, ROM, strength, spontaneous movement. AIMS/PDMS/GMFM/HINE sesuai usia/condition.

## ICF / problem list

Pisahkan impairment, activity limitation, participation restriction, faktor personal, dan faktor lingkungan. Pilih problem prioritas berdasarkan safety, tujuan pasien, dan modifiability.

## Intervensi

Family-centered goal, play/task practice, positioning, strength/control, mobility opportunity, caregiver home routine. Address specific impairment rather than generic exercise.

## Progression dan evaluasi

Outcome measure serial dan functional goals. Monitor rate of acquisition, not only raw age milestone.

## Red flags / escalation

Regression, abnormal neurological signs, severe hypotonia, feeding/respiratory issue, genetic/systemic suspicion.

## Practical workflow pediatri

### 1. Mulai dari caregiver dan spontaneous behavior

Untuk Developmental Delay, wawancara caregiver tentang pregnancy/birth, diagnosis, feeding, sleep, seizure, milestone, regression, school/play dan tujuan keluarga. Jangan memulai dengan memaksa anak melakukan test formal ketika state atau engagement belum sesuai.

### 2. Observasi natural

Amati spontaneous movement, symmetry, head/trunk control, reaching, transition, floor mobility, standing dan gait sesuai usia. Catat kualitas dan strategi, bukan hanya milestone “bisa/tidak”.

### 3. Pemeriksaan terstruktur

Tone/reflex → ROM/alignment → strength/antigravity control → balance → gross/fine motor sesuai scope → outcome measure yang sesuai usia. Pada kondisi multisistem, screen vision, hearing, cardiac/respiratory dan feeding issue.

### 4. Differential contributor

vision/hearing contributor, orthopedic deformity, neurologic/genetic condition, cardiorespiratory comorbidity, pain/illness causing regression, environmental opportunity/task issue.

### 5. Intervensi

Pilih 2–4 task yang dekat dengan developmental level dan goal keluarga. Gunakan play, repetition, positioning, environmental setup dan caregiver practice. Manual facilitation hanya sebanyak yang diperlukan agar anak berhasil lalu dikurangi.

## Evaluasi

Gabungkan outcome formal dan perubahan nyata di rumah. Contoh: GMFM naik tetapi anak tetap tidak mampu transfer dari lantai di rumah berarti goal participation belum tercapai.

## SOAP pediatri

S: caregiver report tentang kemampuan baru, fatigue, falls, feeding, home practice.

O: video/observation task, level assistance, outcome item tertentu.

A: apakah anak memperoleh skill baru, hanya melakukan kompensasi, atau terbatas oleh tone/ROM/strength/attention?

P: satu progression task dan satu home activity yang realistis.

## Deep Dive: mekanisme sampai evaluasi

### Patofisiologi yang perlu benar-benar dipahami

Delay is descriptive and may reflect global or domain-specific slower acquisition. Causes range from prematurity/environment to genetic, neurologic, sensory, cardiopulmonary or musculoskeletal conditions.

### Pemeriksaan terarah

Detailed pregnancy/birth/medical history, corrected age, hearing/vision, feeding, regression, spontaneous movement and standardized developmental tool such as AIMS/PDMS/GMFM depending age/condition. Examine tone, ROM, strength/postural control and participation.

### Intervensi berdasarkan target impairment

Goal-directed early intervention, enriched play, task practice, positioning, strength/balance as appropriate and caregiver coaching. Address specific cause/comorbidity through interdisciplinary referral.

### Evaluasi dan kriteria progression

Trajectory over time is crucial. Use standardized measure plus home/school skill. Lack of progress or regression prompts diagnostic review.

### Differential / kondisi yang jangan terlewat

CP, Down syndrome, neuromuscular disease, ASD, sensory impairment and medical illness.
