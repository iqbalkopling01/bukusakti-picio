## Basis materi

Materi prinsip konsep tumbuh kembang dan motor learning, optimalisasi tumbuh kembang.

## Definisi

Perkembangan motorik adalah perubahan kemampuan movement sepanjang waktu melalui interaksi maturation, experience, task dan environment.

## Patofisiologi dan hubungan sebab-akibat

Infant memperoleh head control, rolling, sitting, mobility, standing dan walking melalui perubahan musculoskeletal, nervous system, sensory integration dan practice. Variasi urutan dapat terjadi, tetapi regression atau persistent asymmetry perlu perhatian.

## Manifestasi dan impairment

Milestone harus dilihat sebagai rentang dan quality, bukan tanggal tunggal.

## Pemeriksaan fisioterapi

Observe spontaneous movement, prone/supine, head control, rolling, sitting, transition, crawling, pull-to-stand, gait sesuai age. Assess symmetry, antigravity control, variability, play, caregiver interaction.

## ICF / problem list

Pisahkan impairment, activity limitation, participation restriction, faktor personal, dan faktor lingkungan. Pilih problem prioritas berdasarkan safety, tujuan pasien, dan modifiability.

## Intervensi

Floor play, tummy time sesuai kondisi, reaching, transitions, environmental opportunity, caregiver coaching. Gunakan task sedikit di atas kemampuan saat ini tetapi tetap sukses.

## Progression dan evaluasi

Track acquisition skill, quality, symmetry, participation, outcome AIMS/GMFM bila indicated.

## Red flags / escalation

Regression, loss of skill, persistent severe asymmetry, feeding/respiratory issue, seizure, abnormal tone require medical/developmental evaluation.

## Practical workflow pediatri

### 1. Mulai dari caregiver dan spontaneous behavior

Untuk Normal Development and Motor Learning, wawancara caregiver tentang pregnancy/birth, diagnosis, feeding, sleep, seizure, milestone, regression, school/play dan tujuan keluarga. Jangan memulai dengan memaksa anak melakukan test formal ketika state atau engagement belum sesuai.

### 2. Observasi natural

Amati spontaneous movement, symmetry, head/trunk control, reaching, transition, floor mobility, standing dan gait sesuai usia. Catat kualitas dan strategi, bukan hanya milestone “bisa/tidak”.

### 3. Pemeriksaan terstruktur

Tone/reflex → ROM/alignment → strength/antigravity control → balance → gross/fine motor sesuai scope → outcome measure yang sesuai usia. Pada kondisi multisistem, screen vision, hearing, cardiac/respiratory dan feeding issue.

### 4. Differential contributor

vision/hearing contributor, orthopedic deformity, neurologic/genetic condition, cardiorespiratory comorbidity, pain/illness causing regression, environmental opportunity/task issue.

### 5. Intervensi

Pilih 2–4 task yang dekat dengan developmental level dan goal keluarga. Gunakan play, repetition, positioning, environmental setup dan caregiver practice. Manual facilitation hanya sebanyak yang diperlukan agar anak berhasil lalu dikurangi.

## Evaluasi

Gabungkan outcome formal dan perubahan nyata di rumah. Contoh: GMFM naik tetapi anak tetap tidak mampu transfer dari lantai di rumah berarti goal participation belum tercapai.

## SOAP pediatri

S: caregiver report tentang kemampuan baru, fatigue, falls, feeding, home practice.

O: video/observation task, level assistance, outcome item tertentu.

A: apakah anak memperoleh skill baru, hanya melakukan kompensasi, atau terbatas oleh tone/ROM/strength/attention?

P: satu progression task dan satu home activity yang realistis.

## Deep Dive: mekanisme sampai evaluasi

### Patofisiologi yang perlu benar-benar dipahami

Motor development emerges from interaction of nervous system maturation, musculoskeletal capacity, sensory input, task and environment. Milestones are ranges, not rigid dates. Motor learning requires active problem solving and repeated task experience.

### Pemeriksaan terarah

Use corrected age when appropriate, caregiver history, spontaneous movement, symmetry, head/trunk control, transitions, floor mobility, standing/gait and play. Observe quality, variability and adaptation, not just milestone completion.

### Intervensi berdasarkan target impairment

Promote age-appropriate floor/play opportunities, variable practice, caregiver coaching and task/environment modification. Avoid overhandling that removes the child’s active problem solving.

### Evaluasi dan kriteria progression

New skills, quality/independence, participation and caregiver confidence. Regression is different from delayed acquisition and is a red flag.

### Differential / kondisi yang jangan terlewat

Prematurity, sensory impairment, CP/genetic/neuromuscular disorder, orthopedic limitation and environmental opportunity.
