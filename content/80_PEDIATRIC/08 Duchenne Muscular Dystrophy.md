## Basis materi

Materi DMP pediatri.

## Definisi

X-linked dystrophinopathy menyebabkan progressive muscle degeneration.

## Patofisiologi dan hubungan sebab-akibat

Lack dystrophin membuat muscle membrane fragile. Repeated contraction leads damage, inflammation, fibrosis and fat replacement. Weakness progresses proximal lower limb then respiratory/cardiac involvement.

## Manifestasi dan impairment

Gowers sign, calf pseudohypertrophy, difficulty running/stairs, frequent falls, contracture, later respiratory/cardiac decline.

## Pemeriksaan fisioterapi

Functional motor, ROM especially ankle/hip, strength with caution, gait, timed function, respiratory, fatigue. Avoid repeated maximal eccentric testing.

## ICF / problem list

Pisahkan impairment, activity limitation, participation restriction, faktor personal, dan faktor lingkungan. Pilih problem prioritas berdasarkan safety, tujuan pasien, dan modifiability.

## Intervensi

Maintain submaximal activity, stretching/positioning contracture, night splint/orthosis as team, low-moderate aerobic, respiratory support, energy conservation, wheelchair/seating planning. Avoid exhaustive/high-load eccentric exercise.

## Progression dan evaluasi

Timed function, ROM, mobility, respiratory metrics, participation.

## Red flags / escalation

Acute respiratory decline, chest/cardiac symptoms, fracture, sudden loss function.

## Practical workflow pediatri

### 1. Mulai dari caregiver dan spontaneous behavior

Untuk Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy, wawancara caregiver tentang pregnancy/birth, diagnosis, feeding, sleep, seizure, milestone, regression, school/play dan tujuan keluarga. Jangan memulai dengan memaksa anak melakukan test formal ketika state atau engagement belum sesuai.

### 2. Observasi natural

Amati spontaneous movement, symmetry, head/trunk control, reaching, transition, floor mobility, standing dan gait sesuai usia. Catat kualitas dan strategi, bukan hanya milestone “bisa/tidak”.

### 3. Pemeriksaan terstruktur

Tone/reflex → ROM/alignment → strength/antigravity control → balance → gross/fine motor sesuai scope → outcome measure yang sesuai usia. Pada kondisi multisistem, screen vision, hearing, cardiac/respiratory dan feeding issue.

### 4. Differential contributor

vision/hearing contributor, orthopedic deformity, neurologic/genetic condition, cardiorespiratory comorbidity, pain/illness causing regression, environmental opportunity/task issue.

### 5. Intervensi

Pilih 2–4 task yang dekat dengan developmental level dan goal keluarga. Gunakan play, repetition, positioning, environmental setup dan caregiver practice. Manual facilitation hanya sebanyak yang diperlukan agar anak berhasil lalu dikurangi.

## Evaluasi

Gabungkan outcome formal dan perubahan nyata di rumah. Contoh: GMFM naik tetapi anak tetap tidak mampu transfer dari lantai di rumah berarti goal participation belum tercapai.

## SOAP pediatri

S: caregiver report tentang kemampuan baru, fatigue, falls, feeding, home practice.

O: video/observation task, level assistance, outcome item tertentu.

A: apakah anak memperoleh skill baru, hanya melakukan kompensasi, atau terbatas oleh tone/ROM/strength/attention?

P: satu progression task dan satu home activity yang realistis.

## Deep Dive: mekanisme sampai evaluasi

### Patofisiologi yang perlu benar-benar dipahami

X-linked dystrophin deficiency causes progressive muscle fiber degeneration, weakness and later cardiomyopathy/respiratory involvement. Eccentric high-damage exercise and overwork are concerns.

### Pemeriksaan terarah

Functional mobility, transfers, gait, timed tasks, ROM/contracture, strength without excessive fatigue, respiratory/cardiac status from team, scoliosis and equipment. Ask falls and fatigue.

### Intervensi berdasarkan target impairment

Maintain submaximal activity, gentle stretching/positioning, contracture prevention, low-to-moderate aerobic activity, energy conservation, orthoses/equipment and respiratory management. Avoid heavy eccentric or exhaustive strengthening. Plan transitions to mobility devices proactively.

### Evaluasi dan kriteria progression

Timed function, ROM, falls, walking/transfer independence, respiratory/cardiac function and participation. Sudden decline may reflect medical complication.

### Differential / kondisi yang jangan terlewat

Other muscular dystrophy, SMA, inflammatory myopathy and peripheral neuropathy.
