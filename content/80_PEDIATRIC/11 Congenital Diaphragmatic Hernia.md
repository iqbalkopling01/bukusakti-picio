## Basis materi

Materi CDH pediatri.

## Definisi

Defect diaphragm memungkinkan abdominal organs herniate ke thorax dan mengganggu lung development.

## Patofisiologi dan hubungan sebab-akibat

Prenatal compression menghasilkan pulmonary hypoplasia dan pulmonary hypertension. Survivors dapat memiliki respiratory, growth, motor and endurance issues after intensive care/surgery.

## Manifestasi dan impairment

History neonatal respiratory failure, reduced endurance, developmental delay, chest wall/postural issue on some survivors.

## Pemeriksaan fisioterapi

Medical/surgical history, respiratory status, SpO2, developmental assessment, motor function, endurance, scar/posture.

## ICF / problem list

Pisahkan impairment, activity limitation, participation restriction, faktor personal, dan faktor lingkungan. Pilih problem prioritas berdasarkan safety, tujuan pasien, dan modifiability.

## Intervensi

Developmental play, gross motor, conditioning, breathing/chest mobility, scar mobility after healed, caregiver program.

## Progression dan evaluasi

Developmental scales, endurance, respiratory response, participation.

## Red flags / escalation

Respiratory distress, cyanosis, recurrent severe symptoms.

## Practical workflow pediatri

### 1. Mulai dari caregiver dan spontaneous behavior

Untuk Congenital Diaphragmatic Hernia, wawancara caregiver tentang pregnancy/birth, diagnosis, feeding, sleep, seizure, milestone, regression, school/play dan tujuan keluarga. Jangan memulai dengan memaksa anak melakukan test formal ketika state atau engagement belum sesuai.

### 2. Observasi natural

Amati spontaneous movement, symmetry, head/trunk control, reaching, transition, floor mobility, standing dan gait sesuai usia. Catat kualitas dan strategi, bukan hanya milestone “bisa/tidak”.

### 3. Pemeriksaan terstruktur

Tone/reflex → ROM/alignment → strength/antigravity control → balance → gross/fine motor sesuai scope → outcome measure yang sesuai usia. Pada kondisi multisistem, screen vision, hearing, cardiac/respiratory dan feeding issue.

### 4. Differential contributor

regional mechanical disorder, neurological disorder, vascular disorder, infection/inflammation, fracture/trauma, systemic disease.

### 5. Intervensi

Pilih 2–4 task yang dekat dengan developmental level dan goal keluarga. Gunakan play, repetition, positioning, environmental setup dan caregiver practice. Manual facilitation hanya sebanyak yang diperlukan agar anak berhasil lalu dikurangi.

## Evaluasi

Gabungkan outcome formal dan perubahan nyata di rumah. Contoh: GMFM naik tetapi anak tetap tidak mampu transfer dari lantai di rumah berarti goal participation belum tercapai.

## SOAP pediatri

S: caregiver report tentang kemampuan baru, fatigue, falls, feeding, home practice.

O: video/observation task, level assistance, outcome item tertentu.

A: apakah anak memperoleh skill baru, hanya melakukan kompensasi, atau terbatas oleh tone/ROM/strength/attention?

P: satu progression task dan satu home activity yang realistis.

## Deep Dive: mekanisme sampai evaluasi

### Patofisiologi yang perlu benar-benar dipahami

Diaphragmatic defect permits abdominal organs into thorax during development, impairing lung growth and causing pulmonary hypoplasia/pulmonary hypertension. Survivors may have respiratory, feeding and developmental issues.

### Pemeriksaan terarah

Review surgical/respiratory history, oxygen/pulmonary hypertension, feeding, growth and developmental milestones. Assess breathing, exercise tolerance, posture/trunk and gross motor.

### Intervensi berdasarkan target impairment

After medical stabilization: developmental motor intervention, graded physical activity, respiratory strategies if ongoing impairment, posture/strength and caregiver education. Respect cardiopulmonary limits.

### Evaluasi dan kriteria progression

Growth, respiratory symptoms, activity tolerance and motor development.

### Differential / kondisi yang jangan terlewat

Recurrent hernia, pulmonary hypertension, chronic lung disease and feeding/nutrition problems.
