## Basis materi

Materi CP, Bobath anak, pediatric assessment.

## Definisi

CP adalah kelompok gangguan permanent development movement/posture akibat non-progressive disturbance pada developing fetal/infant brain. Clinical musculoskeletal consequences dapat berubah/progress seiring pertumbuhan.

## Patofisiologi dan hubungan sebab-akibat

Brain lesion mengganggu motor control, selective movement, tone, balance dan postural control. Abnormal muscle growth, reduced activity, spasticity/dystonia dan lever-arm changes dapat menimbulkan contracture/deformity sekunder.

## Manifestasi dan impairment

Spastic, dyskinetic, ataxic atau mixed patterns; delayed gross motor, poor trunk control, contracture, gait deviations, feeding/communication comorbidity.

## Pemeriksaan fisioterapi

History, GMFCS bila available, tone, ROM, selective motor control, strength, posture, hip/spine surveillance info, balance, gait. GMFM, SATCo/TCMS, PBS sesuai level.

## ICF / problem list

Pisahkan impairment, activity limitation, participation restriction, faktor personal, dan faktor lingkungan. Pilih problem prioritas berdasarkan safety, tujuan pasien, dan modifiability.

## Intervensi

Goal-directed task training, strength, balance, gait, mobility, stretching/positioning contracture management, orthoses/device collaboration, aerobic/play. NDT/Bobath handling sebagai support task, bukan passive handling only. Family training.

## Progression dan evaluasi

GMFM dimension, transfer, gait speed/device, ROM contracture, participation. Progress based on function and growth.

## Red flags / escalation

Pain/new loss function, hip displacement signs, respiratory/swallow issue, seizure change, sudden regression.

## Practical workflow pediatri

### 1. Mulai dari caregiver dan spontaneous behavior

Untuk Cerebral Palsy, wawancara caregiver tentang pregnancy/birth, diagnosis, feeding, sleep, seizure, milestone, regression, school/play dan tujuan keluarga. Jangan memulai dengan memaksa anak melakukan test formal ketika state atau engagement belum sesuai.

### 2. Observasi natural

Amati spontaneous movement, symmetry, head/trunk control, reaching, transition, floor mobility, standing dan gait sesuai usia. Catat kualitas dan strategi, bukan hanya milestone “bisa/tidak”.

### 3. Pemeriksaan terstruktur

Tone/reflex → ROM/alignment → strength/antigravity control → balance → gross/fine motor sesuai scope → outcome measure yang sesuai usia. Pada kondisi multisistem, screen vision, hearing, cardiac/respiratory dan feeding issue.

### 4. Differential contributor

vision/hearing contributor, orthopedic deformity, neurologic/genetic condition, cardiorespiratory comorbidity, pain/illness causing regression, environmental opportunity/task issue.

### 5. Intervensi

Pilih 2–4 task yang dekat dengan developmental level dan goal keluarga. Gunakan play, repetition, positioning, environmental setup dan caregiver practice. Manual facilitation hanya sebanyak yang diperlukan agar anak berhasil lalu dikurangi.

## Evaluasi

Gabungkan outcome formal dan perubahan nyata di rumah. Contoh: GMFM naik tetapi anak tetap tidak mampu transfer dari lantai di rumah berarti goal participation belum tercapai.

## SOAP pediatri

S: caregiver report tentang kemampuan baru, fatigue, falls, feeding, home practice.

O: video/observation task, level assistance, outcome item tertentu.

A: apakah anak memperoleh skill baru, hanya melakukan kompensasi, atau terbatas oleh tone/ROM/strength/attention?

P: satu progression task dan satu home activity yang realistis.

## Deep Dive: mekanisme sampai evaluasi

### Patofisiologi yang perlu benar-benar dipahami

Non-progressive lesion of developing brain creates permanent movement/posture disorder. Secondary consequences are progressive: spasticity/dystonia, reduced selective control, altered muscle growth, contracture, torsional/lever-arm change, hip displacement and reduced activity can interact over growth.

### Pemeriksaan terarah

Classify motor type/distribution and functional level (GMFCS when available). Assess tone/MAS but also selective control, strength, ROM, hip/spine alignment, trunk (SATCo/TCMS), gross motor (GMFM), balance, gait/device and participation. Ask pain, feeding/respiratory, seizures and vision/hearing.

### Intervensi berdasarkan target impairment

Goal-directed task practice, strength, aerobic/play, balance and gait, standing/weight-bearing, orthosis/device and contracture management. NDT/Bobath may facilitate active task; dosage of active practice remains essential. Coordinate spasticity/orthopedic management and hip surveillance with team.

### Evaluasi dan kriteria progression

GMFM dimension, transfers, gait/device, ROM, pain, participation and caregiver burden. Growth requires repeated musculoskeletal surveillance even if brain lesion is non-progressive.

### Differential / kondisi yang jangan terlewat

Progressive neurologic disease, dystonia/genetic disorder, spinal pathology and orthopedic pain causing apparent decline.
