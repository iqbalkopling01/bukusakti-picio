## Basis materi

Materi Neonatal Care semester 5.

## Definisi

Fisioterapi neonatal menangani positioning, developmental support, respiratory/motor issues pada neonate sesuai medical stability.

## Patofisiologi dan hubungan sebab-akibat

Prematurity dan critical illness mengganggu neuromotor maturation, sensory environment, respiratory function dan musculoskeletal positioning.

## Manifestasi dan impairment

Low tone/high tone, poor state regulation, asymmetry, respiratory support, feeding issue, delayed motor repertoire.

## Pemeriksaan fisioterapi

Gestational/corrected age, vitals, state, spontaneous movement, posture, tone, reflex, head shape, respiratory support, handling tolerance.

## ICF / problem list

Pisahkan impairment, activity limitation, participation restriction, faktor personal, dan faktor lingkungan. Pilih problem prioritas berdasarkan safety, tujuan pasien, dan modifiability.

## Intervensi

Developmental positioning, containment, skin-to-skin support with team, gentle handling, prone positioning only under appropriate monitored/awake clinical context, caregiver education, respiratory PT if indicated.

## Progression dan evaluasi

Tolerance handling, state regulation, respiratory response, movement quality, developmental follow-up.

## Red flags / escalation

Apnea, bradycardia, desaturation, unstable temperature/hemodynamic status.

## Practical workflow pediatri

### 1. Mulai dari caregiver dan spontaneous behavior

Untuk Neonatal Physiotherapy Care, wawancara caregiver tentang pregnancy/birth, diagnosis, feeding, sleep, seizure, milestone, regression, school/play dan tujuan keluarga. Jangan memulai dengan memaksa anak melakukan test formal ketika state atau engagement belum sesuai.

### 2. Observasi natural

Amati spontaneous movement, symmetry, head/trunk control, reaching, transition, floor mobility, standing dan gait sesuai usia. Catat kualitas dan strategi, bukan hanya milestone “bisa/tidak”.

### 3. Pemeriksaan terstruktur

Tone/reflex → ROM/alignment → strength/antigravity control → balance → gross/fine motor sesuai scope → outcome measure yang sesuai usia. Pada kondisi multisistem, screen vision, hearing, cardiac/respiratory dan feeding issue.

### 4. Differential contributor

vision/hearing contributor, orthopedic deformity, neurologic/genetic condition, cardiorespiratory comorbidity, pain/illness causing regression, environmental opportunity/task issue.

### 5. Intervensi

Pilih 2–4 task yang dekat dengan developmental level dan goal keluarga. Gunakan play, repetition, positioning, environmental setup dan caregiver practice. Manual facilitation hanya sebanyak yang diperlukan agar anak berhasil lalu dikurangi.

## Evaluasi

Gabungkan outcome formal dan perubahan nyata di rumah. Contoh: GMFM naik tetapi anak tetap tidak mampu transfer dari lantai di rumah berarti goal participation belum tercapai.

## SOAP pediatri

S: caregiver report tentang kemampuan baru, fatigue, falls, feeding, home practice.

O: video/observation task, level assistance, outcome item tertentu.

A: apakah anak memperoleh skill baru, hanya melakukan kompensasi, atau terbatas oleh tone/ROM/strength/attention?

P: satu progression task dan satu home activity yang realistis.

## Deep Dive: mekanisme sampai evaluasi

### Patofisiologi yang perlu benar-benar dipahami

Prematurity/critical neonatal illness can affect respiratory stability, tone, state regulation, sensory exposure and early motor development. Corrected age and physiologic stability guide intervention.

### Pemeriksaan terarah

Observe state, color, HR/RR/SpO2 as applicable, posture, spontaneous movement, symmetry, feeding cues and tolerance to handling. Use developmental tools when age/stability appropriate.

### Intervensi berdasarkan target impairment

Developmental positioning, containment, graded sensory/motor handling, caregiver skin-to-skin/tummy-time education when medically safe, and support flexed midline organization. Avoid overstimulation.

### Evaluasi dan kriteria progression

Physiologic stability during handling, spontaneous movement, feeding/state regulation and developmental trajectory.

### Differential / kondisi yang jangan terlewat

Acute respiratory/sepsis/neurologic deterioration and pain.
