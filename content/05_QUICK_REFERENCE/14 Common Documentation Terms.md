## Assistance level

Independent: no help/supervision.

Supervision: verbal oversight without physical contact.

Contact guard: contact for safety but no actual lifting assistance.

Minimal assist: patient performs most task, small physical help.

Moderate assist: patient and helper share substantial work.

Maximal assist: helper provides most physical effort.

Dependent: patient provides little/no effective effort or needs multiple assistants/device.

Definitions can vary by institution. Use local standard if different.

## Symptom terms

Paresthesia: abnormal sensation such as tingling.

Hypoesthesia: reduced sensation.

Anesthesia: absent sensation.

Allodynia: pain from normally nonpainful stimulus.

Hyperalgesia: increased pain response to painful stimulus.

## Motor terms

Paresis: partial weakness.

Paralysis/plegia: severe/complete loss voluntary motor function depending usage.

Ataxia: impaired coordination not explained by weakness alone.

Dysmetria: overshoot/undershoot target.

Bradykinesia: slowness and decrement movement in Parkinsonian context.

## Gait terms

Cadence: steps per minute.

Step length: distance heel one foot to heel contralateral.

Stride length: distance one foot contact to next contact same foot.

Base of support/step width: mediolateral distance between feet.

## SOAP

S: patient/caregiver report.
O: measurable observation/test/intervention.
A: interpretation and response.
P: next plan.
