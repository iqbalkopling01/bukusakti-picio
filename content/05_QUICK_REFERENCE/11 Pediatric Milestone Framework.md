Milestone sangat bervariasi. Gunakan rentang dan quality. Corrected age penting pada prematurity sesuai rekomendasi follow-up.

## Early months

Head control berkembang bertahap. Prone tolerance meningkat. Hands mulai lebih midline dan reaching muncul.

## Rolling phase

Rolling supine-prone/prone-supine berkembang bersama trunk rotation dan antigravity control. Asymmetry persisten perlu dievaluasi.

## Sitting

Dari supported sitting menuju independent sitting, protective reaction dan reaching. Trunk control dapat dinilai segmental dengan SATCo pada kondisi tertentu.

## Floor mobility

Pivoting, commando crawling, quadruped, creeping dapat muncul dengan variasi. Tidak semua anak melalui pola identik, tetapi kemampuan transition dan symmetry penting.

## Standing

Pull-to-stand, cruising, independent stand, lalu gait. Foot posture dan base lebar dapat berbeda dari dewasa pada fase awal.

## Red flags developmental

Regression/loss skill selalu penting. Persistent head lag jauh melewati expected, marked asymmetry, no antigravity movement, abnormal tone, feeding/respiratory difficulty, or caregiver concern warrants assessment.

## How to observe

Jangan hanya meminta satu milestone. Lihat spontaneous repertoire, transition masuk-keluar posisi, postural reaction, endurance, play and problem solving.

## Outcome selection

AIMS: infant gross motor.
HINE: infant neurological.
GMFM: gross motor function especially CP/related.
SATCo/TCMS: trunk control.
PDMS-2: broader motor domains.
PEDI-CAT: functional performance/participation.
PBS: balance child.
