## Cryotherapy

Target: analgesia/symptom control. Check skin, sensation, circulation. Reassess pain and movement.

## Superficial heat

Target: comfort/tissue warming before mobility. Avoid impaired sensation/circulation or acute uncontrolled swelling depending context.

## TENS

Target: pain modulation. Need intact skin/safety screen. Outcome pain/function immediately and after session.

## NMES/EMS

Target: muscle contraction/activation. Need peripheral motor excitability. Use visible effective contraction and adequate rest.

## Ultrasound

Target depends protocol. Because clinical benefit condition-specific, do not use merely because tissue is “deep”. Document parameters and reassess.

## SWD/MWD

Deep electromagnetic heating. Strict metal/electronic implant and environmental safety.

## Laser/photobiomodulation

Dose is wavelength/power/energy dependent. Use device-specific evidence/protocol.

## Traction

Target selected radicular/compressive symptom. Reassess distal symptom and neuro. Stop if peripheralization/worse.

## Hydrotherapy

Target unloading, mobility, strength, aerobic/balance. Check wound/infection/cardiopulmonary safety.

## Decision hierarchy

1. Apa problem pasien?
2. Apakah active intervention dapat langsung menangani problem?
3. Apa modalitas menambah value?
4. Apa safety/contraindication?
5. Apa comparable sign sebelum dan sesudah?

Modalitas tanpa reassessment berubah menjadi ritual, bukan clinical reasoning.
