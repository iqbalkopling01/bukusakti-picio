## Scapula

Upper trapezius: elevation/upward rotation. Spinal accessory nerve with cervical contribution.

Middle trapezius: retraction. Spinal accessory.

Lower trapezius: depression/upward rotation and posterior tilt contribution.

Serratus anterior: protraction/upward rotation, stabilizes medial border. Long thoracic nerve C5–C7. Weakness may produce winging and impaired elevation.

Rhomboid: retraction/downward rotation. Dorsal scapular nerve.

## Glenohumeral

Deltoid anterior: flexion/internal rotation contribution. Middle: abduction. Posterior: extension/external rotation contribution. Axillary nerve C5–C6.

Supraspinatus: abduction/compression. Suprascapular nerve.

Infraspinatus: external rotation. Suprascapular.

Teres minor: external rotation. Axillary.

Subscapularis: internal rotation. Upper/lower subscapular nerves.

Pectoralis major: horizontal adduction, adduction, internal rotation. Medial/lateral pectoral nerves.

Latissimus dorsi: extension, adduction, internal rotation. Thoracodorsal.

## Elbow/forearm

Biceps: elbow flexion and supination. Musculocutaneous C5–C6.

Brachialis: elbow flexion. Musculocutaneous mainly.

Triceps: elbow extension. Radial C7–C8.

Pronator teres/quadratus: pronation. Median/anterior interosseous.

Supinator: supination. Deep branch radial/posterior interosseous.

## Wrist

ECRL/ECRB: wrist extension/radial deviation. Radial/deep radial.

ECU: extension/ulnar deviation. Posterior interosseous.

FCR: flexion/radial deviation. Median.

FCU: flexion/ulnar deviation. Ulnar.

## Hand

Thenar opposition/abduction predominantly recurrent median nerve except adductor pollicis ulnar.

Interossei: finger abduction/adduction, ulnar nerve.

Lumbricals 1–2 median, 3–4 ulnar. Assist MCP flexion/IP extension.

Finger flexors: FDS median; FDP lateral part anterior interosseous, medial part ulnar.

Finger extensors: posterior interosseous.

## Clinical shortcut

Shoulder abduction weakness: C5/axillary/suprascapular vs pain cuff.

Elbow extension: C7/radial.

Wrist extension: C6/radial.

Finger abduction: T1/ulnar.

Thumb opposition: median.
