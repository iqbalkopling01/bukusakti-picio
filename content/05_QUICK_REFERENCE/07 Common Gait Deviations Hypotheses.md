## Foot slap

Observation: setelah heel contact forefoot jatuh cepat.

Hypothesis: dorsiflexor weakness atau poor eccentric control.

Verify: MMT tibialis anterior, nerve/root L4-L5, fatigue.

## Steppage

High hip/knee flexion during swing.

Hypothesis: foot drop, ankle plantarflexion contracture, orthosis clearance issue.

## Trendelenburg

Contralateral pelvic drop during stance.

Hypothesis: hip abductor weakness/pain, superior gluteal lesion, altered hip lever.

## Duchenne lean

Trunk leans over stance limb.

Hypothesis: compensation reducing hip abductor moment or pain.

## Knee buckling

Excess knee flexion collapse loading/midstance.

Hypothesis: quadriceps weakness, pain, proprioceptive loss, severe crouch.

## Genu recurvatum in stance

Knee hyperextension.

Hypothesis: plantarflexor spasticity/contracture, quadriceps weakness/control, proprioceptive strategy, limited tibial progression.

## Vaulting

Excess plantarflexion contralateral stance to clear swing limb.

Hypothesis: swing limb too long from reduced knee flexion/foot drop/orthosis.

## Circumduction

Swing leg arcs lateral.

Hypothesis: inadequate knee flexion, hip flexion, dorsiflexion, stiff limb, tone.

## Hip hiking

Pelvis swing side elevated.

Hypothesis: same clearance problems as circumduction.

## Antalgic

Short stance on painful side.

Verify source via joint/tissue/load exam.

## Crouch

Excess hip/knee flexion in stance, common in certain CP patterns.

Hypothesis: contracture, weakness, lever-arm dysfunction, motor-control/tone.

## Festination/shuffling

Short rapid steps, reduced clearance.

Consider Parkinson when paired with bradykinesia, rigidity, reduced arm swing, freezing.

## Ataxic

Variable foot placement, wide base.

Verify cerebellar coordination, proprioception, vestibular and intoxication/medication context.
