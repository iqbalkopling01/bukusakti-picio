## NPRS

0–10 pain intensity. Tentukan konteks: current, worst, task-specific. Gunakan kondisi pertanyaan sama saat repeat.

## 5xSTS

Chair height dan arm use harus dicatat. Lebih cepat tidak selalu better jika movement unsafe. Berguna lower-limb functional power/transition.

## TUG

Catat device, footwear, assistance dan distance protocol. Observe turn and sit, not time only.

## 10MWT

Tentukan comfortable atau fast speed. Gunakan acceleration/deceleration zone konsisten. Gait speed = distance/time.

## 6MWT

Gunakan corridor dan standardized encouragement bila mengikuti protocol. Catat pre/post HR, SpO2, Borg, device, rests dan distance.

## Berg Balance Scale

14 tasks score 0–4. Total 56. Interpretasi fall risk bergantung population/cutoff; jangan gunakan satu cutoff universal.

## Modified Ashworth

Measures resistance to passive movement, not pure spasticity. Document joint, position, speed.

## GMFM

Dimensions A lying/rolling, B sitting, C crawling/kneeling, D standing, E walking/running/jumping. Use version and scoring manual.

## AIMS

Observe infant prone, supine, sitting, standing. Raw score converted percentile by age using manual.

## HINE

Neurological exam infant with multiple sections. Score interpretation depends age and clinical context.

## SATCo

Support trunk at progressively lower segment; assess static, active, reactive control at each level.

## TCMS

Sitting trunk control across static/dynamic/selective components.

## MMSE

Cognitive screening; education/language/sensory factors affect score. Not standalone dementia diagnosis.

## Clock Drawing

Use a defined scoring system; tests executive-visuospatial aspects.

## Goal Attainment

For unusual patient goals, define specific observable scale rather than vague “lebih baik”.
