## Median nerve

Motor penting: pronator, wrist flexor tertentu, finger flexors tertentu, thenar muscles dan lumbrical lateral.

Quick motor screen: thumb opposition/abduction, precision pinch, flexion index depending lesion level.

Sensory: palmar thumb, index, middle dan radial half ring finger; dorsal distal fingertips sebagian.

Common entrapment: carpal tunnel. Proximal lesion menghasilkan deficit lebih luas daripada CTS.

## Ulnar nerve

Motor: interossei, adductor pollicis, hypothenar, ulnar lumbricals, FCU dan ulnar FDP depending level.

Quick screen: finger abduction/adduction, Froment, small finger abduction.

Sensory: little finger dan ulnar half ring finger, ulnar hand.

Common entrapment: cubital tunnel atau Guyon canal.

## Radial nerve

Motor: elbow extension jika lesion proximal, wrist/finger/thumb extension, supination contribution.

Quick screen: wrist extension, MCP extension, thumb extension.

Sensory: dorsoradial hand, terutama first dorsal web space untuk screening.

Common issue: radial neuropathy dapat menghasilkan wrist drop.

## Musculocutaneous

Motor: biceps, brachialis; elbow flexion/supination.

Sensory terminal lateral antebrachial cutaneous: lateral forearm.

## Axillary

Motor: deltoid, teres minor.

Sensory: regimental badge lateral shoulder.

Lesion dapat terjadi pada shoulder dislocation.

## Femoral

Motor: quadriceps, hip flexion contribution via iliacus/sartorius.

Sensory: anterior thigh branches dan medial leg via saphenous.

Reflex: patellar.

## Obturator

Motor: hip adductors.

Sensory: medial thigh variable.

## Sciatic

Motor: hamstrings lalu divides tibial/common fibular. Sensory direct contribution minimal; branches cover most leg/foot except saphenous.

## Common fibular/peroneal

Deep fibular: dorsiflexion, toe extension; sensory first web space.

Superficial fibular: eversion; sensory dorsum foot.

Common lesion near fibular head dapat menyebabkan foot drop.

## Tibial

Motor: plantarflexion, toe flexion, intrinsic foot via branches.

Sensory: sole via plantar nerves.

Reflex: Achilles mainly S1.

## Superior gluteal

Gluteus medius/minimus dan TFL. Lesion dapat menyebabkan Trendelenburg.

## Inferior gluteal

Gluteus maximus. Weakness difficulty stair/rising/running.

## Lateral femoral cutaneous

Pure sensory anterolateral thigh. Meralgia paresthetica tidak menyebabkan true motor weakness.
