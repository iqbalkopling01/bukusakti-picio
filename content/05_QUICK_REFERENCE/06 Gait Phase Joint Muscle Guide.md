## Initial Contact

Goal: position limb for weight acceptance.

Hip: flexed. Gluteus maximus/hamstring prepare control.

Knee: near extension. Quadriceps active for upcoming loading.

Ankle: neutral. Dorsiflexors hold foot for heel contact.

If dorsiflexors weak: forefoot contact/foot slap may appear.

## Loading Response

Goal: shock absorption and stability.

Hip extensors control flexion and stabilize trunk.

Quadriceps eccentrically control knee flexion.

Dorsiflexors eccentrically control plantarflexion from heel contact to foot flat.

If quadriceps weak: knee buckling or hyperextension compensation.

## Mid Stance

Goal: progress body over stable foot.

Hip moves toward extension. Hip abductors stabilize pelvis in frontal plane.

Ankle dorsiflexes as tibia advances. Soleus eccentrically controls tibial progression.

If plantarflexor tightness/overactivity: early heel rise or knee hyperextension may appear.

## Terminal Stance

Goal: body advances beyond forefoot.

Hip reaches extension. Heel rises. Plantarflexors store/release energy and support propulsion.

If plantarflexor weakness: reduced trailing limb and push-off, shorter contralateral step.

## Pre-Swing

Rapid unloading. Knee flexes. Ankle plantarflexes. Hip begins flexion.

## Initial Swing

Hip flexors advance limb. Knee flexion gives foot clearance. Dorsiflexors move ankle toward neutral.

## Mid Swing

Hip continues flexion. Knee extends partly via momentum. Dorsiflexors maintain clearance.

## Terminal Swing

Hamstrings eccentrically decelerate knee extension/hip flexion. Quadriceps complete knee extension. Limb prepares for contact.

## Pelvis and trunk

Pelvic rotation improves step length. Frontal pelvic control depends hip abductors during single support. Arm swing counterbalances trunk rotation and can be reduced in Parkinson or after stroke.

## Clinical use

Saat melihat deviasi, tanyakan “fase mana?”, “joint mana?”, “apa yang seharusnya terjadi?”, “impairment apa yang dapat menghasilkan deviasi ini?”. Setelah itu verifikasi dengan ROM, MMT, tone, sensation, pain, dan balance.
