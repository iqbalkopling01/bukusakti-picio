Nilai normatif berbeda antar sumber, usia, sex, dan method. Angka di bawah adalah kisaran belajar umum. Untuk ujian, prioritaskan angka dan landmark yang diberikan dosen bila berbeda.

## Shoulder

### Flexion
Patient supine/sitting. Axis dekat lateral humeral head/greater tubercle area. Stationary arm sejajar midaxillary thorax. Moving arm lateral humerus menuju lateral epicondyle. Kisaran umum sekitar 0–180°.

### Extension
Axis sama, stationary trunk, moving humerus. Kisaran umum sekitar 0–60°.

### Abduction
Axis anterior acromion. Stationary parallel sternum. Moving anterior midline humerus. Kisaran umum 0–180°.

### Internal/external rotation
Shoulder 90° abduction, elbow 90°. Axis olecranon. Stationary perpendicular floor, moving ulna. Stabilkan scapula untuk glenohumeral ROM.

## Elbow

Flexion-extension axis lateral epicondyle. Stationary humerus menuju acromion, moving radius menuju radial styloid. Flexion sekitar 0–150°, extension sekitar 0° dengan individual hyperextension possible.

## Forearm

Pronation/supination dapat diukur dengan elbow 90°, menggunakan wrist/hand landmark sesuai method. Hindari shoulder rotation compensation.

## Wrist

Flexion-extension axis lateral wrist dekat triquetrum. Stationary ulna, moving fifth metacarpal. Radial/ulnar deviation axis dorsal capitate dengan stationary forearm dan moving third metacarpal.

## Hip

Flexion axis greater trochanter, stationary trunk/pelvis, moving lateral femur. Stabilkan pelvis.

Extension axis greater trochanter. Hindari lumbar extension/anterior pelvic tilt.

Abduction/adduction axis ASIS tested side atau appropriate landmark, stationary line between ASIS, moving femur to patella.

Rotation seated/prone: axis patella, moving tibia, control pelvis.

## Knee

Axis lateral femoral epicondyle. Stationary femur toward greater trochanter, moving fibula toward lateral malleolus. Flexion sekitar 0–135° lebih/kurang depending hip position/body habitus.

## Ankle

Dorsiflexion/plantarflexion axis lateral malleolus. Stationary fibula, moving fifth metatarsal. Knee position changes gastrocnemius contribution. Dorsiflexion weight-bearing lunge dapat lebih functional.

## Cervical

Inclinometer or goniometer method varies. Flexion/extension, side flexion, rotation should minimize thoracic compensation.

## Documentation example

Knee AROM flexion R 0–105° pain end-range, L 0–132°. PROM R 0–112° firm/painful. Ini lebih berguna daripada hanya “ROM terbatas”.
