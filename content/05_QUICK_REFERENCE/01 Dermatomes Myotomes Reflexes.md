## Cara memakai tabel ini

Gunakan pattern, bukan satu titik. Root lesion biasanya dinilai dari kombinasi pain/sensory, myotomal weakness, reflex change, dan provocative test. Peripheral nerve lesion dapat mengenai beberapa roots tetapi mengikuti satu nerve distribution.

| Root | Sensory area yang sering dipakai | Myotome klinis | Reflex yang membantu |
|---|---|---|---|
| C2 | occiput/upper neck | neck flexion/extension contribution | tidak rutin |
| C3 | lower neck | neck side flexion | tidak rutin |
| C4 | shoulder cape | shoulder elevation/diaphragm contribution | tidak rutin |
| C5 | lateral upper arm | shoulder abduction, elbow flexion | biceps |
| C6 | lateral forearm, thumb | wrist extension, elbow flexion | biceps/brachioradialis |
| C7 | middle finger | elbow extension, wrist flexion/finger extension contribution | triceps |
| C8 | little finger/medial hand | thumb extension, finger flexion | finger flexor reflex jarang dipakai |
| T1 | medial forearm | finger abduction/adduction | tidak rutin |

## Pattern radiculopathy cepat

C5: shoulder abduction/elbow flexion weakness + biceps reflex ↓.

C6: thumb sensory + wrist extension weakness + brachioradialis/biceps change.

C7: middle finger sensory + triceps/wrist flexor/finger extensor weakness + triceps reflex ↓.

C8/T1: intrinsic hand/finger weakness, sensory medial hand/forearm.

L4: medial leg sensory, knee extension/dorsiflexion, patellar reflex.

L5: dorsum foot/great toe, great-toe extension weakness, reflex sering tidak membantu.

S1: lateral foot, plantarflexion weakness, Achilles reflex.

## UMN vs root/peripheral

Root/peripheral lesion cenderung hyporeflex pada arc terkait. UMN lesion di atas segment dapat menyebabkan hyperreflexia di bawah lesion, clonus dan Babinski.

## Catatan variasi

Dermatome map bervariasi antar sumber dan antar individu. Gunakan side comparison dan distribution, jangan mengandalkan satu titik kulit.
