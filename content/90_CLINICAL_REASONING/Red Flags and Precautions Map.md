## Neurologis

New/progressive weakness, saddle anesthesia, urinary retention/incontinence baru, bilateral severe radicular symptoms, sudden severe headache, acute cranial sign, altered consciousness, seizure baru, rapid regression.

## Cardiovascular

New chest pressure, unstable arrhythmia, syncope/presyncope, severe hypotension/hypertension with symptom, suspected acute coronary syndrome, signs of DVT/PE.

## Respiratory

Marked respiratory distress, rapidly falling SpO2, cyanosis, hemoptysis, acute pleuritic chest pain with instability, inability to clear airway with deterioration.

## MSK/systemic

Suspected fracture, unexplained fever plus focal spinal/joint pain, cancer history with nonmechanical progressive pain, unexplained weight loss, hot swollen joint with systemic illness, wound infection.

## Post-operative

Wound dehiscence, excessive drainage, fever, sudden swelling/calf pain, neurovascular compromise, loss of function beyond expected course.

## Pediatric

Loss of previously acquired skill, feeding/respiratory compromise, seizure change, lethargy, unexplained pain, new neurologic sign, suspected non-accidental injury.

## Action

Stop or modify intervention, stabilize patient, recheck vitals, communicate with medical team, and document. Emergency escalation depends on severity and setting SOP.

## Red flag vs precaution

Red flag dapat mengubah jalur care menuju urgent medical evaluation. Precaution berarti intervensi masih mungkin tetapi dosis/teknik perlu dimodifikasi, misalnya osteoporosis, anticoagulant use, sensory loss, post-operative restriction atau pregnancy. Dokumentasikan action yang diambil, bukan hanya menulis "red flag negative".
