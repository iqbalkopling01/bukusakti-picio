## Goal harus observable

Gunakan behavior yang dapat dilihat, kondisi, level bantuan, kualitas dan time frame. "Balance membaik" terlalu umum. "Berdiri tanpa upper-limb support selama 60 detik dengan supervision" dapat diuji.

## Hierarki goal

Impairment goal mendukung task goal, dan task goal mendukung participation. Contoh: quadriceps strength → sit-to-stand → toilet transfer → kembali mandiri di rumah.

## Criteria-based progression

Progress bukan karena kalender semata. Gunakan pain/effusion, movement quality, vital response, strength, ROM, balance, fatigue, confidence dan task success. Pada tissue healing, time biology tetap penting tetapi harus dipadukan dengan response.

## Manipulasi dosis

Strength: load, reps, sets, velocity, ROM, support. Endurance: duration, intensity, interval, frequency. Balance: base of support, sensory challenge, perturbation, dual task. Gait: assistance, speed, distance, terrain, turns, device. Pediatric: support level, task complexity, repetition, environment.

## Immediate reassessment

Cocok setelah mobilization, breathing technique, cueing, device adjustment atau symptom modification. Tanyakan apakah comparable sign berubah.

## Long-term reassessment

Gunakan outcome measure dengan interval yang masuk akal. Pastikan measurement condition sama. Catat adverse response dan participation change.

## Bila hasil stagnan

Periksa kembali diagnosis/hipotesis, target, dosis, adherence, recovery, sleep/nutrition, medication, psychosocial barrier, device/environment, dan apakah medical condition berubah.
